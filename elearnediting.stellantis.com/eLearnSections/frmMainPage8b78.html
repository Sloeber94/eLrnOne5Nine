140 - Alfa 159   2.4 JTD 20V   HORNS NOT WORKING   N020   
 

0

  

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check, by operating the horn control on the steering wheel, that the travel is correct

  

Move on to Step 1

  

Travel not correct

  

Remove the steering wheel and check that there are no impediments to the travel.

Op. 4110A11 STEERING WHEEL - R+R FOR REPLACEMENT OR CHECK FOR VERSIONS WITH AIR BAG

If there are problems, replace the faulty component
 

1

  

CHECK ON INTERVENTION OF FUSE

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check that the horn protective fuse has not blown.

It can be found in the engine compartment junction unit and is the third fuse in the first row F10 (15 A).

  

Move on to Step 2

  

The fuse has blown

  

Replace the fuse and search for the cause of the problem.
 

2

  

RELAY CHECK

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Swap the horn relay feed T3 (it is the middle one in the top row) with the one for the main beam headlamps T2 (it is the first on the left in the top row) and check that the horn is working properly.

  

Move on to Step 3

  

The horn continues not to work

  

Restore the relays to their places and move on to Step 4
 

3

  

RELAY CHECK

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check that the main beam headlamps are working. If the result is positive, remove the horn relay feed and check that the contacts do not show signs of oxidation or deformation.

  

End of diagnosis

  

1. The main beam headlamps are not working

  

1. Replace the main beam headlamp relay (which was originally the one for the horns)
 

2. There are problems with the contacts in the engine compartment junction unit.

  

2. Renew the contacts.

If necessary, replace the engine compartment junction unit

 Op. 5505A13 ADDITIONAL JUNCTION UNIT IN ENGINE COMPARTMENT - R.R.
 

4

  

RELAY ELECTRICAL CHECK

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Undo the fastenings for the engine compartment junction unit and check, using a tester, that the power supply and the earth for the horn relay feed are correct.

 Op. 5505A13 ADDITIONAL JUNCTION UNIT IN ENGINE COMPARTMENT - R.R.

  

Move on to Step 5

  

Relay line electrical problems.

  

Move on to Step 8
 

5

  

HORN ELECTRICAL CHECK

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Using a tester, connect to the horn power supply line and check that whilst the horn is working the supply voltage is more than 11.4 V.

Also check the continuity and the insulation of the line

  

Move on to Step 6

  

1. The voltage is below 11.4 V

  

1. Check the cause of the power supply and restore the correct power supply.

Possibly

 See Test 5530BC BATTERY CHARGE CHECK
 

2. Other electrical problems

  

2. Restore the correct electrical connections

 See E3010 HORNS

 

6

  

HORN POSITIONING CHECK

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check that both the horn casing and the bracket are not touching or are not in the immediate vicinity (less than 10 mm) of other objects (other components, cables, pipes, etc.)

  

Move on to Step 7

  

Horn bracket or casing interfering with other components.

  

Eliminate the cause of the interference
 

7

  

STEERING COLUMN SWITCH UNIT CHECK

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Remove the steering wheel and check that there is nothing preventing the operation of the horn control switch.

Using a tester in parallel to the switch check that, whilst it is working, the supply voltage is more than 11.4 V

  

Move on to Step 9

  

1. Switch stuck open

  

1. Replace the steering wheel

Op. 4110A11

 

2. Switch power supply problems.

  

2. Replace the steering column switch unit.

Op. 5550A12

 

8

  

HORN CHECK

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Remove the horn and connect it directly to a battery charger.

 Op. 5550B30 L/H OR R/H HORN (ONE) - R+R

Check that a sound is produced and there are no unpleasant noises (knocking/buzzing)

  

Move on to Step 9

  

Sound faulty or no sound produced.

  

Replace the horn

 Op. 5550B30 L/H OR R/H HORN (ONE) - R+R
 

9

  

ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

The problem is due to the presence of an irregular electrical absorption source in the electrical system.

Search for and eliminate this cause

  

End of diagnosis
