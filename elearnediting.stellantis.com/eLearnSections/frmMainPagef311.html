When the steering is operated normally there is a hardening of the steering action to both the left and the right
 

0

  

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Carry out a test steering fully in various conditions to check the actual onset of the hardening in both directions.

Visually inspect the power assisted steering fluid level in the reservoir.

  

Move on to Step 1

  

1. Hardening in one direction only

  

1. Proceed as instructed in the

 See table H011 STEERING HARD / (IN ONE DIRECTION ONLY)
 

2. Power assisted steering fluid level too low.

  

2. Top up to the correct level, if there are leaks, continue as instructed in the

 See table X030 POWER ASSISTED STEERING SYSTEM FLUID LEAKS
 

1

  

CHECK ON TYPE OF PROBLEM

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Carry out a test steering fully in various conditions: check the way in which the problem starts.

  

Continuous and constant hardening: move on to Step 2

  

1. Hardening only during idling.

  

1. Check for constrictions in the system flexible and rigid pipes. If necessary, replace the damaged parts, replacing all the seals, see Assembly

 Op. 4130B POWER STEERING HYDRAULIC SYSTEM
 

2. Hardening in end of travel position.

  

2. Restore the correct vehicle geometry, see Assembly

 Op. 4450A WHEEL GEOMETRY
 

2

  

STEERING RODS AND ARMS CHECK

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check the condition of the steering rods and arms.

  

Move on to Step 3

  

1. Rods damaged or worn.

  

1. Replace the rods

 Op. 4110B10 STEERING LINKS - R+R ON VEHICLE - CHECK TOE-IN/TOE-OUT SEPARATELY
 

2. Arms damaged or worn.

  

2. Replace the steering box

Op. 4120A10 STEERING BOX - R+R - CHECK WHEEL TOE-IN IF NECESSARY SEPARATELY
 

3

  

CHECK STEERING COLUMN BEARINGS

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check the condition of the steering column support bearings and that they are working properly.

  

Move on to Step 4

  

Bearings worn or damaged

  

Restore the efficiency of the steering column. If necessary, replace the bearings involved

 Op. 4110A30 SUPPORT WITH STEERING CONTROL SHAFTS - R+R
 

4

  

RACK AND PINION CLEARANCE CHECK

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check the rack and pinion clearance with the steering column detached and the wheels straight.

  

Move on to Step 5

  

Rack and pinion clearance too large

  

Replace the steering box

Op. 4120A10

 

5

  

RACK CLEARANCE AND STEERING BOX FASTENINGS CHECK

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check the bushes-rack radial clearance.

Check that the steering box fastening is correct.

  

Move on to Step 6

  

1. Rack-bushes clearance too large.

  

1. Replace the steering box

Op. 4120A10

 

2. Steering box fastening not correct.

  

2. Restore correct fastenings.
 

6

  

CHECK ON CORRECT TENSION OF DRIVE BELT AND CORRECT OPERATION OF POWER ASSISTED STEERING PUMP

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check that the tension of the power assisted steering pump drive belt is correct and that it is not worn.

Also check that the power assisted steering pump is working properly.

  

Move on to Step 7

  

1. Belt not correctly tensioned.

  

1. Restore correct belt tension

Op. 1092C12 POWER STEERING PUMP DRIVE BELT - ADJUST TENSION
 

2. Belt worn.

  

2. Replace the belt

Op. 1092C11 POWER STEERING PUMP DRIVE BELT - R.R. FOR VERSIONS WITH AIR CONDITIONER
 

3. Power assisted steering pump malfunction.

  

3. Replace the power assisted steering pump

Op. 4130B24 POWER STEERING PUMP R R FOR VERSIONS WITH AIR CONDITIONER
 

7

  

STEERING WHEEL RETURN CHECK

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Refit all the components removed.

Test the vehicle and check that the steering wheel returns correctly.

  

End of diagnosis

  

Poor steering wheel return.

  

Restore the correct front wheel geometry

 Op. 4450A10 FRONT WHEEL GEOMETRY - CHECK USING OPTICAL EQUIPMENT - INCLUDES ADJUSTMENT IF NECESSARY
