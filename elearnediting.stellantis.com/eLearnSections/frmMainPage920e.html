Main beam headlights


CUSTOMER'S REPORTED FAULT

Main beam headlights not working

TECHNICAL CAUSE

Customer's reported fault confirmed

OPERATION

Once the customer's reported fault has been confirmed, perform the below mentioned inspections/controls to verify the correct functionality of the steering column stalk:

 

Step

  

Verification

  

OK

  

KO
 

1

  

Check the condition of the main beam headlight protection fuses and of the bulbs

  

Go to step 2

  

 

2

  

Disconnect the female holder connectors attached to male holder connector seats "A" and "B" of the steering column stalk:

check for any possible loose contact, incomplete connections, terminals that are poorly seemed, loose or inverted.

(fig. 1)

  

Go to step 3

  

Repair any faults, reconnect the female holder connectors "A" and "B" to the steering column stalk seat and check that they are working correctly.

If the fault persists, go to step 2
 

3

  

Identify the output 7 on the female holder connector "A" and the output 4 on female holder connector "B" and shunt them after turning on the panel.

After performing the operation described for each engagement on the jumper, the high beam headlamps should come on along with the headlamp flashers. When the jumper is disconnected, the headlight should switch off.

  

Go to step 4

  

Replace the steering column stalk.
 

4

  

Repeat the operation between the output 7 of female holder connector "A" and the output 3 of female holder connector "B". When the jumper is connected, the main beam headlight must switch on; in this case the light must not change its status even when the jumper is disconnected.

The status only changes when a new jumper is engaged

(fig. 2)

  

Go to step 5

  

Replace the steering column stalk
 

5

  

Remove the stalk from steering column. Position an ohmmeter (set to the low full scale) between pin 7 of connector "A" on the steering column stalk and pin 4 of connector "B" of the steering column stalk. Make sure that the stalk is in its rest position.

If it is present, use pin 9 of connector "B" instead of pin 7 of connector "A".

The ohmmeter should show an almost infinite resistance or at least in the order of tens of Mega Ω.

Leaving the ohmmeter connected, bring the stalk to the headlamp flasher position (1st click).

When the stalk is in this position the resistance should immediately fall to a value of between zero and a maximum of 30 Ω.

The prescribed value should be the net resistance of the measuring system used (ohmmeter, cables, prods, etc).

Releasing the lever the resistance should return to close to infinite or in the order of tens of Mega Ω

(fig. 3)

  

Go to step 6

  

Replace the steering column stalk
 

6

  

Repeat the operation between the output 7 of connector "A" and the output 3 of connector "B": at the first click of the stalk (flasher), the circuit should remain open while bringing the steering column stalk to the main beam position (2nd click) should give a maximum ohmmeter reading of 30 Ω.

Releasing the lever the resistance should return to close to infinite or in the order of tens of Mega Ω.

If it is present, use pin 9 of connector "B" instead of pin 7 of connector "A".

(fig. 3)

  

Contact Te.Se.O.
