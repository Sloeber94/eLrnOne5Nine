5570 T 000 AA BLAUPUNKT (BOSCH) RADIO
CD player intermittent malfunction - Network Information


FAULT COMPLAINED OF

CD player intermittent malfunction and/or CD blocked inside Blaupunkt (Bosch) radio CD player.

TECHNICAL CAUSE

Non-conformity of "SA" motor in CD/MP3 mechanism (from July 2006)

CD Changers (CDC) and Fun model radios are excluded (see Fig. 1). The Fun model is fitted on Panda, Dobló, Ducato (250), Multipla and Ypsilon vehicles.

Fig. 1 - FUN radio front panel



OPERATIONS IN PRODUCTION

A new motor for the CD/MP3 mechanism will be introduced in September 2007.

OPERATIONS IN NETWORK

For vehicles included in the production lot between July 2006 and September 2007, contact the Blaupunkt (Bosch) Service Centre to receive the necessary instructions if the fault is verified.

The list of Service Centres is shown below in table 1.

The costs of replacing and delivering the radio are borne completely by Blaupunkt (Bosch)
