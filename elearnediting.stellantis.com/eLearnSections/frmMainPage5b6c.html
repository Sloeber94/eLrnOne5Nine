7035 1 301 MA WINDSCREEN MOUNTINGS
Noise in the area of the windscreen/dashboard - Service Solution


Cancels and replaces the previous Service News with the same number and subject through the updating of the operating cycle and time allowance.

FAULT COMPLAINED OF

Noise/creaking coming from the lower part of the windscreen/dashboard especially on unmade roads (cobblestones).

TECHNICAL CAUSE

Movement between the mountings used for positioning the windscreen and the windscreen housing.

OPERATIONS IN NETWORK

Network behaviour standards towards the Customer

-When this problem is reported and confirmed, reassure the customer that it can be solved by adjusting the mountings at the base of the windscreen.-After the operation show the Customer what has been done and make sure they are satisfied.

Service Solution

If this problem is reported/confirmed, remove the existing (white or black) mountings at the base of the windscreen following the instructions in the cycle below.

OPERATION CYCLE

-With the bonnet open, locate the mountings (2 - which may be black or white) at the sides of the windscreen base (1 - Fig. 1).-Insert an awl with a curved point between the bonnet hinges until the mounting is located.-Then pull the awl until the mounting is completely detached-Repeat the operation on the other side (left or right).

Take great care when removing the mountings (2 - Fig.1) not to damage the windscreen glass (1) and windscreen seal paintwork (3).

The mountings are only used for the operation of bonding the windscreen; when the bonding process is over the mountings can be removed without any adverse effects.
