1060 1 821 RA IRREGULAR ENGINE OPERATION
Jerking/irregular operation whilst driving, EOBD warning light on, power decrease and possible presence of Examiner errors P1206 / P1219 / P2004 / P2013 / P2080 / P2453 / P2455 - Service Solution


Cancels and replaces the previous SN 10.03.09 dated 03/04/2009 for the addition of new information.

FAULT COMPLAINED OF

Irregular operation:

-engine management warning light on due to DPF blocked;-EGR valve fouled;-thermostat not calibrated with slight shuddering due to EGR corrections activated;-during partial release and pulling manoeuvres: slight jerking with noises;-during slight acceleration manoeuvres: jerking;-shuddering/jerking whilst driving, with repeated more or less sharp variations in the engine speed, especially between 1800 and 2000 rpm;-power decrease at constant speed;-the analysis with Examiner revealed the presence of one or more of the following errors: P1206 - P1219 - P2002 - P2013 - P2080 - P2453 - P2455.-knocking.

For further explanation, the table below contains a list describing some engine malfunctioning conditions, with description and possible causes.

 

Fault

 

Description

 

Possible causes

 

POWER LEVELLING

 

Strong delay in engine response during acceleration.

 

Accumulation of carbon residues on the exhaust valve stem.

 

JUDDERING / JERKING

 

Juddering/jerking refers to small and repeated variations in engine revolution, more or less sharp, associated to irregular operation or slight acceleration.

 

Fouled EGR valve / hardening.

Uncalibrated thermostat

Leaks from the intake circuit

Turbocharger malfunctioning

 

JUDDER / OSCILLATION AT IDLING SPEED, WHEN HOT

 

Repeated oscillations of the engine speed at the rated value, in idling conditions.

 

Shut Off valve fouled

 

KNOCKING / IRREGULAR OPERATION WHEN COLD

 

Knocking / irregular operation when cold

 

Leaks from injector washers.

Exhaust valve drive fouled.

Service Solution

If case of claimed fault, before any operation, follow and fill in the below Check List (Table 1).

The Check List (Table 1) must be filled in carefully together with the Data Registration Table (TRD - Table 2) in order to help the F.G.A. staff to find out what the problem is.

Repairs under warranty will only be refund if the Check List and the Data Registration Table are available, correctly filled in and stamped by the workshop and signed by the Workshop Expert.

If not, all repairs will be written off.

              Procedure 3 - Check operation of thermostat

              To check that the thermostat is working properly, carry out a test drive following these steps:

              1. let the engine warm up.

              2. drive on a stretch of road at a speed of between 70 and 90 km/h in fourth or fifth gear.

              3. Using the Examiner, check that the temperature of the coolant is above 85°C.

              If the temperature is below 85°C, replace the thermostat and check that with the new component the temperature of the engine coolant is higher than the reference value (> 85°C).

                  Procedure 5 – DPF SERVICE regeneration

                  Using the EXAMINER (with reference to Op. 0520G12 in the Service Manual) carry out a service regeneration and make sure that it is successfully completed and that error P1206 is no longer present.

                  Safety recommendations

                  Carrying out a service regeneration produces high exhaust temperatures; before starting it is advisable to check that:

                  - The outside of the exhaust pipe and the areas immediately surrounding it are clean.

                  - The gas extraction socket been removed from the vehicle exhaust pipe

                  - No one is passing close to the exhaust pipe during the operation.

                  - The temperature of the engine water is above 80°C.

                  - The fuel tank is at least one quarter full.

                  - The vehicle is at a standstill in a ventilated area.

                  - The engine is started and idling in neutral.

                  - The brake and clutch pedals are released. (check the effective conditions, pressed or released, of the sensors using the EXAMINER diagnosis equipment).

                  - Do not disconnect the differential pressure sensor whilst carrying out the service regeneration.

                  At the end of the service regeneration, check the parameter - "Particulate filter blocked" using the Examiner.

                  If the result of the measurement is around 75%, the vehicle can be handed back over to the customer.
