PASSIVE SAFETY SYSTEM

General information

The vehicle is equipped with an electronic control system that governs the activation of specific containment devices in the event of frontal or side impact:

The frontal protection system includes:

- driver and passenger front air bags with two-stage activation;

- single-stage driver''s knee bag, single-stage passenger''s knee bag (optional);

- front airbags with pretensioner on buckle and degressive load limiter;

- direct mesh rear seat belts (without idler ring) and strap buckle branches

- decentralised supplementary front impact detection sensor;

- passenger air bag manual disabling switch.

The side protection consists of:

- two sidebags under the fabric on the front seats;

- two window bags housed in the under-roof side members;

- two impact detection sensors located in the central pillar.

The front protection system features new Air Bag Smart 2 activation software that automatically adapts activation parameters according to impact severity.

The driver and passenger front air bags feature dual-stage activation;

- when the impact is of moderate severity, the electronic control unit controls the first airbag activation stage and then, following a significant temperature increase, the second stage so that the energy discharged onto the occupant is not harmful.

- for very severe impact, the control unit activates the second stage after a small temperature rise, i.e. almost immediately, so that as much of the occupant''s kinetic energy as possible can be absorbed before he or she strikes the steering wheel or dashboard.

A supplementary ECS “Early Crash Sensor” on the front beam near the bonnet lock also helps the electronic control unit to operate more quickly than a conventional system so that the airbag activation stage is completed before the occupant''s body begins to move towards the steering wheel or dashboard.

the system components have been specifically designed to work on a specific vehicle. For this reason THEY CANNOT be tampered with, adapted or installed on other types of vehicle.

The car must be handed over to the customer with the passenger airbag deactivation switch in the ON position and control panel warning light off.

For safety reasons, NO continuity checks can be carried out on the wiring.

The electrical connection between the air bag system components is via dedicated wiring built into the dashboard cable loom and the rear cable loom.

The connection between the control unit and the system is achieved via two connectors, one for the dashboard cable and one for the rear cable.

The control unit is connected to the vehicle CAN, through which it exchanges information with other nodes, turns on warning lights in the dashboard and the front courtesy light and performs diagnostic operations.

Because the control unit is a node in the CAN, the air bag system is connected:

- to the standard diagnostic socket via the CAN and not the dedicated K line;

- to the instrument panel, via the CAN, for the operation of the air bag system failure warning light and seat belt not fastened warning light (from JAN 06)

- to the Body Computer for potential end of line customisation (Proxy) with a consequent reduction in the number of spare parts.

The control unit also controls the "seat belt unfastened" buzzer and the "seat belt unfastened" and "passenger airbag deactivated" warning lights on the front courtesy light via a direct connection.

SEVERE DANGER!The front air bag module MUST be deactivated if a child seat is fitted to the front passenger seat.

If the vehicle is equipped with a side bag, deactivation of the front passenger side airbag module does not disable the side modules. This means that any children carried in the car are protected against side impact.

Composition

The system consists of the following devices:

- an electronic control unit;

- two (driver and passenger) front air bags with dual stage activation,

- a driver''s knee bag with single-stage activation as standard,

- a driver''s knee bag with single-stage activation as an option,

- an ECS decentralised supplementary impact detection sensor (Early Crash Sensor),

- passenger air bag manual disabling switch,

- two sidebags under the fabric on the front seats;

- two window bag modules for the side protection of occupants;

- two side impact sensors in the side pillars;

- two electronic pretensioners on the front seats.

The system also provides the following to indicate the efficiency status:

- system fault warning light (red) in the instrument panel:

- passenger air bag disabled warning light on the front courtesy light;

- front seat belts not fastened warning light (in the front courtesy light until DEC 05 and later on in the instrument panel)

- connection via B- CAN with the diagnostic socket - integral with the Body Computer - to test operation via the EXAMINER.

Electronic control unit

The following diagram shows the control unit.


1 - Electronic control unit

2 - Indication showing direction of motion

The control unit is the central occupant protection processing unit and is fastened rigidly to the vehicle floor pan near the central tunnel. It manages all the containment system detection and activation devices by processing signals from the various sensors distributed throughout the vehicle and internal sensors.

The control unit should always be fitted with the arrow, stamped on the adhesive plate, facing the direction of travel of the vehicle. Always check that there are no foreign bodies between the control unit and the body and tighten the bolts to the recommended torque.

If the control unit falls to the ground or is subject to abnormal knocks during handling, it MUST be replaced.

Composition

The control unit contains an electronic deceleration sensor that enables safety device activation. This sensor consists of two-directional accelerometers that are part of the ECS front sensor and power the operation of the side sensors.

Operation

Frontal impact:

When the impact is of moderate severity, the electronic control unit controls the first airbag activation stage and then, following a significant temperature increase, the second stage so that the energy discharged onto the occupant is not harmful.

Conversely, in the case of very severe impact, the control unit activates both stages almost simultaneously to absorb most of the kinetic energy from the occupant''s body before it strikes the steering wheel or dashboard.

In addition, the E.C.S. supplementary sensor allows the activation of the air bags to be advanced compared with a traditional system so that the activation of the air bags is completed even before the occupant starts moving towards the steering wheel or the dashboard.

Side impact:

Similarly, when there is a side impact, the control unit is capable of recognising the direction and intensity, activating the two side bags on the side affected by the impact. To provide total cover against side impacts, two satellite sensors located in the centre pillars are used.

When a side impact takes place, the satellite sensors detect the impact signal directly along the transverse axis of the vehicle and send it to the control unit. This signal, processed by the control unit microprocessor, makes it possible to determine the severity of the side impact and, as a result, only decide whether to activate the side bags on the side concerned if the control unit safety sensor gives the go ahead.

The side air bags (window bags and side bags) are activated simultaneously and independently of the front safety devices.

After each activation of one of the controlled systems (pretensioners, front airbags, sidebags), the control unit stores the activation in its permanent memory and controls activation of the fault warning light on the instrument panel.

Before it is replaced, the control unit guarantees activation of individual devices within the following limits:

- 3 impacts with activation of front seat belt pretensioners alone;

- 3 impacts in total (right or left) with side bag activation;

- 1 impact with activation of the pretensioners and front air bags;

- any combination of the above cases up to the maximum number.

If one of the limits described is not reached, it will be possible to reuse the control unit after carrying out the RESET procedure using the EXAMINER once system operating conditions have been restored. The final activation, corresponding to the maximum number for the limits indicated above, will prevent further reset procedures.

Self-diagnosis

The control unit carries out continuous self-diagnosis of system operation. Specifically:

- it detects and stores faults;

- it diagnoses the connections with the system components and the possible type of fault;

- it signals the onset of these faults by turning on the warning light in the instrument panel.

The faults memorised in the control unit can only be deleted after the fault has been repaired using the EXAMINER diagnostic equipment.

If a fault is detected in the passenger airbag disablement switch, the control unit treats is as though it is off for safety reasons. Module operation is therefore inhibited and the instrument panel warning light is turned on.

Driver''s air bag module


1 - Cover

2 - Containment case

3 - Gas generator

4 - Retaining rings

5 - Bag

Specifications

The driver''s air bag is a passive safety device. It takes the form of a bag that inflates automatically in the case of frontal impact placing itself between the driver''s body and front passenger compartment structures. The air bag module is installed in the middle of the steering wheel.The module cover also acts as a horn control.

Composition

The module comprises:

- a plastic cover that tears at preset points when activated, to allow the bag to expand correctly;

- a 60 litre bag, made out of nylon thread woven to minimise skin abrasions in the event of contact and folded in such a way as to ensure that inflation is gradual and not directed toward the driver;

- two pyrotechnic gas generators;

- a containment case.

Operation

The Bag is inflated by a gas generator with two operation stages that can be activated separately according to the severity of the impact.

Each gas generator is activated via two electrical heater coils that burn the propellant inside the generator allowing the bag to expand. When fully inflated, the bag is in an optimum position to protect the occupant. The bag deflates immediately thanks to two openings in the lower part of the bag. These openings also serve to “soften” the passenger’s impact against the bag and prevent abrasions.

Specifications

The clock spring transfers the electrical signal to controls on the steering wheel (horn and other optional controls) and connects the air bag module without risk of breakage.

Composition

The clock spring is fitted with three leads on the steering wheel side.Two of these are used to connect the air bag module (first and second stage) and one to connect the steering wheel electrical functions (horn, radio controls).

A ring on the lower plate, steering wheel side, automatically prevents both plate from turning in relation to one another when the device is disconnected from the steering wheel.

The new clock spring is equipped with a safety key for transport, which must be removed after installation.

Operation

The connecting cables for the air bag module and the various electrical functions are wound inside the two plates so that they can follow the rotation of the steering wheel. The rotation of the upper plate is activated by means of the fasteners inside the lock ring which engage in the seat on the steering wheel hub. In a working position, the steering wheel presses on the ring to release it from its fasteners and thus allow both plates to turn freely. When the steering wheel is removed the ring, thrust by a spring, stops the rotation between the two plates. This function prevents the upper plate, now no longer secured to the steering wheel, from turning freely and unwinding or winding the coil inside the device and possibly causing it to break.

In operations that require the removal of the steering wheel and the device it is compulsory to observe the following:

- always work with the car''s wheels aligned straight ahead;

- secure both plates with a band, for safety, before removing the device from the steering wheel interface.

If the upper plate should turn in relation to the lower plate for any reason so that the exact starting position is no longer certain, the clock spring MUST be replaced.

Passenger-side air bag module


1 - Bag

2 - Gas generator

3 - Connector

Specifications

The passenger air bag module is a passive safety device that protects the front passenger in the event of front impact by means of a bag that places itself between the occupant and the vehicle dashboard.

Composition

The module comprises:

- a metal containment mount;

- a paper bag protection that opens along predefined tear marks in the event of activation to allow the bag to emerge;

- a 120 litre nylon bag, a fabric that minimises skin abrasions in the event of contact. The bag is folded so that it does not inflate directly towards the occupant;

- two pyrotechnic gas generators;

- bag guide brackets;

Operation

The passenger airbag operates in exactly the same way as the driver-side airbag.

Bag deployment opens the flap built into the dashboard along predefined cut lines. When fully inflated, the bag is in an optimum position to protect the occupant.

The harness inside the air bag unfolds in such a way as to allow inflation to the maximum specified dimensions to prevent collateral injuries to passengers.

Deflation is immediate due to the presence of vent holes in the end of the bag.

Passenger side bag disablement key switch

A key switch at the end of the dashboard on the passenger side (accessible only with the door open) allows the user to deactivate the passenger airbag so that a child seat can be installed without problems. On the front seats, child seats are positioned facing towards the back of the car.

Operation

A switch operated by means of the emergency key in the TEG allows enablement (ON) and disablement (OFF) of the air bag module on the passenger side.

In this case, the control unit cuts out passenger air bag activation but enables normal operation of the pretensioner (only with seat belt fastened) and the side bags. This happens because if a passenger is present but the switch has been left OFF by accident, the air bag will not activate - only the pretensioner.

Deactivation is indicated by a warning light on the front courtesy light coming on.


1 - Key-operated switch

2 - OFF position: passenger-side airbag disabled

3 - ON position: passenger-side airbag enabled

4 - Passenger side air bag disablement warning light

Composition

The unit consists of a case that houses the switch, a key barrel inserted in the case and held in place by a clip and a plate that indicates the positioned assumed by the switch (ON or OFF).

Switch faults are indicated by activation of the air bag system fault warning light.

During the normal key on diagnostic procedure at key ON (4 seconds), the electronic control unit checks the switch status: if the status identified is OFF, the module is excluded from possible activation, the corresponding signal is saved and the warning light on the control panel is turned on. If the recognised status is ON, the warning light flashes for an additional four seconds at the end of the four second diagnostic procedure and then goes off.

In the case of switch anomalies or faults, the system reacts as follows:

- passenger side module is not activated

- air bag system fault warning light comes on

- passenger side air bag module disablement warning light comes on

- deactivation status is saved.

The switch is not operated with the ignition key ON (e.g. using the second key). In this case, the electronic control unit saves an error, indicates it by turning on the air bag system fault warning light and deactivates the module.

Side airbag module


1 - Outer cover

2 - Fastening

3 - Gas generator

4 - Bag

Specifications

The front seats contain two side bag modules installed beneath the trim to increase the protection offered by the body in the event of side impact.

This device ensures the air bag is always in an optimum position in relation to the occupant, regardless of the angle of the seat or the size of the occupant.

The side bag protects the chest and pelvis areas and also - together with the door panels - protects critical areas of the body such as the ribs and abdomen. Its position on the seat always guarantees maximum effectiveness regardless of the position of the latter even if the occupant is not sitting correctly because the opening dynamics of the bag minimise the risk of injury to the body caused by impact with the bodyshell.

Do not cover the front seat backrests with extra covers.

Do not wash the seat backrest with water or steam under pressure.

Composition

The Side Bag module consists of a metal container that houses the gas generator and a nylon bag with a capacity of approx. 12 litres.

The plastic container includes a paper bag protection with preset break points. When the backrest cover is fitted, the trim exterior in the area over the bags includes seams designed to yield easily to allow the bag to inflate.

The module is connected to the electrical system by means of two leads with yellow sheaths. These are contained, together with the seat earth connection lead, in a protective fabric-lined duct secured to the seat structure. The two cables terminate with a connector that is secured to the floor under the seat.

Operation

The gas generator is electrically activated by means of a signal from the electronic control module. Following this signal, pyrotechnic activation is triggered, causing the gas in the generator to expand. The expanding gases exit through dedicated openings to inflate the bag. The bag''s deployment causes the seams designed to yield easily on the backrest cover to yield, allowing the bag to open correctly. When fully inflated, the bag is in an optimum position to protect the occupant. Deflation is immediate due to the presence of a vent hole.

It is not possible to carry out operations on seats fitted with air bags, apart from seat removing-refitting.

Seats with air bags must not be dismantled.

Side window airbag module


1 - Connector

2 - Gas generator

3 - Fastening plates

4 - Bag retaining clips

5 - Bag

6 - Sealing brace

Specifications

The window bags are activated together with the side bags and place themselves between the occupant and the vehicle to prevent the head coming into contact with highly intrusive objects such as glass, pillars etc.

Because the window bags extend from the front pillar to the luggage compartment, they protect front and rear passengers. The window bag system offers the best performance due to the broad area covered and their ability to be self-supporting even without a backing.

Composition

The window bag module consists of:

- a gas generator secured by mounts to the rear car pillar;

- a flexible pipe in permeable cloth, secured by a band to the gas generator, which diffuses the gas evenly over the entire length of the bag;

- a bag of approximately 30 litres in volume, fastened to the gas generator together with the flexible pipe, made from a permeable nylon fabric and folded inside a containment casing. The bag is designed to ensure, once fully inflated, correct absorption of impact energy while maintaining the occupants'' heads at a proper distance from the impact area.Once fully inflated, the gas leaves the bag immediately through the porous fabric.

- plastic clips that secure the bag to the ceiling side rail;

- plastic references to secure the bag to the vehicle;

- a retaining strap secured to the front pillar that holds the bag in position once inflated;

Operation

The gas generator is electrically activated by means of a signal from the electronic control module. Following this signal, pyrotechnic activation is triggered, causing the gas in the generator to expand. The expanding gas emerges through special openings and is distributed evenly along the length of the bag through the hose to make the bag inflate. The increase in the bag volume causes the container to break, the stitching to yield and the opening of the trim thereby allowing the bag to come out correctly downwards. The bag is made to inflate correctly by a retaining strap on the front end of the bag. When fully inflated, the bag is in an optimum position to protect the occupant. The bag deflates immediately after being inflated by the gas because the fabric is permeable.

Side impact satellite sensors


Specifications

The side protection system consists of the air bags and also an electronic control circuit that ensures the system operates correctly. Two satellite sensors containing an accelerometer are installed inside the side pillars to measure accelerations resulting from side impact.

Operation

If the acceleration measured by the sensor is above a preset level, the information is compared with the measurement from the safety sensor housed in the air bag system electronic control unit. If the values measured are consistent, the control unit controls the activation of the side bags and the windows on the side affected by the impact only.

Front seat belt pretensioners

Specifications

The pretensioners - located on the front seat belt buckles - are pyrotechnic devices that are activated by a signal from the electronic control unit. The same logic that controls the Air Bags controls the activation of the pretensioners of the seat belts.

The pretensioners perform the function of recovering any slack in the seat belt and retaining the occupant against the backrest from the very start of the impact, thus reducing the overall movement of the occupant inside the passenger compartment.

All the seat belts are also fitted with load limiters which decrease the force transmitted by the belts to the chest: the limiter power is sufficient to considerably reduce the risk of fracturing shoulder blades and ribs, even in people with brittle bones (e.g. the elderly).

Operation

The following figure shows the pretensioner mechanism.


1 - Gas generator

2 - Piston

3 - Cylinder

4 - Metal cable

When the car decelerates to a sufficient extent, as measured by the system accelerometers, the electronic sensor in the control unit sends a signal to trigger the gas generator pyrotechnic charges. Propellent combustion develops a chemical reaction with the formation of an inert gas. The gas pressure generates a force that presses a piston. The piston is connected to a metal cable that pulls the buckle branch down by mean of a rotation pin.

Once the pretensioners have been activated, the belts are locked and must be replaced.

Air bag system warning lights


1 airbag system fault warning light on the instrument panel

2 passenger air bag disabled warning light on the front courtesy light;

3 warning light signalling seat belts not fastened, in the instrument panel (until DEC 05, in the front courtesy light)

Air bag system fault warning light

With the TEG inserted, the fault warning light (red) comes on for about four seconds (initial self-diagnosis stage) and then goes off. If the control unit detects a fault in the warning light, it memorises the corresponding fault code.

If no system faults are detected at key on and no fault conditions are present in the fault memory, the warning light goes off after four seconds of self-diagnosis.Otherwise it stays on.

The warning light remains lit or comes on during driving in the following instances:

- the control unit detects a fault in the air bag system;

- the control unit detects an impact with system activation;

- when a fault is detected in the warning light connection circuit.

As a consequence of a crash when the pretensioners or side bags alone are activated, the warning light stays on until system operating conditions are restored (replacement of affected part or control unit repair using an EXAMINER)

If the crash causes the front air bag modules to activate, the warning light stays on permanently because the control unit cannot reset (in this case the control unit must be replaced).

If, during the life of the control unit, internal operating errors are detected that cannot be reset by an EXAMINER, this is signalled by the permanent lighting up of the warning light.

Passenger air bag disabled warning light

With the TEG inserted, the yellow passenger side air bag disabled warning light comes on for about four seconds (initial self-diagnosis stage) and flashes for the next 4 seconds.

If the control unit detects a fault in the warning light, it memorises the corresponding fault code, lights up the warning light and keeps the passenger-side Air Bag disabled.

To restore system operation, operate using an EXAMINER as with the system failure warning light.

The passenger side module MUST be disabled only with the TEG NOT INSERTED IN THE TEG READER or this operation will cause an error to be stored in the control unit.

Seat belt reminder

"FIRST REMINDER CYCLE"; WARNING LIGHT SIGNAL

The "seat belts" warning light signals that the (driver''s or passenger or rear seat) seat belts are not fastened in accordance with the following strategy:

- the warning light comes on in fixed mode, at key-on, if the seat belt has not already been fastened;

- the warning light comes on in fixed mode if, with key on, the belt is unfastened (unless the following conditions apply, when the warning light should flash);

- if the belt is already fastened, the warning light remains off at key on;

- the warning light goes out if, the seat belt is fastened at key on.

- the warning light should remain off at key off.

The constant warning light indication cannot be excluded.

"WARNING CYCLE": INDICATION VIA WARNING LIGHT AND BUZZER

The warning cycle is activated for approximately 90 seconds whenever at least one of the following conditions is present under key-on conditions with the seat belt unfastened:

- time elapsed equal to 30 secs and vehicle speed above 4 km/h

- vehicle speed above 25 km/h

- distance travelled more than 500 m

The warning cycle comprises of the warning light flashing (at a frequency of 2 Hz D.C. 50 %), combined with a buzzer sounding.

Operation during the 90 second cycle:

- if the seat belts are fastened, the warning cycle is interrupted immediately (the warning light goes out and the buzzer stops) and the system is ready for a new signal cycle.

- if one of the three events that caused the activation of the warning cycle ceases, it continues for 90 secs.

- if a further warning is required, the warning light indicates the new event by interrupting the current warning cycle (the acoustic signal stops and the seat belt reminder warning light changes from a flashing to a fixed light) and activating the new signal. The system then resumes the warning cycle prior to the new, most recent event. Of course, if the seat belt is fastened during the indication of the new event, the warning cycle is terminated.

- if the key is turned off, the warning cycle stops immediately.

Operation after 90 seconds:

if the seat belt unfastened status persists:

- the acoustic signalling is deactivated

- the warning light flashing cycle stops and the warning light remains on in fixed mode.

The warning cycle is carried out only once in the event of the above conditions arising: therefore, when the cycle is completed (after 90s), the warning cycle is not repeated if the seat belt remains unfastened.

New warning signal:

The warning cycle is only repeated when the seat belt unfastened condition is detected once again (transition from seat belt fastened to seat belt unfastened), and one of the three conditions mentioned above is present.

Warning cycle exclusion mode (warning light flashing /buzzer)

The user can temporarily deactivate the warning cycle using the following procedure:

Within one minute of key-on, proceed as follows:

- fasten the driver and passenger side seat belts. To make the procedure easier, the seat belts can be fastened before the key on.

- keep the belts fastened (simultaneously) for at least 20 s (with the TEG inserted).

- unfasten at least one of the seat belts.

The warning cycle automatically becomes active again at each new key-on.

The user can permanently deactivate the warning cycle only via the intervention of Technical Service.

The deactivation procedure must be carried out using a diagnostic instrument (Examiner) in communication with the instrument panel.

No one can deactivate the signal produced by the warning light in the dashboard.This is always present (fixed light) if the seat belt is unfastened.
