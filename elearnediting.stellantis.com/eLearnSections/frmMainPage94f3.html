FLUID LEVEL CHECK

  

OK (NO FAULTS DETECTED)

  

FAULT DETECTED

  

INTERVENTION
 

 

Engine oil level

  

Check whether the engine oil level is below the min. mark on the dipstick; in this case, measure the quantity of oil in the sump. It should not be less than 1.5 kg

  

Go to Step 3

  

Quantity of oil in the sump less than 1.5 kg

  

Restore the standard operating conditions; the repair is not covered by the warranty and is at the Customer's charge, because the prescriptions of the Owner Handbook were not complied with.

INTERVENTION
 

 

Exhaust line

  

Check for bleeds in the coupling areas:

- exhaust manifold / cylinder head

- exhaust manifold / turbocharger

- turbocharger / catalytic converter

  

Go to Step 4

  

Presence of bleed in the coupling areas shown

  

Restore the tightening of the fastenings where exhaust gas leaks; carry out a test on the car to make sure that the fault has disappeared
