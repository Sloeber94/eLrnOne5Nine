Comando unità di preriscaldo (C.C. a +Vbatt)

Lo strumento di diagnosi segnala: Errore P0380 (Comando unità di preriscaldo), con dettaglio “C.C. a +Vbatt”

This error is detected as a diagnosis of the final control stage (with details of s.c. to earth, s.c. to +Vbatt or open circuit), or if the electronic control module temperature reading is not within the specified limits for at least 0.3 s (wtih detail of excessive temperature).
 

 

 

Test results
 

0

  

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

  

All OK

  

Problems encountered

  

Operation
 

 

Check that the error details is not S.C. to earth

  

Move on to Step 2

  

The error detail is S.C. to earth

  

Move on to Step 1
 

1

  

ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK

  

All OK

  

Problems encountered

  

Operation
 

 

Ensure fuse F59 (second from the left on the second row from the bottom) in the engine compartment junction unit has not blown.

  

Move on to Step 2

  

Fuse F59 has blown

  

Replace the engine compartment junction unit

 Op. 5505A13 ADDITIONAL JUNCTION UNIT IN ENGINE COMPARTMENT - R.R.
 

2

  

GLOW PLUG OPERATION CHECK

  

All OK

  

Problems encountered

  

Operation

 See Test 5520CA GLOW HEATER PLUGS OPERATION CHECK
