140 - Alfa 159   2.4 JTD 20V   GEARBOX NOISE - IN A SPECIFIC GEAR   S023   

With the engine running, there is a noise (whistling inside the gearbox) when a specific gear is engaged. This noise decreases or disappears on changing gear
 

 

 

Results of the tests
 

1

  

SYNCHRONIZER RING CHECK

  

All OK

  

Problems encountered

  

Operation
 

 

Overhaul the gearbox

 Op. 2110B20 MANUAL GEARBOX (5-6 SPEED) WITH DIFFERENTIAL - DISMANTLING AND REASSEMBLY - WASH AND CHECK PARTS - REPLACE SYNCHRONISERS AND INTERNAL CONTROLS IF NECESSARY

In particular, check the synchronizer rings of the gear engaged: there should be no wear on the inner surfaces.

  

Move on to Step 2

  

Seals worn

  

Replace the synchronizers

 Op. 2110B20 MANUAL GEARBOX (5-6 SPEED) WITH DIFFERENTIAL - DISMANTLING AND REASSEMBLY - WASH AND CHECK PARTS - REPLACE SYNCHRONISERS AND INTERNAL CONTROLS IF NECESSARY
 

2

  

GEAR CHECK

  

All OK

  

Problems encountered

  

Operation
 

 

Overhaul the gearbox

 Op. 2110B20 MANUAL GEARBOX (5-6 SPEED) WITH DIFFERENTIAL - DISMANTLING AND REASSEMBLY - WASH AND CHECK PARTS - REPLACE SYNCHRONISERS AND INTERNAL CONTROLS IF NECESSARY

In particular, check the teeth of the gear engaged: there should be no signs of wear or seizing

  

Move on to Step 3

  

Gears worn

  

Replace the gears involved

 Op. 2110B20 MANUAL GEARBOX (5-6 SPEED) WITH DIFFERENTIAL - DISMANTLING AND REASSEMBLY - WASH AND CHECK PARTS - REPLACE SYNCHRONISERS AND INTERNAL CONTROLS IF NECESSARY

REDUCTION GEAR CHECK

  

All OK

  

Problems encountered

  

Operation
 

 

Check the reduction gear

More specifically:

- it should not be worn or damaged;

- there should not be excessive clearance

  

End of diagnosis

  

Reduction gear damaged

  

Restore the clearance or, if necessary, replace the reduction gear
