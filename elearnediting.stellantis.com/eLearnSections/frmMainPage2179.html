140 - Alfa 159   2.4 JTD 20V   Timing/revs synchronisation   P0016   

Lo strumento di diagnosi segnala: Errore P0016 (Sincronizzazione fase/giri).

This error is detected if the engine speed is above 50 rpm, the control module does not recognize any camshaft signal or the signal is not plausible. The above conditions should be satisfied for at least 0.8 secs.
