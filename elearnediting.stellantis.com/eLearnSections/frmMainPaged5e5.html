Removing ( Refitting )

The following procedure describes the removing-refitting of the left front wheel hub; carry out the same operations for the right front wheel hub.

- Position the vehicle on a lift.

   Op. 4450B02 LEFT FRONT WHEEL - R.R.

1. Remove the two stakings and undo the nut (1a) securing the front wheel hub to the driveshaft using the tool (1b) for counter-torque.

    Op. 3310A48 FRONT BRAKE DISC (ONE), LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R

    Op. 3310A59 FRONT BRAKE DISC GUARD (ONE), LEFT OR RIGHT- R.R

1. Undo the screws (1a) and remove the front wheel hub (1b) from the pillar.

Refitting ( Removing )

1. Place the front wheel hub (1a) in its housing and secure it by tightening the screws (1b) to the prescribed torque.

Counter-torque

  

Loosen/tighten nut securing front driveshaft to wheel hub

  

- Tighten the nut to a torque of 13.5 ÷ 16.5 daNm.

- Undo the nut by 90° ÷ 115°

- Pretighten the nut to a torque of 6.7 ÷ 7.4 daNm.

- Tighten the nut through an angle of 45° ÷ 49°

- Check that the final tightening of the nut is 20.0 ÷ 38.0 daNm.

- Stake the nut collar twice.
