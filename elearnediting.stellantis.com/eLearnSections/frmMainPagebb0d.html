OPERATIONS IN NETWORK

During a service operation that involves replacing the bumper, the new part obtained from the Parts Dept. may have different size slots (A – Fig. 1): 12 mm or 17 mm.

As it is no longer possible to supply mounting brackets part no. 50501476 with the pre-modification M6 female screws (which use 12 mm slots), the new mounting brackets part no. 50505251, available from the Parts Dept., have post-modification M8 female screws (which use 17 mm slots).

The repair conditions described below will then be verified:

1) – the replacement of the pre-modification lower mounting brackets (part no. 50501476 – with M6 female screws) with post-modification brackets (part no. 50505251 – with M8 female screws);

2) – the replacement of the front bumper only, obtained from the Parts Dept. with 17 mm slots (A – Fig. 1) with pre-modification brackets (with M6 female screws);

3) – the replacement of the front bumper only, obtained from the Parts Dept. with 12 mm slots (A – Fig. 1) with post-modification brackets (with M8 female screws);

4) – the replacement of the post-modification lower mounting brackets (with M8 female screws) with brackets of the same type;

If any of the conditions described above is present, carry out the operation following the Operating Cycle below.

OPERATION CYCLE

1) – Replacement of the pre-modification lower mounting brackets (with M6 female screws - part no. 50501476) with post-modification brackets (with M8 female screws - part no. 50505251)

Remove the guard under the engine with reference to Op. 7055B54 in the Service Manual then, working on both sides of the vehicle, undo the fixing bolts and remove the lower pre-modification bumper mounting brackets (with M6 female screws).

Before fitting the new post-modification brackets (with M8 female screws) it is necessary to:

-protect the bumper appropriately at the bracket mounting points (D – Fig. 1) in order not to damage the paintwork.-using a suitable size round file or a cutter for sensitive materials, extend the left and right lower side fastening slots for the bumper from 12 mm to 17 mm as illustrated in Fig. 1.

    2) – Replacement of the front bumper only with a new one with 17 mm slots (A – Fig. 2) with pre-modification brackets (with M6 female screws)

    Remove the front bumper with reference to Op. 7210E10 in the Service Manual.

    Working on both sides of the vehicle, undo the fixing bolts and remove the pre-modification lower bumper mounting brackets (with M6 female screws), replace them with the post-modification brackets (part no. 50505251 with M8 female screws) and tighten the fixing bolts.

    Then refit the new front bumper with reference to Op. 7210E10 in the Service Manual.

    3) – Replacement of the front bumper only with a new one with 12 mm slots (A – Fig. 2) with post-modification brackets (with M8 female screws)
