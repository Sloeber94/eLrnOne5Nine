Removing ( Refitting )

   Op. 1016A10 SOUND-PROOFING COVER - R.R


1. Disconnect the pipe from the injectors to the fuel recovery manifold, fuel recovery manifold side.

2. Push springs (2a) manually and disconnect pipe (2b) from the coupling on the injectors, then remove it.
Refitting ( Removing )

- Fit the pipe from the injectors to the fuel recovery manifold and connect to the fuel recovery manifold.

- Connect the pipe from the injectors to the fuel recovery manifold to the injectors and push home until the clips click into place.
