140 - Alfa 159   2.4 JTD 20V   CLIMATE CONTROL AIR FLOW IRREGULAR   L003   

APPLICABLE TO VERSIONS WITH: Automatic climate control

The flow of air sent by the fan in the passenger compartment using the manual speed control is not sufficient; it does not correspond to the speed set.

APPLICABLE TO VERSIONS WITH: Automatic climate control

 

0

  

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Connect the Examiner diagnostic equipment and check the following at the Body Computer Node: - that the Logistic Mode is deactivated (with this function activated there is a limit on the passenger compartment heater fan speed)

  

Move on to Step 1

  

Logistic Mode function activated.

  

Deactivate the Logistic Mode function.
 

1

  

BATTERY CHECK

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Disconnect the battery and reconnect it with the Key Off and wait for 2 minutes for the Key On checking whether there is a variation in the air flow rate.

  

End of diagnosis

  

There is no variation in the air flow rate.

  

Move on to Step 2
 

2

  

CHECK ON OPERATION OF FAN BY CLIMATE CONTROL SYSTEM CONTROL UNIT

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

With the engine started, set the climate control system control unit to the AUTO position, adjust the temperature to 22°C, place the recirculation at the outside air intake and the distribution to the front position.

Adjust the fan button (+ side) gradually increasing the speed set and then decrease the speed using the button (- side), thereby checking the effect of the air flow rate variation.

  

End of diagnosis

  

There is no variation in the air flow rate.

  

Move on to Step 3
 

3

  

CHECK USING DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Connect the Examiner diagnostic equipment and check the following at the Climate Control Node:

  • that there are no errors at the passenger compartment fan;

  • that there are no errors at the distribution actuators;

  • that there are no errors linked to the fan (short circuit errors).

  

Move on to Step 4

  

1. Error at the passenger compartment fan.

  

1. Move on to Step 7
 

2. Short circuit to earth error at the distribution actuators.

  

2. Check that the Lin Bus cables are not caught between the metal parts (pay special attention to the gearbox bolts) and move on to Step 7.
 

3. Short circuit to error to +12V at the distribution actuators.

  

3. Check that the Lin Bus cables are not caught between the metal parts (pay special attention to the gearbox bolts) and move on to Step 7.
 

4. Mechanical lock error / interference at the distribution actuators.

  

4. Move on to Step 9
 

5. Short circuit error at the passenger compartment fan.

  

5. Move on to Step 7
 

4

  

CHECKS USING DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Keeping the Examiner diagnostic equipment connected, check the following through active diagnosis: - the correct operation of the air distribution actuators

  

Move on to Step 5

  

Air distribution actuator not working.

  

Move on to Step 6.
 

5

  

CHECK ON AIR DUCTS AND POLLEN FILTER

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check that the air ducts are intact and the vents are not blocked and are correctly positioned in the opening. Check that the pollen filter is not obstucted.

  

Move on to Step 5

  

1. Air ducts or vents obstructed.

  

1. Restore the operation of the air ducts and the vents removing any material causing an obstruction.
 

2. Pollen filter blocked.

  

2. Replace the pollen filter

 Op. 5010B09 PURIFYING (POLLEN) FILTER FILTER ELEMENT - REPLACE
 

6

  

ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check the continuity/insulation and visually inspect for any looseness or damage at the electrical connection contacts:

  • of the air distribution actuators

  • of the climate control system control unit connector

 See E6020 AIR CONDITIONING

 

Move on to Step 7

  

1. Electrical connections loose or damaged.

  

1. Renew the electrical connections.
 

2. Electrical connection broken or not insulated.

  

2. Restore the continuity/insulation of the electrical connection.
 

7

  

CHECK ON ELECTRICAL CONNECTIONS AND FAN OPERATION

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check whether the electrical wiring connections are loose.

Disconnect/reconnect the connection to the engine mounting.

Disconnect the connector and connect to a feeder.

In these conditions the motor should rotate at the maximum speed indicating that the operating module is not working

  

Move on to Step 8

  

1.Electrical connections loose.

  

1.Renew the correct connections

 See E6020 AIR CONDITIONING

 

2.The fan does not work.

  

2.Replace the fan

 Op. 5040C30 AIR CONDITIONING FAN - R.R.
 

3.The operating module does not work.

  

3.Replace the fan operating module

Op. 5040C40

 

8

  

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION OF DISTRIBUTION ACTUATOR

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check whether the electrical wiring connections are loose.

Disconnect and visually inspect for damage at the connector contacts and reconnect the connection to the motor mounting.

If it persists, before replacing the actuator, check its operation by manually activating the function that the actuator should carry out.

With the engine running, switch on the fan at maximum speed.

Place the flaps in the various distribution positions via the control/control unit (manually).

Check the air flow in the passenger compartment with your hand at the vents.

  

End of diagnosis

  

1. Electrical connections loose or damaged.

  

1. Renew the electrical connections

 See E6020 AIR CONDITIONING

 

2. There is no air flow intake into the passenger compartment through the vents.

  

2. Move on to Step 9.
 

9

  

CHECK FOR IMPEDIMENTS TO THE ROTATION OF THE DISTIRUBTION ACTUATOR

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Remove the actuator and check using the control unit control that the actuator turns checking for mechanical impediments to its rotation.

  

End of diagnosis

  

1.The actuator does not turn.

  

1.Replace the timing actuator

Op. 5040D63 AIR DISTRIBUTION FLAP CONTROL ACTUATORS- R.R. WITH AIR CONDITIONING CASING REMOVED
 

2.Kinematic components or flaps broken/stuck

  

2.Replace the components that is faulty.
