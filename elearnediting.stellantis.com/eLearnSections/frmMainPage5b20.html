Removing ( Refitting )

The 4x2 version is described below

- Position the vehicle on a lift.

1. Disconnect the electrical connection for the exhaust gas temperature sensor on the particulate filter.

   See K188 CAT. DOWNSTREAM TEMPERATURE SENSOR

2. Open the wiring retaining clip for the exhaust gas temperature sensor on the particulate filter.

1. Undo the exhaust gas temperature sensor on the particulate filter and remove it.

- Place the exhaust gas temperature sensor in its housing on the particulate filter and tighten it to the recommended torque.
