Engines

The MY 2008 Alfa 159 versions feature the following engines (already fitted on the previous versions).

-1.8 (1796 cc) four cylinders in line, 16 valves, running on petrol with Multipoint electronic injection with a power output of 103kW (140 bhp) at 6300 rpm.-1.9JTS (1859 cc) four cylinders in line, 16 valves, running on petrol with direct electronic injection with a power output of 118kW (160 bhp) at 6500 rpm.-2.2JTS (2198 cc) four cylinders in line, 16 valves, running on petrol with direct electronic injection with a power output of 136kW (185 bhp) at 6500 rpm.-3.2JTS (3195 cc) six cylinders, 24 valves, running on petrol with direct electronic injection with a power output of 191kW (260 bhp) at 6200 rpm.-1.9JTDM (1910 cc) four cylinders in line, 8 valves, running on diesel with common rail direct injection with a power output of 88kW (120 bhp) at 4000 rpm.-1.9JTDM (1910 cc) four cylinders in line, 16 valves, running on diesel with common rail direct injection with a power output of 110kW (150 bhp) at 4000 rpm.-2.4JTDM (2387 cc) four cylinders in line, 20 valves, running on diesel with common rail direct injection with a power output of 147kW (200 bhp) at 4000 rpm (for Q-Tronic versions)-2.4JTDM (2387 cc) four cylinders in line, 20 valves, running on diesel with common rail direct injection with a power output of 154kW (210 bhp) at 4000 rpm (excluding Q-Tronic versions)

4x4 transmission

As on previous versions, the Alfa 159 3.2JTS and 2.4JTDM will have permanent 4x4 drive with three differentials (the centre one being Torsen C self-locking).

The Alfa Romeo Q4 four driving wheels system works with permanent and dynamic distribution of the drive to the four wheels producing maximum levels of active safety and sports performance.

The Torsen C self-locking centre differential manages the drive torque in the most suitable way sharing it 57% to the rear wheels and 43% to the front wheels. The system also ensures excellent drive in all grip conditions thanks to the automatic slipping control.

3.2jts v6 my 08 with 4x2 transmission

In addition to the 4x4 version the 3.2JTS V6 MY 08 version also has 4x2 drive like on all other versions.

We also wish to point out that the other mechanical specifications on this version remain unchanged compared with the 4x4 version.

Gearbox

All the gearboxes are 6 speed (except the 1.8 version which has a 5 speed gearbox) with ratios designed to highlight performance. The engagement guides have been reduced and special synchronizers have been used to facilitate sliding and manoeuvrability during gear changes.

3 types of gearbox are currently adopted to take full advantage of the performance of the Alfa 159 engines:

-F17 - 5 speed for 1.8 versions-M32 - 6 speed for 1.9JTS, 2.2JTS and 1.9JTDM 8/16v versions-F40 - 6 speed for 3.2JTS and 2.4JTDM 20v versions.

Braking system

As on previous versions, the braking system for the Alfa 159 MY 08 is available (fitted as standard or on request) with the most sophisticated electronic and mechanical assistance systems, namely:

-ABS: anti-lock brakes-EBD: electronic brakeforce distribution between the front and rear wheels-HBA (Hydraulic Brake Assistance): automatic increase in braking pressure during emergency braking-VDC: vehicle electronic stability control.-Q2 electronic (with VDC): this allows an increase in stability when cornering reacting in real time to the loss in grip of the wheels through local intervention of the braking system-ASR (with VDC): traction control via management of the brakes and engine control unit-MSR (with VDC): Engine torque increase when changing down or braking in certain conditions, preventing the wheels from locking-Hill-holder (with VDC): assisted hill start system that keeps the vehicle braked while parked on slopes and automatically releases the brakes when the driver wishes to set off.

Brembo front brake calipers

All Alfa 159 MY 08 versions have monobloc aluminium front calipers with radial mountings (they were previously only fitted on teh 3.2JTS and 2.4JTDM 20v versions) which means that the caliper (A- Fig. 1) is a single block of machined aluminium instead of being composed of two shells bolted to one another like on previous versions.

Although the design of the new calipers (A) is the same their dimensions differ (smaller on the 1.8, 1.9JTS, 2.2JTS, 1.9JTDM 8/16v).

These technical solutions have been chosen to increase the strength of the caliper, whilst being lighter and therefore provide higher performance in sports use.

Both calipers have larger pads fitted which requires the use of four pistons (two on each side) to distribute the braking pressure at the disc evenly.

The fitting of new brake calipers (A) on new aluminium steering knuckles has involved a new layout of the pipe (B).

    Q2 electronic system

    Introduced on the Alfa 159 MY 08 versions (Saloon and Sportwagon) the new Q2 Electronic system is described briefly below.

    The Q2 Electronic system is a device incorporated with the VDC stability control system and allows an increase in stability when cornering reacting in real time to the loss in grip of the wheels through the local intervention of the braking system producing behaviour similar to a limited slip differential (for example a Torsen mechanical device).

    In particular, the front braking system, in cornering acceleration conditions, acts appropriately on the inner wheel, increasing the drive of the outer wheel (with more load) and sharing the torque between the front drive wheels dynamically and continuously according to the driving conditions and the road surface.

    On 4x2 versions the Q2 Electronic system is combined with the front suspension.

    Whilst on 4x4 versions the Q2 Eletronic system is linked with the rear axle further increasing the pleasant and easy driving conditions of the Q4 giving an even greater feeling of sporty driving: the behaviour is like a 4x4 with a centre Torsen and rear electronic self-locking differential.
