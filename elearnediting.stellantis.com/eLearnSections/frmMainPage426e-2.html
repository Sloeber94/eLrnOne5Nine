Removing ( Refitting )

- Position the vehicle on a lift.

- Raise the vehicle.

1. Remove the right (1a) or left (1b) rear side protection after having undone the bolts (1c) or (1d).
Refitting ( Removing )

- Check that the rear side protection is not damaged.

- Position the rear side protection and tighten the bolts.

- Remove the vehicle from the lift.
