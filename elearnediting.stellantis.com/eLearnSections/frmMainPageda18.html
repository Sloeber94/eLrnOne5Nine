Noise - Service Solution


FAULT COMPLAINED OF

Noise from front suspension during steering, parking or in case of wide radius bends.

TECHNICAL CAUSE

Incorrect matching between bearing and post.

OPERATIONS IN PRODUCTION

Adoption of new A posts with improved matching.

OPERATIONS IN NETWORK

Network behaviour standards towards the Customer

-Once the claimed fault has been confirmed, reassure the Customer that the problem will be solved by replacing both A posts with new ones, from the Spare Parts Dpt., having improved features.-After the operation, show the Customer what has been done and make sure he is satisfied.

Service Solution

Once the customer's claimed fault is found:

-for 159 only: on cars with chassis number ranging between 7205227 and 7283690 only;-for Brera model: on cars with chassis number higher than 5023999 only;

replace both front A posts with new ones, from the Spare Parts Dpt., according to the following Operating Cycle.

OPERATION CYCLE

Place the car on the lift then, with reference to Op. 4450C28 of the Service Manual:

-Undo both fixing screws (2) and remove the front hub (1 - Fig. 1) from the front suspension post. Repeat the operation on the opposite side of the car.

Fig. 1



    -Remove the A post (1 - Fig. 2) with reference to Op. 4450C10 of the Service Manual. Repeat the operation on the opposite side of the car.

    Pay attention while arranging the clamps of the extractor to remove the head of the lower wishbone.

    Fig. 2



      -Replace the removed posts with new ones, from the Spare Parts Dpt. and install them on the car, with reference to Op. 4450C10 of the Service Manual.-Refit the previously removed hubs on both suspension posts, with reference to Op. 4450C28 of the Service Manual.

      Check the front wheel geometry, with reference to Op. 4450A10 of the Service Manual.
