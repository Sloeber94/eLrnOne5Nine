While driving with the roof fully open, noise can be heard from the sunroof area
 

0

  

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check whether the noise only occurs with the roof open.

  

Move on to Step 1

  

1. The noise is heard with the roof closed

  

1. Proceed as instructed in the diagnosis

 See table S076 SUN ROOF NOISY / WITH ROOF CLOSED
 

2. The noise is heard with the roof open to the swivel position.

  

2. Proceed as instructed in the diagnosis

 See table S077 SUN ROOF NOISY / WITH HINGED OPENING SUN ROOF
 

1

  

SUN ROOF FASTENING CHECK

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check the correct fastening:

  • of the transparent roof panel to the slides;

  • of the full roof mechanism to the body

  

Move on to Step 2

  

Faulty fastenings.

  

Restore the fastening of the entire mechanism and roof

 Op. 7030B12 SLIDING ROOF (ASSEMBLY) - R+R
 

2

  

CHECK SUN ROOF CONTROL DEVICES

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check the operation of the sun-roof operating devices:

  • with the sunroof open to the swivel setting, ensure the rubber fittings are present on the rear part of the slides;

  • with the sunroof open to the swivel setting, check the condition and correct fastening of the end travel clips and central gutter slides;

  • with the sunroof fully open, check the condition and correct fastening of the spoiler rubber end travel stops.

  

End of diagnosis

  

1. Faulty fastenings.

  

1. Restore all the components and the fastening of the entire mechanism

 Op. 7030B SLIDING ROOF
 

2. Faults in the sunroof operating devices.

  

2. Replace the affected parts

 Op. 7030B SLIDING ROOF
