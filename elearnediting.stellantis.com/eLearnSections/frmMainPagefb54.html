Removing ( Refitting )

To remove the alternator, adopt one of two procedures according to the lower alternator fastenging type.

If the head of the lower bolt is on the outside of the vehicle, the engine must be removed.

If the head of the lower bolt faces inward, proceed as follows.

    Op. 0010T84 AIR CONDITIONING SYSTEM REFRIGERANT - DEHYDRATING AND REPRESSURIZING

    Op. 7055B54 PROTECTION/GUARD UNDER ENGINE -R.R.

    Op. 4450B04 RIGHT FRONT WHEEL - R.R.

    Op. 7055B91 RIGHT FRONT WHEEL ARCH LINER - R.R.

    Op. 7210E10 R.R. FRONT BUMPER

    Op. 1092C26 BELT FROM AIR CONDITIONER COMPRESSOR DRIVING POWER STEERING PUMP - R + R

    Op. 5040A20 AIR CONDITIONING COMPRESSOR - R.R. INCLUDES CHECKING FLUID LEVEL AND TOPPING UP IF NECESSARY

1. Working from the top of the engine compartment, unscrew electrical connections (1a) and (1b) of alternator (1c).

3. Keep the alternator releasing the retainers for the cables.

Refitting ( Removing )

- Check that the alternator is not damaged.

- If the engine has been removed, proceed as follows:

1. Place the alternator in the housing.

2. Tighten the lower bolt.

4. Tighten the alternator fixing bolts to the recommended torque.
