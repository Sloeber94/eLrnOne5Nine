140 - Alfa 159   2.4 JTD 20V   FLASHING, SWITCHING OFF OR VARIATION IN DIPPED HEADLAMPS BRIGHTNESS   I060   

The headlamps come on normally, but they go off/come back on or flash during operation.

NOTE: if the headlamp control unit is being replaced, the system must be calibrated using the diagnostic equipment.
 

1

  

CHECK USING DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check that there are no errors by connecting to the slave headlamp (right side) control unit.

Check that there are no errors by connecting to the master headlamp (left side) control unit.

  

End of diagnosis

  

1. Presence of error detected

B1010 - Xenon fails to come on

  

1. Move on to Step 2
 

2. Presence of error detected

B1050 - Supply wiring resistance

  

2. Move on to Step 3
 

3. Presence of error detected

B1001 - Xenon high voltage operation

  

3. Move on to Step 4
 

2

  

CHECK ON CONNECTOR AT HEADLAMP

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check that the 14-way connector on the slave lamp (right side) is correctly fitted

Check that the 14-way connector on the master lamp (left side) is correctly fitted

 See E2011 DIPPED HEADLAMPS

 

Move on to Step 3

  

Connector at headlamp not correctly fitted

  

Refit correctly

 See E2011 DIPPED HEADLAMPS

 

3

  

ELECTRICAL SYSTEM LIGHTS CHECK

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check that the fuses are correctly inserted.

Check the condition of the wiring from the under-dashboard junction unit to the headlamp

 See E2011 DIPPED HEADLAMPS

Check that the headlamps are receiving the correct power supply, by carrying out the

 See Test 5540BA DIPPED BEAM CIRCUIT CHECK

  

Move on to Step 4

  

 

 

4

  

CONTROL UNIT CONNECTORS CHECK

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check that the connector for the screened cable for the headlamp bulb is correctly fitted in the housing for the ballast control unit.

Check that the connector for the screened cable for the headlamp bulb is correctly fitted in the housing for the bulb connector.

Check that the connector for the headlamp is correct fitted in the one for the ballast control unit.

  

Move on to Step 6

  

1. Screened cable not correctly fitted in the ballast control unit

  

1. Refit correctly (master and slave headlamp)

 See E2011 DIPPED HEADLAMPS

 

2. Screened cable not correctly fitted on the bulb

  

2. Refit correctly (master and slave headlamp)

 See E2011 DIPPED HEADLAMPS

 

3. Coupling between headlamp connector and ballast control unit not correct

  

3. Refit correctly (master and slave headlamp)

 See E2011 DIPPED HEADLAMPS

 

5

  

CHECK ON OPERATION OF BULBS

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check whether the fault disappears if the bulbs are replaced

  

End of diagnosis

  

The fault persists

  

Move on to Step 6
 

6

  

CHECK ON OPERATION OF BALLAST CONTROL UNITS

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Replace the ballast control units and check that the dipped headlamps are working properly

  

End of diagnosis

  

The fault persists

  

Contact the Service Department for further information on the problem
