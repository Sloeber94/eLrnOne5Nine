7010 1 541 BA REAR WINDOW LIFTERS
Breakage of the window lifter - Service Solution


FAULT COMPLAINED OF

Impossibility of moving the window glass of one or both rear doors.

TECHNICAL CAUSE

Breakage of one or both window lifting mechanisms.

OPERATIONS IN PRODUCTION

The assembly of improved window lifting mechanisms is guaranteed.

OPERATIONS IN NETWORK

Network behaviour standards towards the Customer

-Once the claimed fault is confirmed, reassure the Customer that it can be solved by replacing one or both window lifting mechanisms.-After the operation, show the Customer what has been done and make sure he is satisfied.

Service Solution

If the claimed fault is confirmed, replace one or both window lifting mechanisms proceeding according to the Operating Cycle below.

OPERATION CYCLE

Replace one or both window lifting mechanisms, with reference to Op. 7010M27 of the Service Manual.
