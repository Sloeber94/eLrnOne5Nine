140 - Alfa 159   2.4 JTD 20V   NOISE DURING FRONT SEAT ADJUSTMENT   S066   (Electric seats)

Noise during adjustment
 

1

  

CHECK ON FASTENING OF MOTOR TO SEAT FRAME

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check that the motor(s) is(are) correctly fastened to the seat frame

  

Move on to Step 2

  

Fastening of motor(s) to seat frame loose.

  

Retighten motor(s) on seat frame to torque.
 

2

  

SEAT INTERFERENCE CHECK

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check for interference between the seat and the vehicle floor carpet (lengthwise adjustment).

  

Move on to Step 3

  

Interference between carpet and seat (sliding guides).

  

Remove interference or move parts in contact apart.
 

3

  

CHECK ON CONTACTS BETWEEN MOTOR AND SEAT FRAME

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check for the contacts between the motor(s) and the seat frame or interference with the gears/linkage.

  

End of diagnosis

  

Contacts/interference between motor(s) and frame.

  

Remove interference or space contacts apart, if necessary replacing seat frame or motor
