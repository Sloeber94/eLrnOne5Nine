140 - Alfa 159   2.4 JTD 20V   THE CRUISE CONTROL SET- CONTROL IS NOT WORKING   D075   

The Cruise Control system does not decrease the speed when the ring nut is turned to "SET -".
 

0

  

PRELIMINARY CHECK

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check, by carrying out a road test, then the vehicle speed is higher than 40 km/h when the SET- ring nut is adjusted

  

Move on to Step 1

  

Speed below 40 Km/h

  

Bring the speed to above 40 Km/h
 

1

  

CHECK USING DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Connect the diagnostic test equipment (Examiner or other instruments) to the tester input.

Check for the presence of faults in the engine management control unit

  

Move on to Step 2

  

1. Error detected in the control unit

  

1. Continue following the instructions for the diagnostic equipment
 

2. No communication with the control unit

  

2.Restore the connection between the engine management control unit and the diagnostic connector:

 See E5060 CRUISE CONTROL
 

2

  

CHECK USING DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Connect the diagnostic equipment to the engine management control unit and check that the SET- position for the lever with the correct operaiton of the Cruise Control system contacts

Check the continuity of the Cruise lever contacts

 See Test 5580AD Cruise Control "SET -" control check

  

Move on to Step 3

  

1.No continuity for lever contacts

  

1. Replace the Cruise Control lever assembly

 Op. 5580A10 CRUISE CONTROL LEVER ASSEMBLY - R+R
 

2. Power supply to lever interrupted

  

2. Move on to Step 3
 

3

  

SELECTOR CONTROL CHECK

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check that the Cruise Control contacts are working properly in the "-" position

  

Move on to Step 4

  

No continuity

  

Replace the Cruise Control lever assembly

 Op. 5580A10 CRUISE CONTROL LEVER ASSEMBLY - R+R
 

4

  

SUPPLY CHECK

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

With the ignition key in the ON position and the Cruise Control in the ON position and the ring nut at "Set-", check that there is a voltage signal from the Cruise Control to the engine management control unit:

 See E5060 CRUISE CONTROL

  

Move on to Step 5

  

No voltage detected due to broken wiring

  

Repair the connections:

 See E5060 CRUISE CONTROL
 

5

  

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check that the fault is no longer present by carrying out

 See Test 5580AE CRUISE CONTROL OPERATION ROAD TEST

  

End of diagnosis

  

The fault persists: control unit defective

  

Replace the engine management control unit

 Op. 1056B82 INJECTION/IGNITION SYSTEM E.C.U. (ONE) - R + R
