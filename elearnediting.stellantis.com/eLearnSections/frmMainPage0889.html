- The side window sealant strip can be cut using a piano wire or vibrating knife; both methods are described below.

- To cut using a piano wire, proceed as described below.

1. Cut a length of wire of about 50 cm then, with the help of pliers, introduce one end (1a) of the piano wire through the bead of sealant (1b) securing the windscreen (1c).

2. Fasten one end (1a) of the wire to the special retaining tool (2a) located inside the vehicle. Fasten one end (2b) of the wire to the traction handle (2c) located outside the vehicle.

3. The mechanic inside the vehicle should keep the end of the tool (2c) on the bead of sealant (3a) about 30 cm from the point where the wire passes through. The other mechanic, outside the vehicle, should pull the handle (2c) following the surround of the rearscreen and they should cut the bead of sealant (3a). Repeat the operation in sections of about 30 cm until the bead of sealant (3a) is completely cut. Take care during the cutting stage over the two window centering pins (3b).

APPLICABLE TO VERSIONS WITH: Station Wagon

- To cut using a vibrating knife, proceed as described below.

1. Use the vibrating knife (1a) and blade (1b) and cut the sealant strip (1c) along the perimeter of the window working from the inside of the vehicle. Take care during the cutting stage over the two window centering pins (1d).

2. After cutting round the entire sealant strip, apply suction pads to the window; then remove the rearscreen and rest it on a support stand.

APPLICABLE TO VERSIONS WITH: Station Wagon


 Refitting ( Removing )

1. Using suitable equipment, cut and level the bead of sealant in the side window housing leaving a thickness of between 0.25 and 1 mm, taking care not to scratch the paintwork. The thickness of the sealant remaining in the housing will act as a support for the new bonding.

2. Thoroughly clean the side window housing using an air jet and degrease fully using heptane. When bonding, 60 minutes after the bead of sealant has been cut and levelled, the surface of the actual sealant must be treated using the special activating product and it is necessary to wait for 15 minutes before fitting the windscreen.

- New window preparation.

1. Degrease the screen-printed perimeter of the new window to be bonded using heptane.

2. Apply the glass adhesion promoter (primer) (2b) to the serigraphed perimeter using the wick (2a) supplied in the Kit.

Wait for about 15 minutes before continuing with the next operation to allow the adhesion promoter (primer) thinner to evaporate.

APPLICABLE TO VERSIONS WITH: Station Wagon

- Preparing a removed window.

1. A removed window is reusable only if it did not suffer damage during removal; in particular, the seal (1a) present along the entire perimeter of the window must be intact.

2. Using suitable equipment cut and level the bead of sealant on the window so that it is as thin as possible.

The remaining sealant does not have to be completely removed; take care not to damage the black serigraphy on the glass perimeter.

3. Clean the perimeter of the serigraphed area of the windscreen thoroughly with compressed air and degrease it using heptane.

Check that the glass is not damaged.

4. When bonding, 60 minutes after the bead of sealant has been cut and levelled, the surface of the actual sealant must be treated using the special activating product and it is necessary to wait for 15 minutes before fitting the windscreen.
