Removing ( Refitting )

The 4x4 version is described below

    Op. 7055B80 UNDERBODY SIDE PROTECTION (ONE) LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R.

    Op. 4450B13 REAR WHEELS (TWO) - R.R

1. Unscrew the plug and drain the rear differential fluid into a special container.

    Op. 1076B22 REAR EXHAUST SILENCERS - LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R.


1. Unscrew the bolts securing the propeller shaft rear mount to the body and recover any alignment spacers.

Take care to use the same alignment spacers on refitting.

2. Mark the position of the constant velocity joint with the RDA flange, unscrew the bolts securing the propeller shaft to the rear differential and move aside, securing it to the body.

1. Unscrew bolts (1a) and remove rear suspension beam reinforcement bracket (1b).

    Op. 2720C84 REAR DIFFERENTIAL TO CROSSMEMBER MOUNTING BRACKET - R.R.

1. Undo nuts (1a) and remove rear silencer heat shield (1b), left side.

1. Disconnect the handbrake cable from the brake calliper.

2. Undo bolts (2a) securing brake calliper bracket (2b) to the wheel strut.

1. Undo the upper bolt securing the wheel strut to the rear suspension upper transverse arm.

1. Loosen bolt (1a) and undo bolt (1b) securing lower wheel strut (1c) to the longitudinal arm.

1. Undo bolt (1a) securing wheel rpm sensor (1b) to the wheel strut.

- Prise the half-axle out of the differential.

1. Remove the wheel strut complete with half-axle.

- Proceed in the same way to remove the other wheel strut assembly complete with half-axle.

- Position a hydraulic jack fitted with a suitable tool and support the rear differential.

1. Undo bolts (1a) securing rear differential (1b) to the suspension crossmember and remove it.

    Op. 4420C10 REAR SUSPENSION STABILIZER BAR - R+R

- Position a hydraulic jack fitted with a suitable tool and support the rear suspension longitudinal arm.

1. Undo the bolt securing the shock absorber to the rear suspension longitudinal arm.

1. Unscrew the bolts and remove brackets (1a) securing handbrake cables (1b) and move aside.

2. Undo the bolts securing the rear suspension longitudinal arm to the body.

1. Unscrew bolt (1a) and remove rear suspension longitudinal arm (1b).

- Position a hydraulic jack fitted with a suitable tool and support the rear suspension beam.

1. Disconnect the electrical connection for the headlamp alignment sensor.

2. Undo bolts (2a) and remove rear suspension beam (2b).

Refitting ( Removing )

- Assemble both longitudinal arms with the rear suspension beam without tightening the bolts to torque.

- Fit the locating tool on the lift hydraulic jack.
