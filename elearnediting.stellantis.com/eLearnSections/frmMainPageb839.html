THIS PROCEDURE PRODUCES VERY HIGH EXHAUST TEMPERATURES.

Before starting this procedure, proceed as follows:

1. check that the outside of the exhaust pipe and the areas immediately surrounding it are clean;

2. remove the gas extraction intake from the exhaust pipe and make sure that nobody is passing close to the exhaust pipe during the whole procedure;

3. place the vehicle in an open area, far from heat sources;

4. keep the bonnet open during the whole procedure.

The conditions required to perform this procedure are:

1. water temperature higher than 70°C;

2. vehicle at a standstill;

3. engine started and idling;

4. brake and clutch pedals released.

- Connect the diagnosis equipment and follow the specific instructions for this type of operation.
