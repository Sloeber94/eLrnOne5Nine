140 - Alfa 159   2.4 JTD 20V   PASSENGER COMPARTMENT WATER PENETRATION, REAR AREA   V002   

After rain or washing, water infiltration into the rear part of the passenger compartment is noticed.
 

1

  

CHECK FOR INFILTRATIONS THROUGH THE REAR SCREEN

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check for sealant on the rearscreen

 See Test 7035OA Check fixed window glass penetration (windscreen and rearscreen)

  

Move on to Step 2

  

Sealant missing.

  

Restore the sealant

 Op. 7035B04 REAR WINDSCREEN - ELIMINATE LEAKS
 

2

  

REARSCREEN CHECK

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check the rearscreen

In particular, visually check:

  • glass condition;

  • flatness of contact surface.

  

Move on to Step 3

  

Faults in rearscreen.

  

Replace the rearscreen

 Op. 7035B14 REAR WINDOW GLASS - R.R INCLUDES CLEANING HOUSING
 

3

  

BOOT SEAL CHECK

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check the boot seal.

In particular, make sure:

  • there are no kinks in the seal;

  • the seal is intact.

  

Move on to Step 4

  

Seal installation faulty

  

Refit the seal correctly

 Op. 7025B66 BOOT COMPARTMENT SEAL - R+R
 

4

  

CHECK BOOT ALIGNMENT

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check boot door installation.

In particular, check:

  • correct door/compartment alignment

  • seal adheres correctly to the door.

  

End of diagnosis

  

Faults in boot lid installation.

  

Refit the boot lid correctly.

 Op. 7210E54 R.R. BOOT
