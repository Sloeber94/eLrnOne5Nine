Cleaning - Service Manual Update


In addition to the information in the Service Manual, we wish to notify you that the seats, the ceiling, the steering wheels/gear lever knobs and the linings/trims in general should be cleaned in the way described in the operating cycle below.

OPERATING CYCLE

Introduction

We wish to remind you that in order for the fabrics and leathers used for the seat covers, the ceiling linings, the steering wheels/gear lever knobs and the trimsin general to remain in good condition they must be treated with due care, especially when they are being cleaned. If you have any doubts, we advise you to check the cleaning product on a small area of the cover/lining that is not visible.

If you wish to use cleaning products, remind the Customer to use only neutral PH soaps and avoid using products containing alcohol and/or solvents at all costs as they will soon damage the leather and/or fabric irreparably.

Remind the Customer that during cleaning operations (for example cleaning the windscreen) if a special product for glass (containing alcohol) is used and any drops should accidentally come into contact with the seat covers/roof linings, steering wheel/gear lever knob trims or other trims in general, it should be removed immediately and the area involved washed with neutral PH natural soap foam and water.

Also remind the Customer that if a mechanical anti-theft device is fitted to the steering wheel, special care should be taken over positioning it to prevent damaging the leather trim.

When using chemicals, closely follow the safety instructions given on the safety sheet which the supplier must give to the user (in Italy, in accordance with Legislative Decrees 52/97 and 285/98 and later modifications and inclusions).

A - SEAT COVERS

Cleaning fabric or velour seats

.Remove any dust or particles using a vacuum cleaner and then a natural bristle brush which has been dampened first (alternatively, use brushes with an adhesive roller to remove any foreign bodies)..Rub the seats with a sponge soaked in a solution of neutral detergent and water..To remove any stains, such as grease or dirt marks, use the special products available on the market, taking care over the usage instructions.

With velour, the possible formation of fluff is not a sign of a defect/wear; this effect is caused by static electricity and can be removed by cleaning appropriately.

Cleaning "microfibre" fabric seats

.Remove any dust and foreign bodies using a chamois leather or a slightly damp cloth without rubbing;.for stains such as coffee, chocolate, fruit, biro, mud, etc. clean the seats using a cloth soaked in water only and either a water based or amonium solution (2 tablespoons per litre)..for stains such as grease, pencil, etc. we recommended the use of special products available on the market (e.g. dry cleaning products) taking care over the usage instructions.

Cleaning leather trims (seats, panels, steering wheel, gear lever knob, etc.)

Remind the Customer that leather is a natural product and, as such, will always show any imperfections. The appearance of leather with its "imperfections" should therefore not be considered a defect, but rather a feature of its authenticity.

We wish to remind you that real leather seat covers with "imperfections" that may appear to look like aesthetic defects are not that at all but rather a feature of this natural material.

The leather in the area of the seat and the grip may, in time, become shiny; this should not be considered as a defect because it is part of the leather tanning process.

For any doubts over "imperfections" contact Te.Se.O. Product for the Market.

B - CEILING LINING

Cleaning

.Remove any dust and foreign bodies using a vacuum cleaner. Alternatively, use brushes with adhesive rollers capable of removing any foreign bodies..To remove any stains, such as grease or dirt marks, use the special products available on the market, (for example for dry cleaning) taking care over the usage instructions.

Remind the Customer to rub the ceiling linings gently because the composition of the special fabrics used could be altered with piling created because the material is sensitive to rubbing.
