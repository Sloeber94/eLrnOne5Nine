Refitting ( Removing )

- Check that the electric window motor is not damaged.

The bush in which the window motor bolts are engaged must be replaced.

1. Replace the bushes.

2. Position the window motor.

3. Tighten the fixing bolts.

4. Connect the electrical connection.
