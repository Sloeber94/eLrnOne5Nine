- Raise calibrated pin (1c) and check that it comes into contact with the propeller shaft. If this is not the case, proceed with the removing/refitting of the propeller shaft because if the refitting operation has not been carried out correctly the support mountings may be pre-tensioned with the constant velocity joints obviously outside the permitted angle.

The spacers available from the Parts Dept. are 2 and 5 mm and should never be used in pairs.

After fitting the spacers refit the brackets using the special bolts depending on the shim used. For 2 mm shims (no. 50505232) use M8 x 1.25 x 32 bolts (no. 50505230); for 5 mm shims (no. 50505234) use M8 x 1.25 x 40 bolts (no. 50505231).

After fitting the spacers, check the propeller shaft angles once again.
