140 - Alfa 159   2.4 JTD 20V   KNOCKING FROM FRONT SEAT CUSHION   S067   

APPLICABLE TO VERSIONS WITH: Electric seats

Noise/knocking

APPLICABLE TO VERSIONS WITH: Electric seats

 

0

  

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

"Rock" in the seat resting against the backrest; check the fastenings to the floor (two M8 bolts) releasing the tension and check the front mountings.

  

Move on to Step 1

  

Creaking; non alignment of guides during sliding stage (twist effect).

  

Retighten the seat to the floor following the correct guide alignment cycle.
 

1

  

CHECK ON FAULTY CONTACTS AT GUIDE BAYONETS

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Remove the seat from the floor and check for possible irregular contacts at the guide bayonets (front).

  

Move on to Step 2

  

Noise disappears

  

Check the plastic inserts on the floor and lubricate the bayonet area with grease.
 

2

  

CHECK ON GUIDE FASTENINGS TO SEAT

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check the fastenings of the guides to the seat (6 M8 bolts) if the noise disappears.

  

Move on to Step 3

  

Noise disappears

  

Retighten the seat to the floor following the correct tightening cycle.
 

3

  

SEAT LIFTING HINGES CHECK

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check for clearance at the seat lifting hinges and for noise at the torsion spring.

  

Move on to Step 4

  

1. Excessive clearance in the seat lifting hinges.

  

1. Replace the seat frame

 Op. 7045A86 FRONT SEAT FRAME (ONE), LEFT OR RIGHT, R.R.
 

2. Noise from torsion spring.

  

2. Lubricate the torsion spring and check the condition of the bush.
 

4

  

BRACKET CONTACT CHECKS

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check for the presence of contacts or detachment of the bracket.

  

Move on to Step 5

  

1. Contact with cushion components

  

1. Move the bracket away from possible contact with the cushion.
 

2. Detachment of the bracket

  

2. Replacement of connector holder bracket.
 

5

  

CHECK ON FASTENING OF MOTORS TO FRAME

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check that the bolts securing the motors to the frame are correctly tightened.

  

Move on to Step 6

  

Bolts missing/slack on the casing securing the motor to the frame

  

Retighten the motor to the correct torque.
 

6

  

PULLMAFLEX FASTENINGS CHECK

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check for possible detachment of the pullmaflex fastenings on the frame or possible breaks.

  

End of diagnosis

  

Pullmaflex released, pullmaflex fastening tabs open or broken.

  

Reattach pullmaflex and tighten fastening tabs or replace pullmaflex.
