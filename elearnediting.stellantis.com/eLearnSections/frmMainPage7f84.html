1072 1 031 RA SWIRL VALVES ACTUATOR
Irregular operation - Network Information


Cancels and replaces subject 10.01.06 on the same subject transmitted with CD 01/06 through the update of the Parts Availability table.

FAULT COMPLAINED OF

EOBD warning light coming on; the analysis using the Examiner detects the presence of error P2004 and/or P2013.

TECHNICAL CAUSE

Failed / incorrect operation of the flow modification valves actuator in the inlet ducts (swirl) on some Euro4 vehicles at the start of production.

OPERATIONS IN PRODUCTION

Adoption of improved swirl valve actuators (Q9 version) starting from engine no. 4689071.

OPERATIONS IN NETWORK

On Euro4 vehicles fitted with swirl valve actuators with engine numbers below 4689071, if the EOBD warning light is on and errors P2994 and/or P2013 are detected, before replacing the swirl valve actuator or other components, carry out the reset/error deletion procedure described in the Operating Cycle.

If, after having successfully carried out the Operating Cycle below, the fault reoccurs in exactly the same way after a short-medium time, proceed with replacing the swirl valves actuator without repeating the cycle.

IT SHOULD BE REMEMBERED THAT THERE IS CURRENTLY A CAMPAIGN IN PROGRESS FOR A BATCH OF VEHICLES EQUIPPED WITH SWIRL VALVE ACTUATORS DESIGNED TO PREVENT THE EOBD WARNING LIGHT COMING ON THROUGH THE ACTIVATION OF ACTIVE DIAGNOSIS USING THE EXAMINER. THIS CAMPAIGN SHOULD ONLY BE CARRIED OUT ON VEHICLES WITH ENGINE NUMBERS OF 4689071 OR HIGHER AND WHERE THE EOBD WARNING LIGHT IS NOT ON PERMANENTLY.

THEREFORE, IF THE EOBD WARNING LIGHT IS ON PERMANENTLY, IT IS NECESSARY: - IF THE VEHICLE IS IN THE CAMPAIGN BATCH AND HAS AN ENGINE NO. OF MORE THAN/EQUAL TO 4689071 - REPLACE THE SWIRL VALVE ACTUATOR - FOR ALL VEHICLES WITH ENGINE NOS. BELOW 4689071 - CARRY OUT THE OPERATING CYCLE BELOW.

OPERATION CYCLE

-Check the battery charge and, if necessary, recharge it.-Remove the sound insulation cover and check the conformity of the linkage (Fig. 1); in particular, make sure that there are no foreign bodies or excessive dirt that could hinder correct operation; then refit the sound insulation cover.-Delete the errors present in the engine management control unit using the Examiner.-If the errors are deleted correctly the procedure is over; if, on the other hand, the errors continue to be present, check the engine number.-If the engine number is 4689071 or more, replace the swirl valve actuator (see Op. 1072B60 of the Service Manual).-If the engine number is below 4689071, carry out 10 "ignition ON and then OFF" cycles (key on - key off) keeping the key in the OFF position for at least 15 seconds before turning it ON.-If the errors are still present after the 10 key on - key off cycles, continue with up to a maximum of 35 cycles then, if the errors are still present, replace the swirl valve actuator.-If the errors are no longer present, turn the key to the ON position and keep it there for longer than 2 seconds then start the engine.-If no errors are detected after having started the engine, the procedure is over; if, on the other hand, the errors reappear, replace the swirl valve actuator.
