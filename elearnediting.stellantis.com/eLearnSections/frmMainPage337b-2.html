140 - Alfa 159   2.4 JTD 20V   MIL WARNING LIGHT NOT WORKING (REMAINS OFF BEFORE STARTING)   K100   

Go back to GENERAL FAILURE WARNING LIGHT ON CONSTANTLY

The MIL warning light does not come with the ignition on;

the engine failure warning light is switched on by means of an earth command directly from the "engine management node"; during normal operation the engine failure warning light should come on when the ignition is on and go out when the engine is started up.
