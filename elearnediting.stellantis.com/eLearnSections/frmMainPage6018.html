FUNCTIONAL DESCRIPTION

Radio P20 is supplied directly (4 of connector A) from the battery via the line of fuse F23 of the engine compartment junction unit B1 .

The radio P20 receives a "key-on" (INT) power supply (pin 7 of connector A) through the line for fuse F49 of the control unit under the dashboard B2.

Pin 8 of connector A is earthed.

Pin 6 of connector A of P20 receives the power supply for the dimmer lighting, coming from pin 18 of connector H of M1.

    See E2535 CONTROL LIGHTS

The following cables with signals for the speakers leave connector B of P20:

- pins 5 and 6 to the left front speakers P35 and P45;

- pins 3 and 4 to the right front speakers P36 and P46;

- pins 7 and 8 to the left rear speakers P30 and P47;

- pins 1 and 2 to the right rear speakers P31 and P48;

The aerial P25 is connected to the radio by means of the special coaxial cable.
