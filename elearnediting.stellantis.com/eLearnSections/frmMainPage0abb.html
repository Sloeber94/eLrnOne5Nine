Removing ( Refitting )

   Op. 4450B13 REAR WHEELS (TWO) - R.R

- Remove the rear exhaust silencers, with reference to

   Op. 1076B22 REAR EXHAUST SILENCERS - LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R.

   Op. 2720D10 DRIVESHAFT WITH LEFT OR RIGHT JOINT (ONE) - R.R.

1. Unscrew the propeller shaft rear mount bolts to the body and recover any alignment spacers.

Take care to use the same alignment spacers on refitting.

2. Mark the position of the constant velocity joint with the RDA flange, unscrew the bolts securing the propeller shaft to the RDA and move aside, securing it to the body.

- Position a hydraulic jack fitted with a suitable tool and support the rear differential.

1. Undo bolts (1a) securing rear differential (1b) to the suspension crossmember and remove it.

2. Undo the bolt securing rear differential (1b) to the crossmember reinforcement.

- Slowly lower the hydraulic jack to remove the rear differential.
Refitting ( Removing )

- Place the rear differential back in its housing using the hydraulic jack.

- Tighten the bolt securing the rear differential to the crossmember reinforcement.
