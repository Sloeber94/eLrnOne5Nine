Removing ( Refitting )

The 4x2 version is described below

The following procedure describes removal/refitting of the right rear wheel hub. Proceed in the same way for the left rear wheel hub.

- Position the vehicle on a lift.

    Op. 4450B08 RIGHT REAR WHEEL - R.R

    Op. 3310B57 REAR BRAKE CALIPER BRACKET (ONE), LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R.

    Op. 3310B48 REAR BRAKE DISC (ONE), LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R.

1. Unscrew bolts (1a) and remove rear wheel hub (1b).

Refitting ( Removing )

1. Place rear wheel hub (1a) in its housing and secure it by tightening bolts (1b) to the recommended torque.

Counter-torque

 

Loosen/tighten nut securing front driveshaft to wheel hub

 

- Tighten the nut to a torque of 13.5 - 16.5 daNm.

- Undo the nut by 90° - 105°.

- Pretighten the nut to a torque of 6.6 - 7.3 daNm.- Tighten the nut through an angle of 43° - 47°.

- Stake the nut collar twice.
