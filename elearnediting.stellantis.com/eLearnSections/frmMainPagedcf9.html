Instructions for repairing connectors - Service Manual update


Introduction

In addition to the information in the Service Manual in the procedures for assembly 55, we are enclosing the repair instructions for the engine prewiring connectors.

The repairs are as follows:

-Replacement of one or more terminals by cutting the existing cable and join and a thermal restricting tube with a new cable with an already crimped terminal.-Replacement of connector block (housing).-Replacement of connector assembly (terminal and connector block).

OPERATION CYCLE

The following instructions are given below:

-Instructions for dismantling connector blocks; the dismantling procedures and the special tools to be used are given for each family of connectors;-Instructions for replacing terminal(s);the standard procedure, valid for all connectors, for cutting and replacing the terminal(s) is given.

All the tools mentioned in the removal instructions described below, with the exception of two (n° 2000018617 and n° 2000018618), are housed in a single case, part number 2000018600.

Instructions for dismantling connector blocks

Family 91317/42 - Tool 2000018618

Dismantling

On this type of connection, the terminal is not replaced, only the connector block; if the cause of the problem is the terminal, then the engine prewiring assembly must be replaced.

-Working on the retaining clips (1 - Fig. 1) release the cable guide (2) and remove it.

Fig. 1



    -Fit tool 2000018618 (1 - Fig. 2), release the terminal retaining tabs and extract it from the connector block (3).

    Fig. 2



      Reassembly

      -Fit the terminal in the connector block checking that the retaining tabs are secured.-Fit the cable guide removed previously.

      Family 91332/48 - Tool 2000018615

      Dismantling

      -If fitted, work on the retaining clips (1 - Fig. 3), then release the cable guide (2) and remove it.

      Fig. 3



        -In the case of a one-way connector, work on the point (1 - Fig. 4) and remove the terminal lock (2).-In the case of a multi-way connector, work on both sides of the point (3 - Fig. 4) and remove the terminal lock (4).

        Fig. 4



          -Using tool 2000018615 (1 - Fig. 5), raise the retaining clip (2) for the connector block (3) and extract the terminal involved.

          Fig. 5



            For the operations of cutting and replacing the terminal(s), refer to the relevant procedure; for replacing the connector block, proceed with extracting the remaining terminals.

            Reassembly

            -Fit the terminal in the connector block checking that the retaining clips are secured.-Fit the terminal lock and the cable guide removed previously.

            Family 91337/42 - Tool 2000018604

            Dismantling

            On this type of connection, the terminal is not replaced, only the connector block; if the cause of the problem is the terminal, then the engine prewiring assembly must be replaced.

            The ways for this connector may not all be used; the free ones should be closed using the caps in the kit.

            -Remove the protective tape. Work on the retaining clips (1 - Fig. 6), then release the terminal lock (2) and fold it over.

            Fig. 6



              -Using tool 2000018604 (1 - Fig. 7), release the terminal retaining tabs and extract them from the connector block (2).-Make a note of the ways to be closed later on in the new connector block using the replacement caps (3).

              Fig. 7



                Reassembly

                -Close the ways not used with the replacement caps.-Fit the terminals in the connector block checking that the retaining tabs are secured.-Fit the terminal lock and renew the protective tape removed previously.

                                            Instructions for replacing a terminal

                                            These illustrations only refer to replacign an individual terminal for the connector block illustrated, but are valid for all families of connectors; as far as extracting the terminal from the connector block is concerned, refer to the connector family being worked on.

                                            If several terminals are being replaced, make offset joins between them in order to restrict the size of the cable loom and thereby facilitate the subsequent refitting in the outer casing.

                                            Take great care not to get the cables to be joined dirty as this will adversely affect the continuity of the electrical conduction.

                                            The drier used in this procedure is available commercially whilst the heat shield can be ordered using part number 2000029200.

                                            -Release the cable for the terminal to be replaced sufficiently to work and cut at the midway point as illustrated in Fig. 22.

                                            Fig. 22



                                              -Measure the cable that has just been cut (1 - Fig. 23), then cut the replacement cable (2) 15 mm longer than the cable to be replaced.

                                              Fig. 23



                                                -Expose the new replacement cable (1 - Fig. 24) and the engine prewiring cable (2) for a maximum length of 10 mm.

                                                To avoid cutting the strands and losing part of the cable''s conduction powers, it is advisable to expose the cables using the cable strippers (3 - Fig. 24) homologated by the Parts Dept. part number 2000027100.

                                                Fig. 24



                                                  -Fit the thermal restricting coupling tube on the prewiring cable (1 - Fig. 25).-Starting as illustrated in the figure (2 - Fig. 25) twist the strands (3), then move the coupling tube (1) until the coupling is covered with the seal (4) by the twisted strands (3).

                                                  Fig. 25



                                                    A. Thermal restricting coupling tube

                                                    B. Seal

                                                    C. Seal

                                                    D. Twisted strands

                                                    -Using the drier (1 - Fig. 26) fitted with the heat shield 2000029200 (2) heat the thermal restricting tube (3) until the seal (4) melts as illustrated in figure (5). Wait for the seal to cool down, then refit the wiring.

                                                    Fig. 26



                                                      During the operation of heating the thermal restricting tube, as a result of the high temperatures reached, the drier and the heat shield must not, under any circumstances, come into contact with any part of the engine compartment.
