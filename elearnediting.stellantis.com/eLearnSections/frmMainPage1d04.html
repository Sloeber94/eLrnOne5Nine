Removing ( Refitting )

   Op. 7050B18 REAR SEAT ARMREST - R+R

1. Working from both sides of the arm-rest seat, unscrew bolts (1a) and remove armrest pin rotation plates (1b).

1. Using clippers, cut all the retainers (1a) securing the perimeter of the cushion cover (1b) to the metal frame (1c). Also release the ends (1d) and (1e).

2. Release the cover (2a) from the upholstery (2b) and from the metal frame (1c) along the entire perimeter of the cushion.

3. Remove the metal frame.

1. Fold the cushion over and, using clippers, cut all the backrest trim front retainers.

2. Separate the backrest trim (2a) from the padding (2b).
Refitting ( Removing )

- Check that the backrest trim is not damaged.

- Position the backrest trim on the front part of the padding and crimp the retainers using special pliers.

Mould the cover on the upholstery during the various refitting stages.

- Position the metal frame on the upholstery.

- Fit the trim on the padding along the entire perimeter of the backrest.

- Fasten one tensioning end to the metal frame, then pull the other end until the trim is taut; lastly, secure the other tensioning end to the metal frame.

- Using special pliers, attach the retainers fastening the perimeter of the cushion cover to the metal frame.

- Working from both sides of the armrest seat, position the pin rotation plates and tighten the bolts.
