1072 B 600 AA SWIRL VALVES ACTUATOR
Instructions for cleaning - Network Information


OPERATIONS IN NETWORK

In addition to the instructions in Service News 10.10.06 transmitted with CD 04/06 we wish to remind you that:

when fitting the swirl valves a special grease is applied to the bushes (arrows in Fig. 1) that guarantees lubrication and a seal against gas.

as a result, we wish to point out that:

-the removal of the grease, especially if it is done as a "precaution" may have a detrimental effect as far as the locking of the component is concerned.-a manifold with lubrication must not be cleaned using liquid products such a "Svitol".-cleaning carried out in this way could damage the efficiency of the modifications to the swirl valve management software introduced recently.

The removal of any foreign bodies or dirt from between the linkage (see arrows in Fig. 1) should be carried out without removing any of the mechanical components and only using an air jet.
