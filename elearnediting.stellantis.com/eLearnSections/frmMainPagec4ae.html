140 - Alfa 159   2.4 JTD 20V   LUMBAR ADJUSTMENT NOISE   S069   

Noise during operation
 

1

  

LEVER POSITION CHECK

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check that the adjustment lever and support disc are correctly positioned.

  

Move on to Step 2

  

Not correctly positioned (rubbing, interference)

  

Reposition them correctly (or replace the knob and/or disc) and lubricate with Krytox grease.
 

2

  

CHECK ON BACKREST ADJUSTMENT ASSEMBLY

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

After partly removing the backrest upholstery, check whether the noise (creaking) is coming from the adjustment control unit.

  

Move on to Step 3

  

The noise is coming from the regulator body.

  

Lubricate the bowden cable with Krytox grease (to confirm the diagnosis) and then apply grease.
 

3

  

CHECK ON POSITIONING/ATTACHMENT OF PULLMAFLEX PLATE

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check that the Pullmaflex plate and/or adjustment cables are correctly positioned/attached

  

Move on to Step 4

  

Positioning/attachment not correct.

  

Reposition the Pullmaflex plate and/or adjustment cables correctly.
 

4

  

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check that the fault is no longer present.

  

End of diagnosis

  

The fault persists

  

Replace the adjustment assembly
