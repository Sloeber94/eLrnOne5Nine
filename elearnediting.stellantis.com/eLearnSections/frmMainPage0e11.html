FUNCTIONAL DESCRIPTION

The electronic device inside the steering column switch unit H5 and the controls on the right lever receive a power supply at pin 11 of connector A from the ignition-controlled line (INT/A) of relay T12 protected by fuse F43, both housed in the junction unit under the dashboard B2.

Pin 7 of connector A and 9 of connector C of H5 are earthed.

According to the function selected, stalk unit H5 (connector A) sends the following control signals to the wiper motor N15:

- from pin 8 for fast operation;

- from pin 9 for slow and/or intermittent operation,

- pin 6, on the other hand, receives the earth signal coming from the motor end of travel cam which stops operation. The windscreen wiper motor N15 is then earthed.

The steering column switch unit H5 windscreen washer switch, once activated, sends two signals from pins 10 and 12 of connector A which operate the windscreen washer pump N22 which turns sending the detergent liquid to the windscreen wiper.

The rain sensor housed in the mirror P65 receives a supply from the INT line protected by fuse F49 (F51 from 04/07) of the junction unit B2 and sends the automatic operating signal to pin 2 of connector A of H5 via the A-BUS serial line of the Body Computer M1 pin 7 of connector D and pin 25 of connector B.

The Body Computer M1 is connected via the CAN line to the instrument panel E50 to manage the failure signal if there are problems with the rain sensor.

The screen washer fluid level sensor k27 sends an earth signal to the instrument panel E50 - pin 12 of connector D - which lights up the warning light directly.
