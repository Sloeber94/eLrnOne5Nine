Operation
 

 

Check that there are no faults with the anti-roll bar See Test

  4410CA Verifiche sulla barra stabilizzatrice

More specifically:

  • Anti-roll bar correctly positioned in relation to the centre-line of the vehicle

  • Paint not worn at the points where the bar and the track control arms come into contact

  • Condition of flexible mountings in the contact area with the bar

  • Fastenings to torque between bar and crossmember

  • Fastenings to torque between bar and shock absorber connecting rod.

Also check, after having disconnected the upper track rod end for the connecting rod at the shock absorber, that the anti-roll bar does not creak at the centre flexible mountings when moved manually with a symmetrical load applied and that the loose connecting rod does not move unevenly or stick.
