140 - Alfa 159   2.4 JTD 20V   HANDBRAKE APPLIED WARNING LIGHT   K072

N.B.: The command to switch on the handbrake warning light is sent by the switch on the lever to the Body Computer Node which makes it available on the B-CAN to the Instrument Panel Node.
