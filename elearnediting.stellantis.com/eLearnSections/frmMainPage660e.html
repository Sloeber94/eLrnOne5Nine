140 - Alfa 159   2.4 JTD 20V   FRONT SEAT BACKREST ANGLE ADJUSTMENT NOT WORKING   O002   

The backrest electrical adjustment is not working / not working properly.
 

1

  

CHECK ON SWITCH STATUS

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Select the following control unit at the diagnostic equipment:

Driver''s/passenger seat control unit.

Check the value of the following parameter:

Backrest switch and check that it indicates released when the switch is released and indicates pressed when the switch is pressed.

  

Move on to Step 5

  

There is no change in the status of the switch.

  

Move on to Step 2
 

2

  

CHECK ON WIRING CONNECTIONS AT BACKREST SWITCH

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check the connection for the wiring at the electric seat control switch.

Also check for any oxidation/impurities at the connector/switch.

  

Move on to Step 3

  

1.Switch wiring connector detached or badly positioned on seat mounting.

  

1.Fit connector correctly on switch.
 

2. Presence of oxidation/impurities at connector/control unit.

  

2.Eliminate oxidation/impurities at connector/switch.
 

3

  

CHECK ON WIRING CONNECTIONS AT CONTROL UNIT

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check the connection for the wiring at the electric seat control unit.

Also check for any oxidation/impurities at the connector/control unit.

  

Move on to Step 4

  

1.Switch wiring connector detached or badly positioned on seat control unit.

  

1.Fit connector correctly on control unit
 

2. Presence of oxidation/impurities at connector/control unit

  

2.Eliminate oxidation/impurities at connector/control unit.
 

4

  

BACKREST SWITCH OPERATION CHECK

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check that the backrest switch is working properly

  

Move on to Step 9

  

Switch not working correctly.

  

Replace the switch

Op. 7045J50

 

5

  

CHECKS USING DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Keeping the diagnostic equipment connected, check:

that the backrest actuator is working properly through active diagnosis.

  

Move on to Step 9

  

Incorrect operation of backrest actuator.

  

Move on to Step 6
 

6

  

CHECK ON CONNECTION AT MOTOR

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check that the connection is correctly attached to the motor

Also check for any oxidation/impurities at the connector/motor.

  

Move on to Step 7

  

1.Connectors at motor detached or badly positioned.

  

1.Fit connector correctly on motor
 

2.Presence of oxidation/impurities at connector/motor.

  

2.Eliminate oxidation/impurities at connector/motor.
 

7

  

CHECK ON ELECTRICAL SEAT WIRING

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check the condition of the electric seat wiring

  

Move on to Step 8

  

Presence of cuts/tears to seat wiring.

  

Replace cut/broken wiring see wiring diagram

 See E3076 ELECTRICALLY-ADJUSTABLE SEATS
 

8

  

CHECK ON CORRECT ENGAGEMENT OF PROPELLER SHAFTS

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check that the backrest movement motor is working properly.

  

Move on to Step 9

  

Backrest adjustment motor not working properly

  

Replace the backrest adjustment motor

 Op. 7045J34 FRONT SEAT BACKREST ANGLE MOTOR, LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R. FOLLOWING ELECTRICAL WIRING CHECK
 

9

  

DRIVER''S/PASSENGER SEAT CONTROL UNIT

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Replace the driver''s/passenger seat control unit

 Op. 7045J36 SEAT MOVEMENT ECU R R FOLLOWING ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK

  

End of diagnosis

  

The fault persists

  

Contact technical service for further information on the problem

Hardness during the adjustment of the backrest
 

0

  

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Operate the backrest adjustment knob without resting your back against the backrest and check whether the hardness persists.

  

End of diagnosis

  

1. Hardness disappears

  

1. Explain to the customer that the adjustment should be carried out without resting their back on the backrest.
 

2. Hardness persists

  

2. Move on to Step 1
 

1

  

CHECKS ON KNOB ON COVER

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check for interference with the cover (knob on cover).

  

Move on to Step 2

  

Presence of interference at the knob on the cover.

  

Remove the interference and check that the regulator is working properly.
 

2

  

CHECKS ON THE SEAT

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Remove the seat from the vehicle

 Op. 7045A10 FRONT SEAT (ONE), LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R.

remove the side cauterization (left and right), release the joints and check for the presence of paint lumps and/or machining residues

  

Move on to Step 3

  

Presence of paint lumps and/or machining residues.

  

Eliminate the foreign bodies, lubricate and run in with at least five complete operating cycles.
 

3

  

CHECK ON THE CORRECT PHASING OF THE BACKREST ADJUSTERS

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check that the phasing of the backrest adjusters is correct by moving the backrest.

  

Move on to Step 4

  

The backrest moves with a sinusoidal movement

  

Partly release the joint connecting rod (removing the washer fitted on the rod) and rephase the adjusters.
 

4

  

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check that the fault is no longer present.

  

End of diagnosis

  

The fault persists

  

Replace the seat frame

 Op. 7045A86 FRONT SEAT FRAME (ONE), LEFT OR RIGHT, R.R.
