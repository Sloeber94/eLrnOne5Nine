Removing ( Refitting )

- Position the vehicle on a lift.

   Op. 1048A10 AIR FILTER ASSEMBLY - R.R.

- Undo the upper bolts fixing the front bumper and move it as necessary to gain access to the upper bolt fixing the cold air intake for the air filter.

1. Undo the bolt fixing the air filter cold air intake to the upper radiator crossmember.

1. Undo the lower bolt fixing the air filter cold air intake to the bodyshell.

1. Remove the cold air intake for the air filter.
Refitting ( Removing )

- Place the cold air intake for the air filter back in its housing and secure it using the bolts.

- Tighten the upper front bumper retaining bolts.

   Op. 1048A10 AIR FILTER ASSEMBLY - R.R.

- Remove the vehicle from the lift.
