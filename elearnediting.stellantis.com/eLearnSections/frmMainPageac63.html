The climate control is working properly and sends air according to the preset flows but this air does not guarantee the required heating.

CHECK ON CORRECT CLIMATE CONTROL SYSTEM OPERATION

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

With the engine on and the compressor off, in manual mode with the temperature knob at the maximum hot HI position.

Also set the recirculation button to the closed position and set the fan speed to 6 bars (display) and the distribution to front ventilation.

Use the diagnostic tester to check that the engine temperature reaches at least 70 °C.

Check that the air temperature at the front distribution outlet is greater than 60 °C.

  

End of diagnosis

  

The temperature of the air coming out of the vents is not as desired.

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION OF MIXTURE ACTUATOR

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check whether the electrical wiring connections are loose.

Disconnect and visually inspect for damage at the connector contacts and reconnect the connection to the motor mounting.

If it persists, before replacing the actuator, check its operation by manually activating the function that the actuator should carry out.

With the engine running, switch on the fan at maximum speed.

Place the flaps in the various distribution positions via the control/control unit (manually).

Check the air flow in the passenger compartment with your hand at the vents.

  

End of diagnosis

  

1. Electrical connections loose or damaged.

  

1. Renew the electrical connections

 See E6020 AIR CONDITIONING

 

2. There is no air flow intake into the passenger compartment through the vents.

  

2. Move on to Step 7.
