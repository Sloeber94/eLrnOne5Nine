140 - Alfa 159   2.4 JTD 20V   GEAR ENGAGEMENT OR RELEASE DIFFICULT   C002   

Go back to CLUTCH NOT RELEASING CORRECTLY

It is sometimes difficult to engage the gears; there is also a (grating) noise coming from the area of the gearbox
 

0

  

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check that the clutch is correctly released: with the engine running press the clutch pedal and engage a gear

  

Move on to Step 1

  

The gearbox always grates with any of the gears: the clutch does not disengage

  

Proceed as instructed in the

 See table E001 CLUTCH NOT RELEASING CORRECTLY
 

1

  

GEAR SELECTION CHECK

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check the condition of the gear selector ball springs (see Overhauling gearboxes,

Assembly 2110B

)

 Op. 2110B MANUAL GEARBOX WITH DIFFERENTIAL GEARS AND SYNCHRONISERS

  

Move on to Step 2

  

Springs or balls damaged

  

Replace the affected parts.
 

2

  

SYNCHRONIZER RING CHECK

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Overhaul the gearbox (see Overhauling Gearboxes,

Assembly 2110B

)

 Op. 2110B MANUAL GEARBOX WITH DIFFERENTIAL GEARS AND SYNCHRONISERS

Check the synchronizer rings in particular: there should be no wear on the inner surfaces.

  

Move on to Step 3

  

Seals worn

  

Replace the synchronizers.
 

3

  

GEAR CHECK

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Overhaul the gearbox (see Overhauling Gearboxes,

Assembly 2110B

)

 Op. 2110B MANUAL GEARBOX WITH DIFFERENTIAL GEARS AND SYNCHRONISERS

Check the gear front teeth in particular: they should not show signs of notches or excessive wear

  

Move on to Step 4

  

Gears worn

  

Replace the gears involved.
 

4

  

CHECK ON HUBS AND SLIDING SLEEVES

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Overhaul the gearbox (see Overhauling Gearboxes,

Assembly 2110B

)

 Op. 2110B MANUAL GEARBOX WITH DIFFERENTIAL GEARS AND SYNCHRONISERS

Check the hubs and the sliding sleeves in particular:

- They should not show signs of notches, wear or excessive clearance;

- They should slide freely

  

End of diagnosis

  

Sleeves or hubs worn

  

Replace the sleeves or hubs involved.
