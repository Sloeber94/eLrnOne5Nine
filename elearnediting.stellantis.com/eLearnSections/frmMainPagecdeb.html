Removing ( Refitting )

The pre-modification solution is described below, the post-modification solution involves a single particle filter pressure intake pipe connected to the differential pressure sensor.

If the particle filter differential pressure sensor is being replaced, when the refitting is complete the following operations must be carried out using the Examiner.

   Op. 1016A10 SOUND-PROOFING COVER - R.R

1. Disconnect the electrical connections from the glow plug control unit.

Refitting ( Removing )

- Connect the pipes to the particle filter differential pressure sensor, then place it back in its housing and tighten the nut.

- Connect the electrical connection to the particle filter differential pressure sensor.

- Connect the electrical connections to the heater plugs control unit.
