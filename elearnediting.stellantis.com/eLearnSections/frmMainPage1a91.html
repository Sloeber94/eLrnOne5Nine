The list of pre-delivery inspection (PDI) operations specific for the applicable model and related to the assigned time (shown below) is given here below.

TIME ALLOWANCE: 1.50 (hundredths of an hour)

Preparation for the delivery of a new alfa 159 vehicle

 

DEALERSHIP IDENTIFICATION DATA
 

Corporate name
 

Sincom code
 

VEHICLE IDENTIFICATION DATA
 

Model
 

Version
 

Chassis No.
 

CONFORMITY
 

❑ On-board documentation conformity
 

❑ Accessory check
 

ENGINE COMPARTMENT
 

Check and, if necessary, restore:
 

❑ Battery charge status (refer to Service News 55.08.09).
 

❑ Hydraulic brake-clutch fluid level
 

❑ Engine oil level
 

❑ Selespeed fluid level
 

❑ Screen washer fluid level
 

❑ Fitting of aesthetic guards (where prescribed)
 

VEHICLE INTERIOR

 

Carry out:

 

❑ Fitting fuses or deactivating Logistic Mode
 

❑ Cigar lighter operation check
 

❑ Anti-theft device/remote control operation check
 

Start up the engine and check the operation of:
 

❑ Horns, front and rear window washer/wiper, hazard warning lights
 

Switch off the engine and check:
 

❑ Handbrake lever travel
 

❑ Seat belt mountings and sliding
 

❑ Locks and child-locks on rear doors
 

❑ Adjust clock, date, language and unit of measurement
 

DYNAMIC TEST
 

Before the start of the test:
 

❑ Check tyre inflation pressure (including spare wheel)
 

❑ Check warning lights come on at key-on
 

❑ Start the engine
 

❑ Check warning lights go out when the engine has started
 

During the test, check:
 

❑ Instrument panel and gauges
 

❑ Radio - CD player - navigator (where fitted)
 

❑ Heater and/or climate control
 

❑ Cruise Control (where fitted)
 

During the test, check:
 

❑ Engine smooth operation
 

❑ Braking system proper operation
 

❑ Gear - clutch engagement
 

❑ Automatic transmission (where fitted)
 

❑ No drift
 

To be carried out after the test:
 

❑ Warm starting
 

❑ Reset the trip meter
 

CHECKS AFTER DYNAMIC TEST
 

Raise the vehicle and check:
 

❑ Condition of underbody
 

❑ No leaks from lubrication system
 

❑ No leaks from engine cooling system
 

❑ No leaks from the hydraulic braking/clutch system and power steering
 

❑ No leaks from fuel system
 

❑ No leaks from window washer system
 

❑ Condition of differential driveshaft boots
 

❑ Condition of brake hoses and pipes
 

Lower the vehicle and carry out:
 

❑ Fit wheel hub caps (where available)
 

FINAL CHECK
 

❑ Check seats
 

❑ Remove moving/transportation protections
 

❑ Remove labels from windscreen
 

❑ Wash vehicle and check for water penetration
 

❑ Dry vehicle
 

❑ Clean passenger compartment, windows and rear view mirrors
 

❑ Visually inspect vehicle exterior
 

❑ Fit radio aerial
 

❑ Fit mats
 

❑ Check interior and exterior trims
 

❑ Apply “Inspected” labels
 

NOTE: the special cards containing instructions and warnings for the customer should not, under any circumstances, be removed.
 

NOTE: to protect the life of the battery, every time the vehicle is moved or the panel is switched on, extract the electronic key afterwards and place it in the centre console glove compartment.
