5505 A 140 AA ADDITIONAL JUNCTION UNIT IN THE BOOT
Removing-refitting procedure - Service Manual update


OPERATIONS IN NETWORK

In advance of the Service Manual update we are enclosing the procedure for removing-refitting the additional junction unit in the boot (Op. 5505A14) through the introduction of more information on extracting the control unit terminals.

OPERATION CYCLE

Removing

-Disconnect the negative battery terminal.-Remove the luggage compartment trim and the load compartment trim as described in the relevant procedures (Op. 7040B20 and Op. 7040B10).-Remove the luggage compartment seal and the luggage compartment moulding as described in the relevant procedures (Op. 7025B66 and Op. 7040B22).-Remove the left side trim as described in the relevant procedure (Op. 7040B18).-Undo the fixing nuts (1 - Fig. 1), place the control unit (2) in a comfortable position to release the wiring from any retainers and disconnect the electrical connection (3).

Fig. 1



    all the tools mentioned in the removal instructions described below, with the exception of one (n° 2000018617), are housed in a single case, part number 2000018600.

    -Release the secondary lock (detail B) with the help of extractor 2000018608, working at the point shown by the arrow (detail A - Fig. 2) and remove it (detail C).-Carry out the same operations on the other three remaining secondary locks.

    keep the secondary locks because if the control unit is replaced they are not supplied by the Parts Dept.

    Fig. 2



      -Mark the reciprocal position, then extract the fuses (1 - Fig. 3) and the relays (2).

      the number of the fuses, relays and supply leads may vary depending on the systems/optional equipment on the vehicle.

      Fig. 3



        -Mark the reciprocal position of all the terminals on the control unit.

        the control unit (2) cover (1 - Fig. 4) also acts as a secondary lock.

        -Carefully raise the control unit (2) cover (1 - Fig. 4) so that there is a gap around the entire perimeter as shown by the arrow.

        Fig. 4



          -Extract the fuse terminals (1) using the extractor 2000018617 (A – Fig. 5).-Extract terminals (2) using extractor 2000018604 (B – Fig. 5) and extract terminals (3) for the relays using extractor 2000018614 (C).

          Fig. 5



            Refitting

            -Check that the junction unit (1 - Fig. 6) is not damaged; if it is, replace it with a new one supplied by the Parts Dept.

            before continuing, make sure that the cover (2 - fig. 6) with the secondary lock function is raised as illustrated by the arrow. The terminals cannot be fitted with the cover lowered.

            Fig. 6



              -Fit the terminals, each in their proper place, following the markings made during the removal and check that they are correctly fitted by pulling each cable gently.-Press the cover with the secondary lock function (1 - Fig. 7).

              Fig. 7



                -Fit the secondary locks (1 - Fig. 8) in their seats and press them to make sure they are correctly attached.

                Fig. 8



                  -Fit the fuses and relays in the junction unit following the previous markings.-Place the control unit (1 - Fig. 9) in position, tighten the fixing nuts (2) to a torque of 0.7 - 1.1 daNm and connect the electrical connection (3).

                  Fig. 9



                    -Fit the left side trim as described in the relevant procedure (Op. 7040B18).-Remove the luggage compartment moulding and the luggage compartment seal as described in the relevant procedures (Op. 7040B22 and Op. 7025B66).-Fit the load compartment trim and the luggage compartment trim as described in the relevant procedures (Op. 7040B10 and Op. 7040B20).-Connect the negative battery terminal.
