New instructions in case of interventions covered by the warranty - Network Information


This Service News cancels and replaces all previous communications about Bosch car radio replacement in the event of fault.

OPERATIONS IN NETWORK

This procedure applies only to interventions covered by the warranty

From this Service News onwards, if the Bosch / Blaupunkt car radio is faulty, only for the countries listed in table 1, the following new procedure should be complied with:

-Make sure that the car radio is really faulty.-Download and print the attached file (Check_List_Bosch_EN.zip).
-Note down on the Bosch Check List, in the item “Car radio data” the Fiat product code.-If the correct Fiat product code cannot be identified, remove the car radio and note down on the Check List the following codes (1, 2, 3, 4 -- Fig. 1) on the data plate (A), on the cover and on the data plate (B), on the side of the car radio.

1) Serial number (last seven numbers);

2) Car radio model;

3) Bosch code;

4) FIAT Group code.

Fig. 1 - Codes on car radio data plates



    -Inform the Customer that the replacement car radio shall be delivered within a few days and he shall be contacted again to book the replacement operation.-Fill in the Check List (please write clearly).-Only if this is the first time that this new process for ordering a Radio from Bosch is carried out, download, print and fill in (please write clearly) the attached file (Request_for_coding_at_Bosch_EN.zip).

    - Send the following documentation to the Bosch Service Centre, by fax or e-mail, to the numbers and addresses shown in table 1:

    -Copy of the Certificate of Warranty-Copy of the Check_List_Bosch form.-Copy of the Bosch_Electronic_Service form (only if this procedure is being carried out for the first time).

    Information sent by e-mail from UPS shall be available for 10 days from the sending date. Please download the content of the e-mail within this deadline.

      - attaching the label (Fig. 2) received by e-mail to the packaging.

      - arranging a transport document for the return of the faulty car radio, with recipient's reference as shown in Table 1.

      For Italy, Bosch shall deliver the required replacement car radio through GLS (rather than UPS) forwarding agent.

      After the replacement, the faulty car radio must be returned to Bosch within two days from the closing of the Customer's sheet, as follows:

      -preferably using the same packaging as the replacement car radio.-attaching a copy of the Check_List_Bosch form sent by fax.-arranging a transport document for the return of the faulty car radio, with recipient's reference as shown in Table 1.-Contacting the GLS forwarding agent at the phone number 02-36962484.

      - In case of service covered by the warranty , all shipping expenses for new/faulty car radios shall be borne by Bosch.

      - In the event of fault due to misuse by the Customer, Bosch shall send an invoice to the Dealership/Workshop to charge the repair operation, with a description of the works carried out attached.
