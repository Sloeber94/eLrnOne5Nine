Removing ( Refitting )

1. Place the vents in the air flow closed off position.

2. Undo the bolts.

3. Remove the vent assembly (3a) releasing the flexible retainers (3b).
Refitting ( Removing )

- Check that the vent assembly is not damaged.

- Position the vent assembly and attach the retainers.

- Tighten the bolts.

- Check that the vents are working.
