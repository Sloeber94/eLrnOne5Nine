Removing ( Refitting )

The 4x2 version is described below

- Position the vehicle on a lift.

    Op. 7055B54 PROTECTION/GUARD UNDER ENGINE -R.R.

1. Undo the nuts (1a), (1b) and disconnect the intermediate exhaust pipe (1b) from the catalytic converter.

- Remove the gasket and move the intermediate exhaust pipe aside.

- Position a hydraulic jack underneath the gearbox.

1. Undo the bolts fixing the power unit lower reaction rod, engine crankcase sump side.

1. Undo the bolts fixing the power unit rear support flexible mounting, rigid support side.

1. Undo the bolts (1a) and remove the rigid power unit rear support (1b).
Refitting ( Removing )

- Place the power unit rigid rear support in its housing and secure it by tightening the bolts to the recommended torque.

Removing ( Refitting )

The 4x4 version is described below

- Position the vehicle on a lift.

    Op. 7055B54 PROTECTION/GUARD UNDER ENGINE -R.R.

1. Undo nuts (1a), screws (1b) and disconnect intermediate exhaust pipe (1c) from the catalytic converter.

- Remove the intermediate exhaust pipe gasket.

1. Undo bolts (1a) and remove heat guard (1b).

2. Mark the position of the two flanges connecting the rear drive intermediate gear casing to the propeller shaft.

3. Undo the bolts securing the two flanges connecting the rear drive intermediate gear casing to the propeller shaft.

- Position a hydraulic jack underneath the gearbox.

1. Undo the bolts securing the power unit lower reaction rod, engine crankcase sump side.

1. Undo the bolts securing the power unit rear mount flexible mounting block, rigid mount side.

1. Undo bolts (1a) and remove rigid power unit rear mount (1b).
Refitting ( Removing )

- Place the power unit rigid rear support in its housing and secure it by tightening the bolts to the recommended torque.
