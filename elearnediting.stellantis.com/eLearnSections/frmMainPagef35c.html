1088 1 271 EB WATER PUMP FLUID INLET PIPE
Engine coolant leaks - Service Solution


FAULT COMPLAINED OF

Engine coolant leaks/loss from the screw-in plug (A - Fig.1) on the coolant inlet pipe to the water pump.

TECHNICAL CAUSE

Screw-in plug damage (plastic).

OPERATIONS IN PRODUCTION

Adoption of the new pipe for fluid inlet to the water pump, with metal plug.

OPERATIONS IN NETWORK

Network behaviour standards towards the Customer

-Once the Customer's claimed fault is confirmed, reassure the customer that the fault will be solved by replacing the water pump inlet pipe.-After the intervention, check the work done and make sure that the customer is satisfied.

Service Solution

If the claimed fault is confirmed, replace the fluid inlet pipe to the water pump according to the Operating Cycle below.

OPERATION CYCLE

Replace the fluid inlet pipe to the water pump (B - Fig. 1), operating as described in Op. 1088D24 of the Service Manual.
