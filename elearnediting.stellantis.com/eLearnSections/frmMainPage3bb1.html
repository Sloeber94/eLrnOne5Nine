Removing ( Refitting )

The 4x2 version is described below

- Position the vehicle on a lift.

    Op. 7055B80 UNDERBODY SIDE PROTECTION (ONE) LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R.

    Op. 7050A20 ONE-PIECE REAR SEAT CUSHION - R.R.

1. Undo bolts (1a) and remove access cover (1b) for the submerged pump assembly complete with level gauge control.

1. Disconnect the quick coupling of the rear fuel supply pipe, submerged pump assembly side complete with level gauge control.

1. Position a hydraulic jack equipped with the appropriate tool for supporting the fuel tank.

2. Undo bolts (2a) fixing fuel tank support belts (2b).

- Lower the fuel tank as far as necessary to gain access to the clip securing the rear pipe delivering fuel to the body.

1. Disconnect the quick coupling for the rear fuel supply pipe, front fuel supply pipe side.

2. Remove the rear fuel delivery pipe (2a) after releasing it from the fastenings (2b).
Refitting ( Removing )

- Refit the rear fuel delivery pipe to its seat and secure.

- Connect the rear fuel supply pipe quick coupling, front fuel supply pipe side.

- Secure the rear fuel delivery pipe with the fastening collars located under the fuel tank.

- Place the fuel tank in position and secure it by tightening the bolts to the recommended torque.
