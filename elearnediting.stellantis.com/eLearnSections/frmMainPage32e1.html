140 - Alfa 159   2.4 JTD 20V   DEMANDING USE OF THE CAR      

Should the prevailing use of the car be under one of the following specially heavy conditions:

- to tow a trailer or caravan;

- on dusty roads;

- short, repeated journeys (less than 7-8 km) at sub-zero outside temperatures;

- engine often idling or driving long distances at low speeds (e.g. taxi or door-to-door deliveries) or long periods of idleness;

- urban routes.

The folowing checks must be carried out more often than indicated in the Planned Maintenance Programme:

- check front disc brake pad condition and wear;

- check cleanliness of bonnet and boot locks, and cleanliness and lubrication of linkages;

- visually inspect condition of:engine, gearbox, transmission, rigid and flexible pipes (exhaust - fuel system - braking system) and rubber elements (boots - hoses - bushes - etc.);

- check battery charge and battery fluid level (electrolyte);

- visually inspect condition of the auxiliary drive belts;

- check and, if necessary, change engine oil and replace oil filter;

- check and, if necessary, replace pollen filter;

- check and, if necessary, replace air filter.
