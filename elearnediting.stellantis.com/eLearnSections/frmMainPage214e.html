FUNCTIONAL DESCRIPTION

Control unit M66 is supplied at pin 11 of connector D - by a line protected by fuse F36 of the junction unit under the dashboard B2 .

The controls on the driver''s door H44 include the joystick and control switch for the exterior rear view mirrors:

- selection of the right/left mirror: signal to pin 30 of connector A of M66,

- vertical movement: signal to pin 28 of connector A of M66,

- horizontal movement: signal to pin 29 of connector A of M66.

On the basis of the commands received, the control unit M66 controls the motor for the mirrors P60 from pin 4 of connector E (joint) and for pins 3 and 5 (horizontal and vertical movement respectively).

Similarly, the same commands are sent to the passenger side mirror P61 from pins 10, 26 and 27 of connector B of M66.
