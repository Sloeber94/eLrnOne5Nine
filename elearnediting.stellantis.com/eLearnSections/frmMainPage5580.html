Removing ( Refitting )

Family 91337/50

The ways for this family of connectors may not all be used; the free ones should be closed using the caps in the kit.

1. If fitted, work on the retaining clips (1a), then release the cable guide (1b) and remove it.



1. Working at the point indicated (1a), carefully slide the terminal lock as far as the stop.



1. Fit the tool (1a), release the terminal retaining tabs and extract it from the connector block.

- To replace the terminal proceed as follows

These illustrations only refer to replacing an individual terminal for the connector block illustrated, but are valid for all families of connectors. If several terminals are being replaced, make offset joins between them in order to restrict the size of the cable loom and thereby facilitate the subsequent refitting in the outer casing.

1. Release the cable for the terminal to be replaced sufficiently to work and cut at the midway point as illustrated in the figure.



1. Measure the cable that has just been cut (1a), then cut the replacement cable (1b) 15 mm longer than the cable to be replaced.



1. Expose the new replacement cable (1a) and the engine prewiring cable (2) for a maximum length of 10 mm.

To avoid cutting the strands and losing part of the cable conduction powers, it is advisable to expose the cables carefully.



1. Fit the thermal restricting coupling tube on the prewiring cable (1a). Starting as illustrated in the figure (1b) twist the strands (1c), then move the coupling tube (1a) until the coupling is covered with the tin ring (1d) by the twisted strands (1c).

1. Using the drier (1a) fitted with the heat shield (1b) heat the thermal shrinking tube (1c) until the tin ring (1d) melts as illustrated in figure (1e).

Wait for the seal to cool down, then refit the wiring.

Take great care not to get the cables to be joined dirty as this will adversely affect the continuity of the electrical conduction. During the heating of the thermal shrinking tube, as a result of the high temperatures reached, the drier and the heat shield must not, under any circumstances, come into contact with any part of the engine compartment.

The drier used in this procedure is available commercially available.
Refitting ( Removing )

- Close any ways not used with the replacement caps.

- Fit the terminal in the connector block checking that the retaining tabs are secured.

- Fit the terminal lock and fit the cable guide removed previously.
