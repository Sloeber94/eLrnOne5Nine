Lubrication of the main shaft - Network information - Service Manual update


OPERATIONS IN NETWORK

On the vehicles listed below, if the clutch is removed-refitted, always lubricate the main shaft on the gearbox as described in the Operating Cycle that follows.

failure to lubricate the main shaft may cause slight sticking (especially when cold) during shifts from neutral to first and neutral to reverse or drag at low revs with the clutch pedal pressed.

OPERATION CYCLE

-Remove the clutch assembly (Op. 1810A10) as described in the relevant procedure in the Service Manual.-Clean both the clutch plate hub (1 - Fig. 1) and the gearbox main shaft (2) thoroughly, firstly with a clean cloth and then using abrasive paper on the splining.-Apply a thin film (0.25 grams) of silicon grease (e.g. PG 21 No. 71736440 or the equivalent) on the gearbox main shaft only and check the sliding of the clutch plate on the actual shaft; remove any excess grease on both sides of the clutch plate hub.

the splined profile should be lubricated with a thin film of grease that is a light as possible; during operation, the gease should not end up on any of the nearby components (especially the clutch plate).
