Removing ( Refitting )

- Open the boot lid.

1. Open the light access flap (1a) releasing the retainer (1b).

2. Release the retainers (2a) and extract the bulb holder (2b).

3. Remove the bulb involved, extracting it from the bulb holder (bayonet fitting).
Refitting ( Removing )

- Check that the bulb is working.

- Fit the bulb in the bulb holder.

- Place the bulb holder back in position and attach the retainers.

- Close the light access cover and attach the retainer.

- Close the boot lid.

- Check that the inner rear light cluster lights are working.

APPLICABLE TO VERSIONS WITH: Station Wagon


 Removing ( Refitting )

- Open the tailgate.

1. Remove the rear light cluster access cover (1a) releasing the flexible retainer (1b) and then the rigid retainers (1c).

APPLICABLE TO VERSIONS WITH: Station Wagon

1. Release the retainers (1a) and extract the bulb holder (1b).

2. Remove the bulb involved, extracting it from the bulb holder (bayonet fitting).

APPLICABLE TO VERSIONS WITH: Station Wagon

Refitting ( Removing )

- Check that the bulb is working.

- Fit the bulb in the bulb holder.

- Place the bulb holder back in position and attach the retainers.

- Place the light cluster access cover in position and attach the rigid retainers and then the flexible retainer.

- Close the tailgate.

- Check that the inner rear light cluster lights are working.
