FUNCTIONAL DESCRIPTION

Current socket/cigar lighter P50 and the one at the back on the tunnel P51 receive a power supply from the ignition-controlled line (INT/A) of relay T12 and are protected by fuses F44 and F52 respectively, both housed in the junction unit under the dashboard B2.

On the STATION WAGON version, the power supply for the rear current socket P52 arrives via the ignition-controlled line (INT/A) of relay T12 protected by fuse F45 both located in the junction unit under the dashboard B2.
