140 - Alfa 159   2.4 JTD 20V   WINDSCREEN MISTING WITH THE FORMATION OF CONDENSATION   L057   (Automatic climate control)

APPLICABLE TO VERSIONS WITH: Automatic climate control

On vehicles equipped with a windscreen demisting sensor, it does not work and the windscreen does not demist with the air conditioning on when there is condensation.

APPLICABLE TO VERSIONS WITH: Automatic climate control

 

1

  

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION OF SENSOR

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check that the demisting sensor lens in the windscreen is not obstructed by foreign bodies or that it is not dirty or marked and that the windscreen is not dirty.

  

Move on to Step 2

  

1.Demisting sensor lens obscured by foreign bodies.

  

1. Remove the foreign bodies from the sensor.
 

2. Demisting sensor lens dirty.

  

2. Clean the sensor lens.
 

3. Demisting sensor lens scratched.

  

3. Replace the demisting sensor

Op. 5010D36 DEMISTING SENSOR R.R. FOLLOWING ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK
 

4. Windscreen dirty.

  

2. Clean the windscreen.
 

2

  

CHECK THAT THE PROXY HAS BEEN CARRIED OUT FOR THE SYSTEM

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Connect the Examiner diagnostic equipment and check that the Proxy has been carried out for the system to recognize the sensor.

  

Move on to Step 3

  

Proxy not carried out for the system to recognize the sensor.

  

Carry out the Proxy for the system.
 

3

  

CHECK USING DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Connect the Examiner diagnostic equipment and check that there are no errors at the windscreen demisting sensor.

If the result is negative, carry out the

 See Test 5040DB Check windscreen demister sensor operation

  

Move on to Step 4

  

1.Error in the demisting sensor: 0% or 100% according to the

 See Test 5040DB Check windscreen demister sensor operation

  

1. Move on to Step 5
 

2. Error in the demisting sensor: 90% or 80% according to the

 See Test 5040DB Check windscreen demister sensor operation

  

2. Move on to Step 6
 

3. Error in the demisting sensor: 10% according to the

 See Test 5040DB Check windscreen demister sensor operation

  

3. Replace the demisting sensor

Op. 5010D36

 

4

  

CHECK USING DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Keeping the Examiner diagnostic equipment connected, check:

  • that there are no errors at the Climate Control Node;

  • that there are no errors at the timing actuator.

  

Move on to Step 8

  

1. Error at climate control node.

  

1. Move on to Step 5.
 

2. Short circuit to earth error at the distribution actuator.

  

2. Check that the Lin Bus cables are not caught between the metal parts (pay special attention to the gearbox bolts) and move on to Step 8.
 

3. Short circuit to +12V error at the distribution actuator.

  

3. Check that the Lin Bus cables are not caught between the metal parts (pay special attention to the gearbox bolts) and move on to Step 8.
 

4. Mechanical interference/stuck error.

  

4. Move on to Step 10.
 

5

  

CHECKS USING DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Keeping the Examiner diagnostic equipment connected, check the correct operation of the air mixture actuator through active diagnosis

  

Move on to Step 6.

  

Air mixture actuator not working

  

Move on to Step 8.
 

6

  

CHECK ON DEMISTING SENSOR ELECTRICAL CONNECTIONS

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check whether the windscreen demisting sensor electrical wiring connections are loose or damaged, disconnect/reconnect the wiring. Check the continuity/insulation of the sensor electrical wiring.

  

Move on to Step 7

  

1. Electrical connections loose or damaged.

  

1. Restore correct wiring.
 

2. Sensor wiring broken or short circuited.

  

2. Restore correct wiring.
 

7

  

CHECK ON THE CORRECT INSTALLATION OF THE DEMISTING SENSOR

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check that the demisting sensor is correctly positioned in its housing.

  

Move on to Step 8

  

Demisting sensor not correctly positioned.

  

Refit the sensor in the correct position.
 

8

  

CHECK ELECTRICAL CONNECTIONS

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check whether the electrical wiring connections between the Climate Control Node and the distribution actuator are loose/damaged.

Disconnect/reconnect the connection to the Climate Control Node and to the actuator and to the demisting sensor.

Check the continuity/insulation of the electrical connection between the Climate Control Node and the distribution actuator and the sensor.

  

Move on to Step 9

  

1.Electrical connections loose/damaged

  

1. Restore the correct wiring.
 

2.Electrical connection broken or not insulated.

  

2. Restore the continuity/insulation of the electrical connection.
 

9

  

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION OF DISTRIBUTION ACTUATOR

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check the operation activating the function that the actuator should carry out manually.

With the vehicle moving, switch on the passenger compartment fan at the maximum speed (6 bars on the display), place the flaps in the max. def. position using the control/control unit (manually) and check the air flow in the passenger compartment by placing your hand over the vents.

  

End of diagnosis

  

Flow of air to the windscreen insufficient.

  

Replace the distribution actuator

Op. 5040D56

 

10

  

DISTRIBUTION ACTUATOR FLAP CHECK

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check that the actuator flap completes its travel without any impediment.

  

End of diagnosis

  

1. Flap travel incomplete.

  

1. Free the air mixture actuator flap from the impediment.
 

2. Flap damaged.

  

2. Replace the flap.
 

3. Kinematic mechanism / components damaged.

  

3. Repair kinematic mechanism.
