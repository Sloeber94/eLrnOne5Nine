FLUID LEAKS CHECK

  

All OK

  

Problems encountered

  

Operation
 

 

Raise the vehicle on a two column lift and check that there are no oil leaks from the shock absorber stem

The check on the shock absorber should only be carried out after driving on a dry road, slight dampness of the stem is considered normal since this is due to evaporation that takes place during the operation of the shock absorber.

If there is persistently oil on the shock absorber stem, make sure that it is not coming from other parts of the vehicle that are lubricated

  

Move on to Step 2

  

Leaks from other parts that are lubricated

  

Search for the cause of the leak following the appropriate diagnostic procedure
 

Leaks from the shock absorber stem valve guide

INTERFERENCE CHECK

  

All OK

  

Problems encountered

  

Operation
 

 

Check that there are no signs of interference between the suspension elements or between the latter and the bodyshell.

Also check that there are no signs of interference between the lower track control arm and the shock absorber fork due to damage/partial removal of the bush.

In particular, check that there are no signs of wear on the suspension springs and that they do not show signs of abrasion.

If the spring shows signs of abrasion, its mechanical properties could be weakened as a result of oxidation.

  

Move on to Step 10

  

Inteference due to incorrect fitting.

  

Eliminate the interference and restore the correct fastening. If this is not possible, replace the defective components.
 

Abrasions or non conformity of the spring.

  

Replace the spring

 Op. 4410A12 FRONT SUSPENSION DAMPER (ONE), LH OR RH - FOR MCPHERSON STRUT SYSTEM - INCLUDES DISMANTLING/REASSEMBLY OF ASSEMBLY AT BENCH - R.R

taking care to fit it correctly
 

Signs of interference between the lower track control arm and the shock absorber fork

  

Replace the lower track control arm.

TIGHTENING TORQUE CHECK

  

All OK

  

Problems encountered

  

Operation
 

 

Locate the area of the possible noise source: top or bottom.

Starting from the area where the knocking was identified, check

 See Test 4410OA CHECKING CORRECT TIGHTENING OF FRONT SUSPENSION

the tightening torques of the non-yield point type fixing bolts:

- the shock absorber stem fastening to the mounting;

- the fastening of shock absorber mounting to the bodyshell;

- the fastening of the shock absorber to the fork;

- the fastening of lower/upper end of the connecting rod for shock absorber fork anti-roll bar;

- the fastening of the shock absorber fork to the lower track control arm;

- the fastening of the track control arm to the steering knuckle;

- the fastening between steering box rod and the steering knuckle;

- the front fastening of the U-bolt securing the anti-roll bar to the engine frame;

- the fastening of the vertical bracket to the engine frame and the bodyshell;

- the fastening of the upper track control arm to the steering knuckle;

- the fastening of brake caliper to the steering knuckle;

- the fastening of the brake disc shield;

Visually inspect that the yield point type bolts have not loosened, the list is given below:

- the fastening of the steering box to the engine frame;

- the rear fastening of the U-bolt securing the anti-roll bar to the engine frame;

- the front/rear bushes securing the lower track control arm to the engine frame;

- the fastening of the upper track control arm to the bodyshell;

- the front/centre/rear fastening of the engine frame to the bodyshell;

- the fastening of the engine side engine connecting rod to the chassis;

- the fastening of the gearbox side engine connecting rod to the chassis.

  

Move on to Step 12

  

Non-shear bolts not tightened to torque.

  

Restore the correct tightening.
 

Yield point type bolts loosened.

  

Replace the yield point type bolts and restore the correct tightening.
