140 - Alfa 159   2.4 JTD 20V   REFRIGERANT CIRCUIT CHECK   5040AB   

Go back to CLIMATE CONTROL NOT WORKING (UNDER ANY CIRCUMSTANCES)

Do not check the air conditioning system in the immediate vicinity of the walls (distance of approximately 2 m) in order to ensure sufficient circulation of the air by the condenser.
 

 

CHECK TO BE PERFORMED

  

RESOLUTION IF THE CHECK IS NOT OK
 

1

  

Connect the air conditioning system repressurizing station.

Start up the engine and keep it idling.

Set the required passenger compartment temperature to COLD (turn anticlockwise to stop).

Set the fan to 4th speed.

Switch on the air conditioning (LED on snowflake symbol on).

Take the reading from the high pressure gauge and ensure it is greater than 13 bars

  

Move on to Step 4
 

2

  

Check the cooling fan is on.

Move on to Step 4

  

Move on to Step 3
 

3

  

Turn the ignition key to the ON position.

With the engine off, perform active diagnosis on the cooling fan and ensure it is successful

  

Restore the electrical connections between the fan and engine control unit

   See E5020 ENGINE COOLING

If necessary, replace the fan

   Op. 1088E10 RADIATOR COOLING FAN - R + R

Before draining the air conditioning system, check the coolant circuit to ensure there are no leaks

Op. 5040A14 AIR CONDITIONING REFRIGERANT CIRCUIT - CHECK FOR LEAKS AND ELIMINATE IF NECESSARY BY TIGHTENING CONNECTORS

Check the connection to the expansion valve in particular.
 

 

CHECK TO BE PERFORMED

  

RESOLUTION IF THE CHECK IS NOT OK
 

4

  

Turn the ignition key OFF.

Drain, empty and charge the air conditioning system with the specified amount of coolant.

Op. 5040A10 AIR CONDITIONING REFRIGERANT CIRCUIT - DRYING AND REPRESSURIZING

Run the engine for 15 minutes with the bonnet lid closed.

Switch on the air conditioning (LED on snowflake symbol on).

Set the required passenger compartment temperature to COLD (turn anticlockwise to stop).

Adjust the air distribution to passenger head ventilation.

Make sure that the recirculation is switched off.

Increase the engine rpm up to 1500 rpm for 5 minutes.

Take the reading from the high pressure gauge and ensure it is within the range between 9 and 36 bars

  

Move on to Step 6
 

5

  

Take the reading from the low pressure gauge and ensure it is outside the range between 1.7 and 2.2 bars

The test can be considered over

  

Move on to Step 6
 

6

  

Take the readings from the high and low pressure gauges and ensure the pressure difference is at least 5 bars.

  

Replace the air conditioning compressor

   Op. 5040A20 AIR CONDITIONING COMPRESSOR - R.R. INCLUDES CHECKING FLUID LEVEL AND TOPPING UP IF NECESSARY
 

7

  

Check that the coolant circuit is not blocked with ice or dirty

  

Clean the coolant circuit.

If necessary, replace the damaged components

   Op. 5040A AIR CONDITIONING REFRIGERANT CIRCUIT
 

8

  

Replace the expansion valve

   Op. 5040B30 EXPANSION VALVE (ORIFICE TUBE) - R+R
