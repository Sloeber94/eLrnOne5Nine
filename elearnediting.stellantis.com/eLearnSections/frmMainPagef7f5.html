140 - Alfa 159   2.4 JTD 20V   IGNITION PUSH BUTTON CHECK   5520GA   
 

 

CHECK TO BE PERFORMED

  

RESOLUTION IF THE CHECK IS NOT OK
 

1

  

Insert the key in the key recognition device and ensure that the surround lights up. Continue to Step 3

  

Proceed as instructed

   See Test 5505AF CHECK ON EFFICIENCY OF KEY RECOGNITION DEVICE
 

2

  

Check and if necessary restore the +15 power supply to Pin 1 of the ignition button

   See H002 STARTING PUSH BUTTON

Move on to Step 6.

  

Replace the ignition button

   Op. 5520G30 START/STOP SWITCH FOR EASY-GO SYSTEM STARTER MOTOR - R.R.
 

3

  

With the diagnostic instrument in Voltmeter mode, connect the positive probe to ignition Pin 3 of the ignition device and the negative to earth.

   See H003 IGNITION DEVICE

Check that there is a 12 Volt voltage present.

  

Move on to Step 5
 

4

  

Check and if necessary restore the continuity of the wiring between Pin B2 of the power steering control unit and Pin 3 of the button

   See M089 STEERING LOCK CONTROL UNIT (NBS)

   See H002 STARTING PUSH BUTTON

The test can be considered over

  

Proceed as instructed

   See Test 5580ED ELECTRIC STEERING LOCK CHECK
 

5

  

Disconnect the button connector.

   See H002 STARTING PUSH BUTTON

Connect the diagnostic tester in Ohmmeter mode across Pin 2 and Earth, checking that the resistance reading is less than 1 Ohm.

  

Reset the earth connection of the right facia area

   See E1020 EARTHS

 

6

  

With the diagnostic instrument in Voltmeter mode, connect the positive probe to button Pin 4 and the negative to earth

   See H002 STARTING PUSH BUTTON

Press the ignition button and check the voltage is 12 Volts.

  

Replace the ignition button

   Op. 5520G30 START/STOP SWITCH FOR EASY-GO SYSTEM STARTER MOTOR - R.R.
 

7

  

With the diagnostic instrument in Voltmeter mode, connect the positive probe to device Pin 5 and the negative to earth

   See H003 IGNITION DEVICE

Check for a voltage of 12 Volts. Continue to Step 9.

  

Move on to Step 8.
 

8

  

Check and if necessary restore the continuity of the wiring between Pin A8 of the control unit and Pin 5 of the button

   See M089 STEERING LOCK CONTROL UNIT (NBS)

   See H002 STARTING PUSH BUTTON

The test can be considered over

  

Proceed as instructed

   See Test 5580ED ELECTRIC STEERING LOCK CHECK
 

9

  

With the diagnostic instrument in Voltmeter mode, connect the positive probe to button Pin 6 and the negative to earth

   See H002 STARTING PUSH BUTTON

Press the ignition button and check the voltage is 12 Volts.

  

Replace the ignition button

   Op. 5520G30 START/STOP SWITCH FOR EASY-GO SYSTEM STARTER MOTOR - R.R.
 

10

  

Insert the key into the reader device, with the diagnostic instrument in Voltmeter mode, connect the positive probe to button Pin 7 and the negative to earth

   See H002 STARTING PUSH BUTTON

Check for a PWM signal.

The test can be considered over

  

Move on to Step 11
 

11

  

Check and if necessary restore the continuity of the wiring between Pin C2 of the control unit and Pin 7 of the button

   See M089 STEERING LOCK CONTROL UNIT (NBS)

   See H002 STARTING PUSH BUTTON

  

Proceed as instructed

   See Test 5580ED ELECTRIC STEERING LOCK CHECK
