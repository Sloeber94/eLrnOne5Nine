Removing ( Refitting )

- Raise the seat until it is in the end of travel position.

1. Undo the plastic nut.

2. Remove guide guard (2a) by releasing retainer (2b) and pushing the guard forward.
Refitting ( Removing )

- Check that the guide guard is not damaged.

- Fit the guide guard and attach the retainers.

- Tighten the plastic nut.

- Place the seat in a middle position.

APPLICABLE TO VERSIONS WITH: Electric seats
