5580 0 000 AA RADIO SPEAKERS
Irregular reproduction - Technical Information


With reference to cases where speakers have been replaced on account of:

-not working-irregular operation-interference-vibrations-distorted/intermittent sound

which, following subsequent checks, have been found to be working properly, we wish to point out several checks that should be carried out before the speakers are replaced.

After having made sure that the radio in the vehicle is working properly and the speaker or the tweeter is properly connected to the connector, follow the instructions below:

-make sure that the protection (grille) for the speaker or the tweeter does not contain any foreign bodies that might interfere with normal operation;-make sure that the bolts fixing the speaker or the tweeter are properly tightened;-make sure that the attachment for the speaker or the tweeter on the grille is stable and that they cannot move in the housing;-make sure that the speaker or the tweeter is properly connected to the connector;-make sure that there are no objects (foam, plastic film, wires, bands, adhesive tape, etc.) in contact with the speaker or the tweeter on either the outside (the side which is visible) or the inside (the side which is not visible);-make sure that there are no components in the the areas in close proximity to the speakers that are not properly secured and could vibrate with the actual speakers;

then remove the speaker or the tweeter that may be causing the problem from the vehicle, reconnect it out of its housing and check whether the fault that could be heard previously with the speaker installed is still present (thereby excluding any fitting irregularities).

As far as the above is concerned, the speaker or the tweeter should only be replaced if the problem is confirmed following the instructions in the Service Manuals.
