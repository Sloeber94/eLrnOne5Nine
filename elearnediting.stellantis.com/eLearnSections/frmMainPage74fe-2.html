- Undo the reservoir cap and draw off the power steering fluid using a suitable syringe.

1. Disconnect the fast on fitting of the return pipe from the power steering box to the heat exchanger, heat exchanger pipe side.

2. Unscrew the nut securing the front bracket of the return pipe from the power steering box to the heat exchanger pipe, to the air intake sleeve to the intake manifold.

APPLICABLE TO VERSIONS WITH: C.A.E.

1. Unscrew the nut securing the rear bracket of the return pipe from the power steering box to the heat exchanger pipe, to the gearbox.

APPLICABLE TO VERSIONS WITH: C.A.E.

1. Unscrew the nut securing the front bracket of the return pipe from the power steering box to the heat exchanger pipe, to the gearbox.

- Open the automatic transmission fluid heat exchanger coolant pipe retaining clip.

APPLICABLE TO VERSIONS WITH: C.A.E.

1. Undo the connector (1a) and disconnect the return pipe from the power steering box to the heat exchanger (1b), power assisted steering box side.

APPLICABLE TO VERSIONS WITH: C.A.E.

1. Unscrew bolts (1a) securing the air inlet sleeve to intake manifold (1b), intake manifold side, and move aside.

2. Release the return pipe from power steering box to heat exchanger pipe and remove.

APPLICABLE TO VERSIONS WITH: C.A.E.

Refitting ( Removing )

- Refit the return pipe from the power steering box to the heat exchanger pipe.

- Refit the air intake sleeve to the intake manifold in its seat and secure to the intake manifold with the bolts.

- Connect the return pipe from the power assisted steering box to the heat exchanger pipe, power assisted steering box side and tighten the connector to the recommended torque.
