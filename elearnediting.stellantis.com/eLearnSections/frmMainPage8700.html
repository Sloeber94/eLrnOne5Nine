Diagnostic procedures


Cancels and replaces Service News 10.05.08 of 31/03/2008 for the additional control of air pipe collars.

FAULT COMPLAINED OF

The Customer complains of one or more of the following problems:

A - Whistling during acceleration

B - Hissing during deceleration

C - Loss of engine performance with oil seepage from exhaust pipe.

OPERATIONS IN NETWORK

Depending on the Customer''s claimed fault, carry out the corresponding diagnosis procedure as described below.

A - Diagnostic procedure for the analysis of the causes of the turbocharger noise: whistling during acceleration

 

Step

  

Operations to be carried out

  

Test results
 

1

  

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

  

All OK

  

Problems encountered

  

Operation
 

 

Check the following, in order:

the condition of the manifolds and pipes;

the presence of restricted and/or bent ducts

bent intercooler fins;

correct tightening of air intake circuit pipe collars, especially the air inlet pipe to the turbine

  

Go to Step 2

  

Manifolds and pipes are not in the correct condition

B - Diagnosis procedure for the analysis of the causes of the turbocharger noise: hissing during deceleration

 

Step

  

Operations to be carried out

  

Test results
 

1

  

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

  

All OK

  

Problems encountered

  

Operation
 

 

Loosen the connecting fasteners between the catalytic converter and the exhaust pipe to remove any tension between the components.

Tighten the fasteners again and check the noise with the vehicle stationary:

accelerate with no load to a speed of 3000 rpm;

take your foot off the accelerator and let the engine return to the idling speed.

  

End of diagnosis

  

The hissing during deceleration persists.

  

Replace the turbocharger

(see remarks at the end of SN)

TURBOCHARGING PRESSURE CHECK

  

All OK

  

Problems encountered

  

Operation
 

 

FIXED GEOMETRY TURBINE

Check that the EGR valve and flow meter are working properly (see Service News 10.22.06 / 10.14.04). With stationary vehicle, measure by Examiner the absolute supercharging pressure: at 2500 rpm and with warm engine the value should range between 1050 and 1080 mbar.

VARIABLE GEOMETRY TURBINE

Check the system vacuum tightness:

with tool no. 2000015500 – Vacuum pump measure the vacuum value with the engine in pulling and release conditions and idling; the value measured should range 0.6 and 0.8 bar

with the VGT actuator in vacuum conditions, the value should be close to 0 bar.

  

Go to Step 6

  

Found non-compliant turbocharging pressure

  

Replace the turbocharger

(see remarks at the end of SN)

CHECK OIL FLOW THROUGH THE CATALYTIC CONVERTER/EXHAUST PIPE

(see remarks at the end of SN)

If the turbocharger is being replaced, carry out the following operations:
-Inspect the intercooler/intake circuit and clean thoroughly.-Pre-lubricate the new turbocharger bearings using Selenia WR engine oil through the opening securing the oil supply filler.-On 1.3 JTD engines with fixed geometry turbine, replace the turbocharger air supply hose.-For all engines, except the 1.9 JTD 8v, replace the fillers (1 and/or 4 - Fig. 1) and the sealing gaskets (2 and /or 5)-For 1.9 JTD 8v engines, replace the oil supply hose (3 - Fig. 1), the fillers (1 and /or 4 - Fig. 1) and the sealing gaskets (2 and/or 5).
