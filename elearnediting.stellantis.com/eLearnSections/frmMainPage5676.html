Removing ( Refitting )

On this version it is not possible to remove/refit the intake manifold on the car.

The power unit must be removed-refitted to perform this operation

   Op. 1004B10 POWER UNIT WITH MANUAL GEARBOX - R.R.

or

   Op. 1004B11 POWER UNIT WITH MECHANIC TRANSMISSION - R.R. FOR FOUR-WHEEL DRIVE MODELS

or

   Op. 1004B14 POWER UNIT WITH AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION R + R

and then proceed as described below keeping the power unit attached to a suitable hydraulic lift.

    Op. 1008A46 TIMING SIDE POWER UNIT RIGID SUPPORT - R.R.

    Op. 1032B16 TOOTHED TIMING BELT - R.R. WITH ENGINE REMOVED

    Op. 1060E40 PRESSURE PUMP SUPPORT - R.R.

Refitting ( Removing )

- Fit the support complete with actuator in its housing and tighten the bolts.

- Connect the swirl valves control rod to the actuator and to the control lever.

- Apply suitable sealant to the thread and tighten the degassing pipe connector.

- Place the degassing pipe in its housing, secure it using the bolts and tighten the band connecting it to the connector.

- Place the throttle body complete with a new gasket back in its housing and tighten the bolts to the recommended torque.
