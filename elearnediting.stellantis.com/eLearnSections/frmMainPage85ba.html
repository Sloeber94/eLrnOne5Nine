STEERING COLUMN CHECK

  

All OK

  

Problems encountered

  

Operation
 

 

Check the condition of the steering column support and that it is working properly. In particular, check:

- that there is no clearance between the support bushes and steering column

- that the steering column joints are correctly tightened

- the condition of the steering column supports and that they are working properly

  

Move on to Step 4

  

Bushes worn or damaged

  

Restore the efficiency of the steering column. If necessary, replace the bearings involved

 Op. 4110A30 SUPPORT WITH STEERING CONTROL SHAFTS - R+R
 

Joint spider clearance too large

  

Tighten the bolts or replace the steering column

 Op. 4110A30 SUPPORT WITH STEERING CONTROL SHAFTS - R+R
 

Bearings worn or damaged

  

Restore the efficiency of the steering column. If necessary, replace the bearings involved

Problems encountered

  

Operation
 

 

Check the condition of the bushes and mountings. In the following order:

- bushes for upper and lower track control arms

 Op. 4410B40 LOWER TRACK CONTROL ARM (WITH BALLJOINT) FOR LEFT OR RIGHT FRONT SUSPENSION (ONE) - R+R - CHECK WHEEL GEOMETRY SEPARATELY
 

Anti-roll bar mountings

  

Replace the flexible mountings for anti-roll bar

 Op. 4410C12 FRONT SUSPENSION ANTI ROLL BAR JOINT (END) (ONE), LH OR RH - R.R
