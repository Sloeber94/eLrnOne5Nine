Removing ( Refitting )

   Op. 7050A20 ONE-PIECE REAR SEAT CUSHION - R.R.

   Op. 7050A15 SPLIT REAR SEAT RIGHT BACKREST - R.R.

1. Operate lever (1a) to release left backrest (1b), then fold forward to the end of its travel.

1. Slightly raise the end (1a) of the backrest and disengage its side retainer (1b) from seat (1c).

2. Withdraw central pin (2a) from bracket (2b) and recover the backrest.
Refitting ( Removing )

- Check that the backrest is not damaged.

- Bring the backrest up to its position by holding horizontally and then engaging the central pin in the bracket.

- Insert the backrest side retainer in its seat.

- Lift up the backrest to the end of its travel and and attach it to the retainer.

- Check that the fold-down backrest is working properly.
