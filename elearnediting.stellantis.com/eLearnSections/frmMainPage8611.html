FUNCTIONAL DESCRIPTION

Bluetooth control unit M162 is supplied directly at pin 16 of connector A from the battery via the line for fuse F39 of under-dashboard control unit B2.

Bluetooth control unit M162 receives an ignition-controlled power supply at pin 32 via the line for fuse F51 of under-dashboard control unit B2.

Pin 15 of M162 is earthed.

The CAN line through which the Bluetooth control unit M162 is connected to the radio P20, the controls on the steering wheel - via the clock spring D47 - the Body Computer M1 and the other system nodes, reaches pins 14 and 30 of connector A.

The Bluetooth control unit M162 is connected to the radio P20 - connector C - from pins 5, 22 and 21 with the AUX left, AUX right and AUX earth signals.

The USB port P143 is connected to the Bluetooth control unit M162, to pins 3, 4, 19, 20 and 31 respectively, with signals: signal D+, reference earth, signal D-, 5V power supply and shielding.

The hands-free microphone located in the courtesy light G10 - connector D - is connected to pins 8 and 24 of connector A of M162.

The navigation controls, located next to the front courtesy light G10 - connector G - receive an ignition-controlled supply at pin 1 via the line for fuse F49 of the junction unit under the dashboard B2; pin 2 is earthed.

The reference earth reaches pin 3 from control unit M162 - pin 11, whilst the analogue signal for the buttons pressed leaves pin 4 for pin 28 of M162.
