Removing ( Refitting )

- Position the vehicle on a lift.

1. Undo the bolts securing the bracket attaching the rear differential to the crossmember.

2. Undo the front bolt securing the rear differential to the bracket.

3. Remove the bracket attaching the rear differential to the crossmember.
Refitting ( Removing )

- Place the bracket attaching the differential to the crossmember in position.

- Tighten the front bolt securing the rear differential to the bracket to the recommended torque.
