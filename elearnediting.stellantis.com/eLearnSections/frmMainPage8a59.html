140 - Alfa 159   2.4 JTD 20V   SUN ROOF NOISY / WITH HINGED OPENING SUN ROOF   S077   

Go back to SUN ROOF NOISY / WITH ROOF CLOSED

While driving with the roof open in the swivel position, noise can be heard from the sunroof area
 

0

  

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check whether the noise only occurs with the roof open to the swivel position.

  

Move on to Step 1

  

1. The noise is heard with the roof open

  

1. Proceed as instructed in the diagnosis

 See table S075 SUN ROOF NOISY / ROOF OPEN
 

2. The noise is heard with the roof closed

  

2. Proceed as instructed in the diagnosis

 See table S076 SUN ROOF NOISY / WITH ROOF CLOSED
 

1

  

SUN ROOF FASTENING CHECK

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check the correct fastening:

  • of the transparent roof panel to the slides;

  • of the full roof mechanism to the body

  

Move on to Step 2

  

Faulty fastenings.

  

Restore the fastening of the entire mechanism and roof

 Op. 7030B12 SLIDING ROOF (ASSEMBLY) - R+R
 

2

  

CHECK SUN ROOF CONTROL DEVICES

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check the operation of the sun-roof operating devices:

  • with the sunroof fully open, ensure the felt pads are present beneath the front spoiler;

  • ensure the teflon mount is present between slides and rails with the sunroof fully open;

  • with the sunroof open in the swivel position check the clearance between the central gutter and slides;

  • with the sunroof open in the swivel position, check the condition and correct fastening of the central gutter sliding pads.

  

End of diagnosis

  

1. Faulty fastenings.

  

1. Restore all the components and the fastening of the entire mechanism

 Op. 7030B SLIDING ROOF
 

2. Faults in the sunroof operating devices.

  

2. Replace the affected parts

 Op. 7030B SLIDING ROOF
