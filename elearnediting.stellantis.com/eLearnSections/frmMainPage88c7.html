Specific operations - Network Information


OPERATIONS IN NETWORK

On all Alfa 159 cars, restricted to the chassis numbers listed below, during the routine P.D.I. operations or before the car is handed over to the customer, the operations listed in the Operating Cycle below must be carried out.

OPERATION CYCLE

Operation 1 (Alfa 159 Saloon) - rear parcel shelf noise (from chassis 7200003 to chassis 7205988)

-Remove the rear parcel shelf as described in the appropriate procedure (Op. 7040H80) in the Service Manual.-Recut a suitable length of noise prevention adhesive tape (1 - Fig. 1) from the roll no. 71711971 available from the Parts Dept. and apply it to the crossmember under the rearscreen, under the slide (2), as illustrated.

Fig. 1



    Operation 2 (Alfa 159 Sportwagon) - rear seat backrest noise (from chassis 7200003 to chassis 7202237)

    -Fold the rear seat backrests over.-Fit the anti-abrasion adhesive seal no.46462523 (1 - Fig.2) on the attachment device (2) as illustrated in figure 2.-Then return the seat backrests to their normal position.

    Fig. 2



      Operation 3 (Alfa 159 Sportwagon) - noise from the oddments compartment flap in the load compartment blind side support (from chassis 7200003 to chassis 7202011)

      -Open the oddments compartment flap (1 - Fig. 3), cut a suitable length of noise prevention adhesive tape from the roll no. 71711971 available from the Parts Dept. and fit it to the side area (2).

      Fig. 3



        Operation 4 (Alfa 159 Sportwagon) - tailgate noise (from chassis 7200003 to chassis 7210994)

        -Open the tailgate and check the position of the anti-vibration mounting.-If the anti-vibration mounting (1 - Fig. 4) is fully tightened, loosen it until at least two threads (2) are showing.

        Fig. 4



          Operation 5 (Alfa 159 all versions) - bonnet noise (from chassis 7200003 to chassis 7206910)

          -Open the bonnet.-Cut a suitable length of noise prevention adhesive tape from the roll no. 71711971 available from the Parts Dept. then, working on both sides of the car, fit it to the fixed part of the bonnet hinges in the area shown (1 - Fig. 5).

          Fig. 5



            Operation 6 (Alfa 159 Sportwagon) - seat clip and rear seat backrest out of position (from chassis 7200003 to chassis 7207754)

            -Fold the rear seat backrests over to gain access to the clips.-Working on the mountings, remove the retaining clips (A - Fig. 6) on both sides of the seat and replace them with new clips part n° 50513702 supplied by the Parts Dept.-Then return the seat backrests to their normal position.

            Fig. 6



              Operation 7 (Alfa 159 all versions) - mirror trim and seal not correctly matched (from chassis 7200003 to chassis 7210994)

              -Check the matching of the rear view mirror trim (2 - Fig. 7) and the seals (3).-If they do not match, carry out the operations in the Service Manual with reference to Op 7005E12 using the special tool.-Loosen the rear view mirror fastenings (1 - Fig. 7);-Position the rear view mirror correctly so that once the nuts are tightened the edges of the window guide seal (3) overlap with the edges of the base of the rear view mirror (2), as illustrated in the diagram.

              Fig. 7



                Operation 8 (Alfa 159 all versions) - fuel flap, functional and aesthetic check (from chassis 7200003 to chassis 7210994)

                -Check that the gap between the fuel flap and the body is even and that it opens/closes without interference.-If this is not the case, position the vehicle on a lift and access the inner nuts securing the fuel filler flap with reference to Op 7025C10 in the Service Manual.-Open the fuel filler access flap then, working via the wheel arch, loosen the fixing nuts (1 - Fig. 8).

                Fig. 8



                  -Adjust the flap appropriately to ensure the gaps are the correct size using the figures given in Fig. 9.

                  Fig. 9



                    Operation 9 (Alfa 159 all versions) - windscreen trim connectors distortion (from chassis 7200003 to chassis 7210994)

                    -Check that the bonnet does not interfere with the windscreen trim connector when opening/closing.-If it does interfere, release the cover (1 - Fig. 10), undo the fixing bolt (2) and remove the connector (3).

                    Fig. 10



                      -Reposition the connector (1 - Fig. 11) taking care to inser the retainers (2) correctly in their seats in the body (3).

                      Fig. 11



                        Operation 10 (Alfa 159 saloon) - boot lid hinges chipping (from chassis 7200003 to chassis 7210994)

                        -Open the boot and check whether there is chipping in the areas where the hinges are fastened (figure 12) due to the adjustment of the actual boot lid after painting.-If there is chipping, repaint.

                        Fig. 12
