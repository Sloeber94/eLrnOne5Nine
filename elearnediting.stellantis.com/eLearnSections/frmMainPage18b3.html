OPERATIONS IN NETWORK

Some telephone service providers can provide, on request, two identical SIM cards with a single telephone number (known as twin cards). These cards have several different commercial names e.g.:

-TWIN CARD - for TIM-Servizio BIS - for VODAFONE-DUAL SIM - for WIND

and can be inserted into separate telephone sets (e.g. mobile phone and Connect).

The Connect does NOT support the 128K technology of Vodafone SIM cards.

The two SIM Cards are provided and remain, despite any modification carried out by the User, in a configuration whereby one card is Master and the other is a Slave. They have the following specifications:

-With both SIM cards active, the incoming call is received by the Master.-Whenever the Master SIM card is inactive (inserted into a terminal that is switched off or not inserted at all) and the Slave SIM card is active, the incoming call is received by the Slave.-SMS text messages and messages relating to Targa services can only be received by the Master SIM card. Whenever the Master SIM card is inactive, the messages are NOT forwarded to the Slave SIM card.

remind the User that in the absence of any distinctive external marking, the easiest way of identifying the Master card is as follows:

– Insert both twin SIM cards into active terminals.

– Send an SMS text message to the number on which the twin SIM cards are active, from another telephone.

– The card that receives the SMS text message is the Master and the other is the Slave.

Recommended operation modes on vehicles with connect

There are specific controls available from the telephone service provider for changing the status of the SIM card from Master to Slave. Alternatively, it is possible to physically swap the SIM cards over, according to the desired user profile. Typical operation modes in relation to user preferences are described below.

PROFILE 1 (Standard use):

The first and simplest operation mode consists of inserting the Master SIM card into the mobile phone and the Slave SIM card into the Connect. The User switches off the mobile phone upon entering the vehicle and any calls are automatically diverted to the Connect.

a) In order for calls to be diverted correctly from the Master to the Slave, call answering or call divert/transfer services must be deactivated on the Master SIM card (in this instance, inserted into a mobile phone).

b) SMS text messages are not forwarded, but are displayed on the mobile phone when it is switched back on.

PROFILE 2 (User mode for receiving SMS text messages in the vehicle):

The second operation mode involves inserting the Master SIM card into the Connect and the Slave SIM card into the mobile phone. During the trip, the Connect automatically receives all calls and SMS text messages.

With the Connect switched off or the telephone deactivated (after pressing the TEL OFF button) all voice calls are received on the mobile phone.

In order for calls to be diverted correctly from the Master to the Slave, call answering or call divert/transfer services must be deactivated on the Master SIM card (in this instance inserted into the Connect)

PROFILE 3 (Targa service use):

Profile 2 must be used in order to make correct use of the Targa Connect services. In addition, once the service has been activated by sending and receiving an SMS text message from the Master SIM card in the Connect, this SIM card must physically stay in the Connect.

This is necessary only because the enablement of the Targa Connect services is linked to the physical SIM card and not to the status of the SIM card (Master/Slave).

Considering that the options/services available for twin cards could be developed further, for any further details please refer to your telephone service provider.

When using the twin card service, each time the telephone module on the Connect is switched on (Key on, TEL on and on knob), the dialogue between the system and the network operator exchange must be completed before the telephone line becomes available.

There is normally a wait of around 10 seconds after the enablement of the Phone book button, available on the TELEPHONE screen, in other words when the Phone book writing goes from grey to black.
