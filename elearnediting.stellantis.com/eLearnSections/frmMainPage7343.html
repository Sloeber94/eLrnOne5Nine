140 - Alfa 159   2.4 JTD 20V   INTRODUCTION - VARIOUS ENGINE COMPONENTS DRIVE      

The belt (1), operated by the Poly-V type crankshaft pulley (2), controls the power steering pump (3) and the air conditioning compressor (4).

The air conditioning compressor has a double pulley where the alternator (6) drive belt (5) is fitted.

There is an automatic tensioner (7) for tensioning the belt (1) which means that no maintenance operations are required.

The belt (5) is tensioned when the air conditioning compressor is fitted and does not require further adjustments.
