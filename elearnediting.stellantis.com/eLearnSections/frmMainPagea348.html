Alarm self-activation - Service Solution


FAULT COMPLAINED OF

The anti-theft device activates without reason.

TECHNICAL CAUSE

Excessive sensitivity of anti-theft device volumetric sensors near the courtesy light.

OPERATIONS IN PRODUCTION

Fitting new anti-theft device volumetric sensors with modified activation calibration settings.

OPERATIONS IN NETWORK

Network behaviour standards towards the Customer

-When this problem is reported and confirmed, reassure the Customer that it can be solved by replacing the casing containing the anti-theft device volumetric sensors.-After the operation, show the Customer what has been done and make sure they are satisfied.

Service Solution

If the described fault is revealed, replace the casing containing the volumetric sensors, working according to the following Operation Cycle.

OPERATION CYCLE

Replace the casing (3a - Fig. 1) containing the anti-theft device volumetric sensors as described below:

Fig. 1



    Removing

    -Disconnect the negative battery terminal;-Move the front courtesy light assembly with volumetric sensors (1a - Fig. 1), releasing the retainers (1b);-Detach electrical connection (2);-Disconnect volumetric sensor casing (3a) from front courtesy light (3b), releasing retainers (3c) and attachments (3d).

    Refitting

    -Replace the volumetric sensor casing with a new one from the Spare Parts Dept.;-Connect the volumetric sensor casing to the front courtesy light, engaging the attachments and the retainers;-Connect the electrical connection;-Position the front courtesy light assembly and volumetric sensors and engage the retainers;-Connect the negative battery terminal;-Check that the parts that were removed are working properly.
