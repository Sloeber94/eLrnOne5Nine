140 - Alfa 159   2.4 JTD 20V   60,000KM SERVICE   0010K60   

Detail     Detail
Minimize the details

- Check that the tyre tread is not worn.

Some types of tyres are fitted with wear gauges; they should be replaced as soon as these gauges are visible on the tread.

Do not swap tyres across.

- Check the tyre inflation pressure is correct, spare wheel included.

The tyre pressures must be checked when they are cold.

- Check that the lighting system is operating correctly: headlamps, direction indicators, hazard warning lights, luggage compartment light, passenger compartment light, glove compartment light and instrument panel warnings lights.

- Check the windscreen washer/wiper system is operating correctly and adjust the nozzles if necessary.

- Check that the windscreen and rear window wiper blades are not worn and in the correct position.

- Check condition and wear of front disc brake pads and operation of pad wear indicator.

- Check rear disc brake pad condition and wear.

- Carry out a visual inspection of: body exterior, underbody protection, pipes and hoses (exhaust - fuel system - braking system), rubber elements (boots, sleeves, bushes, etc.).

- Check cleanliness of locks, bonnet and luggage compartment, cleanliness and lubrication of linkages.

- Check and, if necessary, top up the fluid levels of: power steering,

    Op. 0010T71 POWER STEERING FLUID - CHECK LEVEL AND TOP UP IF NECESSARY

windscreen washer, battery, engine cooling, etc.

- Visually inspect condition of accessory drive belt/s.

- Check emissions/fumes

    Op. 1080A20 EXHAUST FUMES - CHECK WITH OPACIMETER

- Replace the fuel filter.

    Op. 1044B10 FUEL FILTER ASSEMBLY - R.R. OR REPLACE

- Replace the air cleaner cartridge.

    Op. 1048A14 AIR FILTER FILTER ELEMENT - REPLACE

- Replace the engine oil and filter

    Op. 0010T12 ENGINE OIL AND FILTER - REPLACE

The actual interval for changing the oil and replacing the engine oil filter depends on the vehicle usage conditions and is signalled by the warning light or message (if present) in the instrument panel. Change the engine oil and replace the oil filter every 24 months.

- Replace the brake fluid.

    Op. 0010T62 BRAKE AND/OR CLUTCH FLUID - REPLACE FLUID INCLUDES WASHING PIPES AND BLEEDING AIR

Irrespective of the mileage it should be replaced every 24 months.

- Replace the pollen filter.

    Op. 5010B09 PURIFYING (POLLEN) FILTER FILTER ELEMENT - REPLACE

Irrespective of the mileage it should be replaced every 24 months.

- Connect the Examiner diagnosis equipment to the car diagnosis socket and carry out an engine check.

At the end of the service, connect the Examiner diagnosis equipment to the diagnosis socket in the vehicle to reset the mileage and days to the start value and write the service details (date, service number, total mileage) that can only be read using the diagnosis equipment in the Trip Computer memory.

Check that the measurement unit set in the instrument panel is the one used in that country - km or miles.
