Removing steering links / track control arms - Service Manual Update


OPERATIONS IN NETWORK

As expected, the front suspension on the new MY 08 versions of the Alfa 159 Saloon/Sportwagon (SN 00.01.08 - CD 02-2008) and the new MY 08 versions of the Alfa Brera/Spider (SN 00.03.08 - CD 02-2008) has a new aluminium steering knuckle with steel bushes in the matching area with the other suspension components.

In the case of servicing operations that involve disconnecting the steering links or the lower track control arms, proceed as described in the Operating Cycle below.

List of procedures involved:

-R/R Front steering knuckle (Op. 4450C10);-R/R Lower fork connecting front shock absorber to lower track control arm (Op. 4410A80);-S/R Steering links (Op. 4110B10);-S/R Front suspension lower track control arm (Op. 4410B40).

OPERATION CYCLE

This procedure illustrates the operations for disconnecting the lower track control arm from the steering knuckle; these operations are also valid for the steering link.

-Undo the fixing nut (1 - Fig. 1) using counter-force (2) on the articulated head (3) pin.

Fig. 1



    -Fit extractor 1840005001 (1 - Fig.1) fitted with brackets 99341009 (2) placing the adaptor (3) in kit 2000030600 between the bolt for the extractor and the pin to be disconnected.

In this case the bush can no longer be refitted in the steering knuckle but the actual steering knuckle has to be replaced.
