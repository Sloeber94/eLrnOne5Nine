Extracting/fitting steering knuckle bushes

  


1. Remove left front wheel strut (1a) complete with driveshaft (1b).

Collect the transmission fluid that comes out in a suitable container.

1. Undo the bolt securing the lower fork connecting the front suspension shock absorber to the lower track control arm, lower track control arm side.

Mark the position of the front suspension lower track control arm in relation to the front suspension frame.

1. Unscrew the front bolts (1a) and the rear bolts (1b) and remove the front suspension lower track control arm assembly (1c).

Refitting ( Removing )

1. Place front suspension lower track control arm (1a) in its housing and secure it by tightening the rear bolts (1b) and (1c) and the front bolts (1d) to the recommended torque.

Make sure they are in the position marked at the removal.
