5580 2 091 RD VOLUMETRIC SENSORS
Missed deactivation – Service Solution


FAULT COMPLAINED OF

The volumetric sensors of the button on the roof light cannot be deactivated

TECHNICAL CAUSE

The cables on the connector harness for the deactivation button were reversed.

OPERATIONS IN PRODUCTION

The correct position of the cables on the deactivation button connector was guaranteed.

OPERATIONS IN NETWORK

Network behaviour standards towards the Customer

-On cars with chassis number ranging between 7225839 and 7283909 only, if the customer's claimed fault is confirmed, reassure the Customer that it will be solved by positioning the cables on the volumetric sensor connector in the proper way.-After the operation, show the Customer what has been done and make sure he is satisfied.

Service Solution

Once the customer's claimed fault is confirmed, position the cables on the volumetric sensor connector cables in the proper way, according to the Operating Cycle below.

OPERATION CYCLE

Open the bonnet and disconnect the battery negative terminal then, working from inside the compartment:

-release the retainers and lower the roof light (1 - Fig. 1) to reach the connections, with reference to Op. 7040E40 of the Service Manual, using the suitable tool.

Avoid using leverage on the roof light lens cover during the removal, in order not to damage it.

Fig. 1



    -disconnect the electrical connection for the volumetric sensors (1 - Fig. 2) from the roof light.

    Fig. 2



      -pressing the tab (1 - Fig. 3), release and take out the cover of the small block (2).

      Fig. 3



        -use the tool no. 2000018605 (A - Fig. 4) to press in point (B) on the cable terminal retaining tab and take out the cable (2) partly; then press the retaining tab of the cable terminal in point (C) and take out the cable (2) from the block. Repeat the same operations and take out the cable (1) from the block.-revert the positions of the cables (1 and 2); the wrong position is that shown in detail D and the correct one is that shown in detail E.
