7035 A 240 AA BONNET LOCK
New safety lever - Service Literature Update - Network Information


OPERATIONS IN PRODUCTION

Starting from the chassis number listed below, a new bonnet lock (A - Fig. 1) has been fitted on the vehicles in question replacing the previous one (B - Fig.1).

The new lock differs from the previous one through the different shape of the safety device release lever (1).

Therefore the bonnet is opened by pressing the new lever upwards (it had to be pulled previously).

Replacing the lock also involves changing the grille in the area of the new lever.

OPERATIONS IN NETWORK

When an operation that involves replacing the lock is being carried out, the instructions below must be followed:

- the new lock (A - Fig. 1) is only used for vehicles with chassis numbers above 7108327.

- the old lock (B - Fig. 1) is only used for vehicles with chassis numbers below 7108326.

AVALABILITY OF PARTS

The modification of the lock is managed under code C/D11535.
