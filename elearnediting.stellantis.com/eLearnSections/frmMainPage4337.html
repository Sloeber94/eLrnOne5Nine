Removing ( Refitting )

- Open the tailgate.

   Op. 7040G36 BULB HOLDER FOR LOAD CARRYING COMPARTMENT lighting on tailgate - R.R.

1. Remove the covers releasing the retainers.

2. Remove the rear light cluster access covers (2a), releasing the flexible retainers (2b) and then the rigid retainers (2c).

1. Undo the bolts.

2. Remove the inner panel (2a) releasing the inner retainers (2b) using the special tool.

Refitting ( Removing )

- Check that the inner panel is not damaged.

- Position the panel and engage the inner retainers.

- Tighten the bolts.

- Place the access covers on the rear light clusters and attach the rigid retainers and then the flexible retainers.

- Position the covers attaching the retainers.

   Op. 7040G36 BULB HOLDER FOR LOAD CARRYING COMPARTMENT lighting on tailgate - R.R.

- Close the tailgate.
