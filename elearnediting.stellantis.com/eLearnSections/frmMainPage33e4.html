1060 G 000 AA WATER IN DIESEL PRESENCE/TEMPERATURE SENSOR
Operating check - Service Manual update


FAULT COMPLAINED OF

The fault diagnosis using the Examiner detects the presence of error P2264 (sensor malfunction)

TECHNICAL CAUSE

Decrease in battery voltage to below 10 V.

OPERATIONS IN NETWORK

If, after the fault diagnosis using the Examiner, the presence of error P2264 is detected, before deciding that the sensor is broken and replacing it, check the operation of the sensor as described in the Operating Cycle below.

OPERATION CYCLE

-Release the diesel filter (A - Fig. 1) from the mounting bracket and disconnect the water presence/temperature connector (B).-Check the operation of the diesel temperature sensor by connecting the digital multimeter (C - Fig. 1) used as a resistance meter; the resistance reading at pins 4 and 5 should be between 1.5 and 2.3 kOhm with the sensor at a temperature of 20°C.-Check the short circuit between the water presence sensor and the temperature sensor by connecting the digital multimeter (C - Fig. 1) used as a resistance meter; the resistance reading at pins 2 and 4 should be more than 1.0 MOhm. Repeat the operation at pins 3 and 4, the resistance reading should be more than 1.0 MOhm.
