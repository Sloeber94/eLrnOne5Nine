Removing ( Refitting )

1. Place the rear differential in a vice fitted with protective jaws and undo bolts (1a) and (1b) for cover (1c).

2. Cut the sealant using the tool and remove cover (1c) complete with spacer.

- Remove the residues of sealant from the matching surfaces of the casing, cover and spacer.
