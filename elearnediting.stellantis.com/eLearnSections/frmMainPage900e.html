Removing ( Refitting )

- Position the vehicle on a lift.

   Op. 4450B06 LEFT REAR WHEEL - R.R

- Or, alternatively.

   Op. 4450B08 RIGHT REAR WHEEL - R.R

   Op. 7055B44 LEFT REAR WHEEL ARCH LINER - R.R

- Or, alternatively.

   Op. 7055B45 RIGHT REAR WHEEL ARCH LINER - R.R


1. Working at the back of the wheel arch, disconnect the electrical connection (1a) for the pressure sender unit (1b).

 

 

Description

  

Connector
 

1a

  

TYRE PRESSURE CONTROL UNIT AERIAL

  

   See P012 TYRE PRESSURE CONTROL UNIT ANTENNA

2. Unscrew the nuts.

3. Remove the pressure sender unit (1b).
Refitting ( Removing )

- Check that the pressure sender unit is not damaged.

- Position the pressure sender unit.

- Screw in the nuts.

- Connect the electrical connection.

   Op. 7055B45 RIGHT REAR WHEEL ARCH LINER - R.R

- Or, alternatively.

   Op. 7055B44 LEFT REAR WHEEL ARCH LINER - R.R

   Op. 4450B08 RIGHT REAR WHEEL - R.R

- Or, alternatively.

   Op. 4450B06 LEFT REAR WHEEL - R.R

- Remove the vehicle from the lift.

- Check that the T.P.M.S. is working properly.
