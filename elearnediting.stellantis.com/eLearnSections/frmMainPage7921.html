140 - Alfa 159   2.4 JTD 20V   DISPLAY DOES NOT SHOW AVERAGE SPEED ON TRIP MODULE   K126   

With the key ON, the average speed reading on the display (one of the displays on the TRIP COMPUTER module) is not operational.

The display provides the average speed processed by the instrument panel node according to the distance travelled and journey time information.

If the speed information (from the brake node) is missing, the consumption data will be replaced by the reading "----"
