140 - Alfa 159   2.4 JTD 20V   ONE WARNING LIGHT ACTIVATED BY STEERING COLUMN SWITCH UNIT ON CONSTANTLY   K144   

When the lights are deactivated using the stalk, the relevant warning light does not go off on the instrument panel

N.B: The warning lights activated when the stalk is operated are:

  • Side lights/number plate lights

  • Main beam headlamps

  • Direction indicators
 

0

  

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

With the key ON, turn all the lights on and off from the stalk, i.e.:

  • Using the wheel, side lights/number plate lights

  • With the stalk, high beams (both fixed and flashing positions)

  • With the stalk, turn signals (right and left) and check they turn on/off
