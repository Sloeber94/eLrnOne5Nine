140 - Alfa 159   2.4 JTD 20V   Accelerator pedal sensor 2   P0220   

Lo strumento di diagnosi segnala: Errore P0220 (Sensore pedale acceleratore 2).

This error is detected if, with fault code P0651 not recognized, the junction unit input voltage (Pin 31) is more than 4.67 V (positive short circuit) or less than 0.15 V. As a consequence the accelerator pedal does not operate.
