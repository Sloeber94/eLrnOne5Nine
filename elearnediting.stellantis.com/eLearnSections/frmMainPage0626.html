Failed folding - Service Solution


FAULT COMPLAINED OF

Incomplete folding of exterior rear view mirrors.

TECHNICAL CAUSE

Interference between the mirror body and the inner boss of the exterior rear view mirrors.

OPERATIONS IN PRODUCTION

Fitting of improved exterior rear view mirrors guaranteed.

OPERATIONS IN NETWORK

Network behaviour standards towards the Customer

-When this problem is reported and confirmed, reassure the Customer that the problem can be solved by removing the plastic burr on the exterior rear view mirror.-After the operation show the Customer what has been done and carry out a test to make sure they are satisfied.

Service Solution

if this problem is reported and confirmed, remove the exterior rear view mirror boss as described in the Operating Cycle below.

OPERATION CYCLE

-Fold the exterior mirror over manually.-Completely remove the boss (1 - Fig. 1) on the lower part of the moving part of the exterior mirror using a cutter.

    -Check that the exterior rear view mirrors fold properly (positions A-B in Fig. 2) using the button (C - Fig. 2) on the driver''s electric window panel.
