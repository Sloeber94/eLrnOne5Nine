140 - Alfa 159   2.4 JTD 20V   BACKREST ADJUSTMENT NOISE   S068   

Noise during adjustment
 

1

  

CHECKS ON POSITIONING OF KNOB

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check that the knob is correctly positioned in relation to the casing

  

Move on to Step 2

  

Position of knob incorrect

  

Loosen and retighten the side cover to torque and reposition the knob correctly.
 

2

  

CHECKS ON KNOB SEAL

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check that the seal of the knob is correct using the retaining elements

  

Move on to Step 3

  

Retaining elements broken/distorted.

  

Replace the knob

 Op. 7045A24 FRONT SEAT BACKREST ANGLE ADJUSTMENT KNOB (ONE), LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R.
 

3

  

CHECK ON INTERFERENCE WITH BACKREST PARTS

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check for the presence of interference between the joint connecting rod and parts of the backrest.

  

Move on to Step 4

  

Interference between rod and components

  

Space the parts interfering with the joint connecting rod apart.
 

4

  

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check that the fault is no longer present.

  

End of diagnosis

Replace the seat frame
