Lo strumento di diagnosi segnala: Errore P0402 (Valvola EGR).

This error is detected if the quantity of exhaust gas recirculation is too high. As a result, the exhaust gas recirculation valve is switched off and the supercharging pressure can longer be controlled and the quantity of fuel injected is reduced.
