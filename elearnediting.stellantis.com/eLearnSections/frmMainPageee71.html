Removing ( Refitting )

The 4x2 version is described below

- Position the vehicle on a lift.

    Op. 7055B54 PROTECTION/GUARD UNDER ENGINE -R.R.

    Op. 1048A50 LOWER ENGINE BLOCK - R.R. CLEAN SEALING SURFACES AND APPLY SEALANT

- Draw off the power assisted steering fluid from the reservoir using a suitable syringe.

1. Undo connector (1a) and disconnect the delivery pipe from the pump to power assisted steering box (1b), power assisted steering box side.

1. Undo connector (1a) and disconnect the delivery pipe from the pump to power assisted steering box (1b), power assisted steering pump side, and remove after releasing from the intermediate fastenings.

Refitting ( Removing )

1. Refit the delivery pipe from the pump to power steering box (1a), secure with intermediate fastenings and connect to the power steering pump. Tighten connector (1b) to the specified torque.
