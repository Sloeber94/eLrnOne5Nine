Removing ( Refitting )

- Position the vehicle on a lift.

   Op. 7055B54 PROTECTION/GUARD UNDER ENGINE -R.R.

1. Undo the nut and disconnect the engine oil supply rear pipe to the cooling radiator, front pipe side.

Collect the engine oil that comes out in a suitable container.

2. Undo the bolt and disconnect the engine oil supply rear pipe to the cooling radiator, engine oil filter mounting side.

3. Loosen the nut securing the mounting bracket for the rear engine oil supply and return pipes to the cooling radiator.

4. Remove the engine oil supply rear pipe to the cooling radiator.

- Remove the O-ring.
Refitting ( Removing )

- Refit the engine oil supply rear pipe to the cooling radiator in its seat complete with new O-ring, connect to the oil filter mount and tighten the bolt.

- Connect the engine oil supply rear pipe to the cooling radiator, front pipe side, and tighten the nut.

- Tighten the nut securing the mounting bracket for the engine oil supply and return rear pipes to the cooling radiator.

   Op. 0010T10 CHANGE ENGINE OIL

   Op. 7055B54 PROTECTION/GUARD UNDER ENGINE -R.R.

- Remove the vehicle from the lift.
