Removing-refitting driveshaft boot bands

 


1. Retract the protective gaiter on the driveshaft.

If the boot cannot be moved back, cut it using an appropriate tool and remove it.

1. Clean the drive shaft constant velocity joint until the safety ring is accessible.

1. Open the safety ring using suitable pliers.

2. Extract the wheel side constant velocity joint from the driveshaft.

NOTE: on joints without safety ring, pull to remove the constant velocity joint from the drive shaft.

1. Remove the wheel side constant velocity joint safety ring.

On joints without a safety ring, the ring is positioned on the driveshaft and must therefore be removed from this position.

1. Remove the protective boot.

Refitting ( Removing )

- Wash the constant velocity joint components thoroughly using fuel oil or petrol and visually inspect that there are no signs of seizing or grooves on the balls or seats.

- Position the new protective boot on the driveshaft.

1. Place a new safety ring in the housing on the constant velocity joint.

Check that the safety ring is correctly fitted.

For joints without a safety ring, fit a new safety ring on the driveshaft.

- Place the wheel side constant velocity joint in its housing on the driveshaft.

Push the constant velocity joint until the safety ring clicks into position.

- Fill the joint and the boot with the specified grease.
