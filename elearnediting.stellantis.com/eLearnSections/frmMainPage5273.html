140 - Alfa 159   2.4 JTD 20V   CLIMATE CONTROL NOT COOLING   L010   

APPLICABLE TO VERSIONS WITH: Automatic climate control

The fan works properly and sends air to the passenger compartment according to the pre-set flows but the air remains at the outside temperature and is not cooled.

APPLICABLE TO VERSIONS WITH: Automatic climate control

The enablement of the engagement of the refrigeration circuit is controlled by both the climate control node and the engine management node
