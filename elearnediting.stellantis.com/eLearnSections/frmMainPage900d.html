140 - Alfa 159   2.4 JTD 20V   SUPRECHARGING PRESSURE CHECK   1064BA   
Refitting ( Removing )

Install connector 1.870.752.000 between the turbocharging sensor and the intake chamber.

Connect the pressure transducer to the tool.

Position the diagnosis equipment in the passenger compartment and make the connections.

Select "pressure gauge" tool on the diagnosis equipment and prepare for the acquisition of data in "intake pressure" mode with a 2000 mmHg full scale value and selection time of 10 seconds.

Road test the vehicle (observing the speed limits in force) and perform the following test:

· On a motorway route, allow the vehicle to reach 2500 rpm in 4th gear.

· Initiate graphic acquisition by pressing "Start" on the diagnosis equipment and approx. 2 seconds later fully depress the accelerator pedal until the engine speed is 3500 rpm.

· Once this speed is reached completely release the accelerator pedal.

· Display the resulting graph and in the pressure constant section, with the exception of the initial peak, read a value of 1900 -2080 mbar (1444 -1581 mmHg) corresponding to a supercharging value of 900-1080 mbar.

· On a motorway route with fast-moving traffic, let the vehicle reach 2500 rpm in 3rd gear.

· Initiate graphic acquisition by pressing "Start" on the diagnosis equipment and approx. 2 seconds later fully depress the accelerator pedal until the engine speed is 3500 rpm.

· Once this speed is reached completely release the accelerator pedal.

Display the resulting graph and in the pressure constant section, with the exception of the initial peak, read a value of 1900 -2080 mbar (1444-1581 mmHg) corresponding to a supercharging value of 900-1080 mbar.
