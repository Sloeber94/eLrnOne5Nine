Noise, wind noise - Service solution


FAULT COMPLAINED OF

Noise, wind noise from external rearview mirrors.

TECHNICAL CAUSE

Infiltration of air under the lower mirror cover

OPERATIONS IN PRODUCTION

Insert a second wind noise foam seal in addition to the existing one.

OPERATIONS IN NETWORK

Network behaviour standards towards the Customer

-After having ascertained presence of the fault, reassure the customer that the problem will be solved by inserting a second wind noise foam seal in addition to the existing one.-After the intervention, check the work done and check that customer is satisfied.

Service Solution

If the fault is reported, on vehicles with chassis numbers from 7200000 to 7229783, insert the new wind noise foam seal as shown in the following cycle.

OPERATION CYCLE

Appropriately protect the area surrounding the lower cover of the external rearview mirrors with adhesive tape, as shown in the figure.

Lift the cover (A - Fig. 1) with a thin tip screwdriver. Proceed with care to prevent damaging the paintwork.

Fig. 1



    Insert the new wind noise foam seal N. 156089150 (B - Fig. 2) procured from Ricambi and appropriately wrap the mirror motor harness.

    Fig. 2



      Refit the lower cover (A - Fig. 1) on the rearview mirror and remove the protective tape.

      Repeat the operations described on the opposite side of the car.
