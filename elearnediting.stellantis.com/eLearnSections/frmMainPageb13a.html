Removing ( Refitting )

The following procedure describes the removing-refitting of the rear suspension right shock absorber, carry out the same operations for the rear suspension left shock absorber.

- Position the vehicle on a lift.

   Op. 4450B08 RIGHT REAR WHEEL - R.R

1. Working via the luggage compartment, undo the nut (1a) and remove the rear shock absorber upper mounting (1b).

1. Unscrew the bolts (1a), the bolt (1b) and remove the rear suspension front shock absorber assembly (1c).

1. At the bench, position the shock absorber assembly (1a) on the tool (1b) for dismantling.
