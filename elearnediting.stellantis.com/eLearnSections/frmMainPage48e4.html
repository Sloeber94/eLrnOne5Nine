Removing ( Refitting )

1. Use a plastic spatula to raise one end (1a) of the moulding by releasing from its adhesive retainer (1b).

2. Fully remove moulding (2a) by undoing side retainers (2b) and the other adhesive retainer at the end.

Refitting ( Removing )

- Check that the moulding is not damaged.

1. Clean area (1a) where adhesive pads (1b) are anchored with heptane.

2. Remove adhesive pad protections (1c) and immediately fit moulding (2b) in place, attaching the adhesive pads and side retainers.

3. Press hard on thethe moulding over the adhesive pads for a few seconds.

- Check that the moulding is correctly positioned.
