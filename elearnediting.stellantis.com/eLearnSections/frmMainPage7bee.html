140 - Alfa 159   2.4 JTD 20V   CHECK ENGINE CONTROL UNIT CONNECTIONS   1060GN   
 

STEP

  

CHECK TO BE PERFORMED

  

RESOLUTION IF THE CHECK IS NOT OK
 

1

  

Press the START/STOP button and turn off the engine.

Disconnect the wiring harness connector from the fuel pressure sensor.

Press the START/STOP button.

Select the following system on the diagnostic equipment: Diesel EDC16C39 electronic injection system.

Check that error P0651 – Power supply to sensors 2 is “Present”.

Move on to Step 5

  

Move on to Step 2
 

2

  

Press the START/STOP button.

Disconnect the wiring harness connector from engine control unit connector B

Press the START/STOP button.

Check that the voltage across pin 43 of the control unit

   See M010 ENGINE MANAGEMENT CONTROL UNIT

and the earth is greater than 0.3 V.

  

Repair or if necessary replace the electrical wiring between the fuel pressure sensor and the engine control unit

   See E5050 DIESEL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

 

3

  

Check that the voltage across pin 8 of the control unit

   See M010 ENGINE MANAGEMENT CONTROL UNIT

and the earth is greater than 0.3 V.

  

Repair or if necessary replace the electrical wiring between the fuel pressure sensor and the engine control unit

   See E5050 DIESEL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

 

4

  

Replace the fuel pressure sensor

   Op. 1060G28 FUEL PRESSURE SENSOR ON FUEL MANIFOLD TO INJECTORS - R.R

The test can be considered over

  

 

5

  

Press the START/STOP button.

Disconnect the wiring harness connector from the air temperature/pressure sensor.

Press the START/STOP button.

Select the following system on the diagnostic equipment: Diesel EDC16C39 electronic injection system.

Check that error P0651 – Power supply to sensors 2 is “Present”.

Move on to Step 9

  

Move on to Step 6
