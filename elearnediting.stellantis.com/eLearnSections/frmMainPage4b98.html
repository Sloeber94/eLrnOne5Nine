New opening securing wiring for electric tailgate opening - Network Information


OPERATIONS IN PRODUCTION

As already stated in SN 00.01.08, the new MY08 versions feature a new function where the tailgate it opened from outside the vehicle by pressing the logo with the electric microswitch illustrated in Fig. 1 (push badge).

Fig. 1



To fit the new electric tailgate opening control it has also been necessary to involve the tailgate panel making a 7 mm diameter opening (A - Fig. 2) to secure the wiring connector.

Fig. 2



OPERATIONS IN NETWORK

On the new MY08 versions, in the case of a service operation that involves replacing the tailgate, it is necessary to make sure that the replacement part has the 7 mm opening in the frame (A - Fig. 2); if there is no opening, it should be made as follows:

-trace the centre of the opening (A) 20 mm from the existing opening (B) at the distances shown in Fig. 3-Chisel the centre of the opening-with a 7 mm diameter bit to make the opening (A)-remove any off-cuts and burrs-clean the surfaces affected by the painting with a cloth soaked in a low solvent detergent (e.g. PPG dx380 or alternative solutions permitted by legislation protecting the environment)-apply the anti-corrosion bicomponent expoxide base coat to the opening made using a brush 40-50 Micron thick (e.g. Glasurit - VOC 801-72 grey, Standox-epg rundierfuller by Dupont grey, or PPG Iviepox 458515 brown or an equivalent product)-dry the base coat with a drier for about 10 minutes at a temperature of 80°- 90° centigrade-Continue with painting the tailgate-then fit the connector (C) for the electric tailgate opening control in the opening made and continue with refitting the components on the tailgate.

Fig. 3
