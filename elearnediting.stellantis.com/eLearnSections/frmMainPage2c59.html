Refitting ( Removing )

Although the various motors on the air conditioner casing are identical in shape, they are not interchangeable.

When ordering a new motor, carefully identify the part code of the motor to be replaced and mark the position of the motor on the air conditioner casing.

- Check that the motor is not damaged.

- Position the motor and engage the splined connection shaft.

- Tighten the bolts.

- Connect the electrical connection.
