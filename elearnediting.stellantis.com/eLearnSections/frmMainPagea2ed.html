CUSTOMER'S REPORTED FAULT

The engine does not perform/does not start.

TECHNICAL CAUSE

Found errors P0521 P0524 in the ECU

OPERATION

After reprogramming the ECUs with the latest DPF handling SW, two Recovery levels were added, to protect the engine from damage due to the lacked control of the engine oil warning light by the customer.

These two Recovery situations are handled by the ECU by setting two errors, when the oil degradation exceeds two thresholds:

P0521 for the "lightest" Recovery

P0524 for the "heaviest" Recovery

These errors can be removed only by changing engine oil.

Therefore, if errors P0521 or P0524 occur, engine oil must be changed

If the ECU is upgraded before the engine oil replacement, if the oil degradation has exceeded the relevant thresholds, the relevant error is validated and the engine Recovery is set.
