Deactivation procedure and description - Network Information


DESCRIPTION

The vehicles of the model in question will be equipped with a new Logistic Mode (LM) function which makes it possible to exclude certain electrical loads, except the main functions (vehicle starting, lights, etc.) in order to maintain the optimum battery charge. This low consumption supply mode is activated by the factory and guarantees the possibility of being able to access the vehicle and drive it without any adverse effects to safety during movement logistic stages.

If the LM mode is activated, some vehicle components and comfort functions may be disabled, such as, for example:

-Central locking-Remote control operation functions (remote control or CID)-Electric window movements-Radio-Navigator-Current sockets/cigar lighters-Clock-Climate control-Additional heater-Electric sun roof-Heated seats-Courtesy lights-Boot/bonnet courtesy lights-Door courtesy lights-Fog lights/rear fog lamps-Main beam headlamps

It is possible that the LM mode may not have been activated on the first vehicles produced; in this case it is necessary to check the presence of all the fuses in the junction unit and renew any that are missing.

Deactivation of logistic mode

After a suitable check, the low consumption supply mode (LM) can be deactivated using the Examiner before the vehicle is handed over to the final customer.

IT IS ONLY POSSIBLE TO DEACTIVATE THE LOGISTIC MODE ONCE; AFTER DEACTIVATION NO FUTHER ACTIVATIONS/DEACTIVATIONS CAN BE CARRIED OUT

Status check

-Insert the electronic key and check that the alternator recharging warning light (A - Fig. 1) is flashing (BLINKING mode); the flashing of this warning light indicates that the low consumption supply mode LM is activated.

If the alternator recharging warning light is flashing, do not carry out any repair/replacement operation because this is not a fault but the indication of the correct Logistic Mode low consumption supply mode activation status.

Deactivation procedure

-Connect the Examiner equipment to the vehicle EOBD socket and turn the ignition ON.-Select the "Control Units Test" function-Select the "Alfa 159" model-Select the "Body Computer Marelli 3" component-Follow the instrument instructions-Press the YES button in response to the question "Do you wish to restore the normal supply mode for the above control units"-Check that the Logistic Mode has been deactivated by checking the alternator recharging warning light (it remains on at the key on until the engine has been started) and the reactivation of all the functions.
