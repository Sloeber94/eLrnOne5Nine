Difficulties in starting - Service Solution


FAULT COMPLAINED OF

Engine starting difficult or impossible.

TECHNICAL CAUSE

Earth lead terminal fastening fixing bolt slack.

OPERATIONS IN PRODUCTION

Protection of welding zone and earth lead terminal with red paint mark and increase in torque to 2.4 daNm (previously 1.5 daNm).

OPERATIONS IN NETWORK

Network behaviour standards towards the Customer

-When this problem is reported and confirmed, reassure the Customer that it can be solved by adjusting the earth lead-After the operation show the Customer what has been done and make sure they are satisfied.

Service Solution

If this problem is reported and confirmed, proceed as follows:

-A) check that the earth lead terminal (A - Fig. 1) is correctly positioned making sure that the surface of the terminal is flat in relation to the earth plate (C) and that the terminal is intact and free from signs of burning (see Operating Cycle A)-B) if there are obvious signs of burning replace the earth lead with a new one (see Operating Cycle B).

OPERATION CYCLE

Cycle a

1) Remove the left front wheeland partly remove the wheel arch liner;

2) loosen the bolt (B - Fig. 1) fastening the earth lead terminal (A) to the earth plate (C) on the side panel strut (D);

3) check the condition of the earth lead terminal (A) and the bolt (B) (there should be no signs of burning or overheating);

If there are signs of burning or overheating, proceed with the replacement (earth lead and bolt) as described in cycle B;

4) in the case of conformity (no burning or overheating) simply tighten the bolt to the new torque of 2.4 daNm using a torque wrench and search for another cause of the problem reported by the customer.

Cycle b

After having carried out points 1 to 3 of Cycle A and having checked for signs of burning or overheating on the earth lead (A) and/or on the bolt (B) and/or on the earth plate (C), proceed with replacing the actual lead and the bolt and cleaning the plate as follows:

1) remove the guard under the engine;

2) loosen the M8 nut securing the earth lead, gearbox side;

3) replace the earth lead (A) with a new one no. 50505962 from the Parts Dept.;

4) clean the contact surface of the lead on the earth plate (C) which also contains the female screw thoroughly;

5) if necessary, manually rethread the female screw (housed in the plate) using an M8x1.25 tool;

No lubricants or other products should be used and it is necessary to check that the opening axis and male element are coaxial through a visual inspection

6) visually inspect the rethreaded female screw removing any machining residues using an acrylic foam buffer (do not use compressed air);

7) place the new earth lead no. 50505962 on the gearbox side securing it using the M8 nut placing the rotation prevention plate on the bolt on the vehicle, then tighten it to a torque of 2.4 daNm;

8) replace the strut side bolt (B) securing the terminal to the earth plate (C) with a new one no. 16296421 obtained from the Parts Dept. (the bolt should be tightened manually for the first 2 threads); then tighten it to the new torque of 2.4 daNm.
