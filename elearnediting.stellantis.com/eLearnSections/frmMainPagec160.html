The brake fluid replacement process is described below.

- Position the vehicle on a lift.

- Draw off the brake/clutch fluid from the reservoir using a suitable syringe.

- Remove the protective cap from the bleed valve on one brake caliper.

- Connect the recovery device (pipe and reservoir) to the bleed valve on the brake calliper.

- Open the bleed valve on the brake caliper.

- Pump the brake pedal until all the fluid emerges.

- Repeat the operations described above for the remaining brake calipers.

- Connect suitable equipment to the brake/clutch fluid reservoir.

- Slowly open the device reservoir cock and refill the brake/clutch systems with the recommended fluid.
