Difficult starting and/or cold knock with irregular rotation for about 30 seconds - Injection/EOBD system failure warning light switching on with Examiner error P010F - Service Solution


Cancels and replaces SN 10.06.10 of 17/02/2010 for validity extension to 2.4JTDm 200 HP Manual Gearbox with DPF.

FAULT COMPLAINED OF

A) Engine difficult starting when cold and knock with engine cold irregular rotation at idle for about 30 seconds from the starting.

B) The engine control/injection system failure warning light (EOBD) comes on.

Examiner shows the presence of error: P010F – Air flow/flow meter inconsistency (only for 3.4 JTDm 20v 200 HP Manual Gearbox with DPF and Automatic Transmission, with or without DRV).

TECHNICAL CAUSE

Injection control unit software fault.

OPERATIONS IN NETWORK

Network behaviour standards towards the Customer

-Once the claimed fault is confirmed, reassure the Customer that the fault will be solved by reprogramming the injection control unit.-After the intervention, check the work done and make sure that the Customer is satisfied.

Service Solution

For the above versions, with chassis higher than no. 7237207, if the customer''s claimed fault is confirmed, reprogramme the injection control unit with the new software WEB Flash 6.03.0 (or higher) available on Examiner Web according to the Operating Cycle below.

OPERATION CYCLE

Using the Examiner with release 8.00.0 (or higher), reprogramme the injection control unit using the WEB Flash 6.03.0 (or higher) available on the Examiner Web.

The vehicle battery must be in full charge conditions or supported by a battery charger/booster battery so as to ensure a rated voltage of 12 V, during reprogramming.
