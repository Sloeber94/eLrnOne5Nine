Removing ( Refitting )

It is not possible to remove-refit the timing side power unit rigid support on the vehicle on this version.

The power unit must be removed-refitted to perform this operation

   Op. 1004B10 POWER UNIT WITH MANUAL GEARBOX - R.R.

or

   Op. 1004B11 POWER UNIT WITH MECHANIC TRANSMISSION - R.R. FOR FOUR-WHEEL DRIVE MODELS

or

   Op. 1004B14 POWER UNIT WITH AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION R + R

and then proceed as described below keeping the power unit attached to a suitable hydraulic lift.

1. Undo the bolts (1a) and remove the protective timing cover (1b).

2. Undo the bolts (2a) and remove the timing side rigid engine mounting (2b).
Refitting ( Removing )

- Place the timing side power unit rigid support back in its housing and tighten the bolts to the recommended torque.
