- Move the gear selector to position "N".

Automatic transmission control unit

  

   See M055 AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION CONTROL UNIT

1. Disconnect the gear selection cable from the gear selector and reaction bracket.

1. Undo nut (1a) and remove lever (1b) from the gear selector on the automatic transmission.

2. Undo the bolts (2a) and remove the electronic automatic transmission control unit (2b).

- Remove the O-ring on the connector for the automatic transmission internal wiring.

Refitting ( Removing )

1. Fit a new O-ring on the connector for the automatic transmission internal wiring.

2. If necessary, remove the seal on the gear selector shaft and replace it.

The new seal for the gear selector shaft may project or be recessed in relation to the gearbox casing surface by 0.3 mm.

3. Check that the gear selector shaft is in position "N" with the bevels positioned as illustrated.

- Check that the connector for the automatic transmission internal wiring is correctly housed on the gearbox casing with the pin (a) positioned as illustrated.

- Place the control unit for the electronic automatic transmission in its housing and tighten the bolts to the specified torque.
