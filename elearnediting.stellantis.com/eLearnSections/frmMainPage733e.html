Removing ( Refitting )

See the Wiring Diagrams section for accurate identification of the electrical connections.

1. Working on the left side of the boot compartment, undo nuts (1a) and move the boot compartment junction unit (1b).

1. Working the rear seat cushion area, unscrew bolts (1a) and move the access lid to fuel pump (1b).

1. Disconnect all the electrical connections for the wiring.

2. Release the wiring from the retainers and remove it.
Refitting ( Removing )

- Check that the wiring is not damaged and that the individual conducts are correctly fastened to the connections.

If necessary, carry out continuity tests.

- Position the wiring and secure it using the various retainers.

- Connect all the electrical connections for the wiring.

- Working in the rear seat cushion area, position the access lid to the fuel pump and tighten the bolts.

- Working on the left side of the boot compartment, position the boot junction unit and tighten the nuts.
