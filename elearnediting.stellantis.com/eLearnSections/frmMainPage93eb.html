CLUTCH UNIT

The clutch-flywheel system is supplied by VALEO. Its features are as follows:

Dry, single plate type clutch with hydraulically operated thrust release, coaxial internal actuator; outer diameter 240 mm; spring loading 640 daN.


"sat" (self adjusting technology) disc wear take-up system

The clutch supplied by Valeo has a mechanical device for the take-up of friction disc wear known as "SAT" which exploits the rotation principle between the two rings with inclined adjacent planes. The components for this system are illustrated in the section below.


1. Toothed plate

2. Clutch cover

3. Diaphragm spring

4. Pressure plate

5. Toothed sector with inclined plane

6. Worm screw

7. Gear

8. Thrust ramp with inclined plane

SYSTEM OPERATING PRINCIPLE

ENGAGEMENT WHEN DISC IS NEW: toothed plate (1) is pressed against the top of gear tooth (2);


RELEASE WHEN DISC IS NEW: toothed plate (1) rests on the clip incorporated in cover (2); in this position it is still in contact with the previous tooth;


ENGAGEMENT WHEN DISC IS USED: toothed plate (1) presses gear tooth (2) with a value corresponding to the wear of the friction disc;


RELEASE WHEN DISC IS USED: toothed plate (1), being able to move because diaphragm spring (2) is released, slides on the next gear tooth (3); it is now in a position to rotate the gear for the next engagement operation;


NEXT ENGAGEMENT: toothed plate (1) presses the tooth for a distance corresponding to the height of the tooth; this operation rotates gear (2) and, at the same time, toothed sector (3) and consequently thrust ramp (4), by means of the system of inclined planes; at the end of the engagement the ramp is moved axially by the exact value of the wear to be recovered.


Driven friction plate

The friction plate contains a spring system which acts as flexible coupling; the connection between the propeller shaft and the gearbox is made through a splined hub. The side facing the flywheel is shown in the photo.
