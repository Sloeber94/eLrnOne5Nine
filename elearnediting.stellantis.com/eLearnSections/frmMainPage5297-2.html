140 - Alfa 159   2.4 JTD 20V   ELECTRIC STEERING LOCK CHECK   5580ED   

Go back to IGNITION PUSH BUTTON CHECK
 

 

CHECK TO BE PERFORMED

  

RESOLUTION IF THE CHECK IS NOT OK
 

1

  

Connect the diagnostic instrument in Voltmeter mode with the positive terminal connected to Pin C1 and the negative earthed to Pin A4 of the steering lock control unit. The direct battery voltage reading should be 12 Volts.

   See M089 STEERING LOCK CONTROL UNIT (NBS)

  

Restore wiring continuity

   See E7065 ELECTRIC STEERING LOCK
 

2

  

Insert a key in the key reader.

Connect the diagnostic instrument in Voltmeter mode with the positive terminal connected to Pin A6 and the negative earthed. The direct battery voltage reading should be 12 Volts.

   See M089 STEERING LOCK CONTROL UNIT (NBS)

Move on to Step 4

  

Move on to Step 3
 

3

  

Check whether the wiring is continuous between Pin 12 of the ignition device and control unit Pin A6.

   See H003 IGNITION DEVICE

   See M089 STEERING LOCK CONTROL UNIT (NBS)

Repair if necessary

   See E7065 ELECTRIC STEERING LOCK

The test can be considered over

  

Continue as indicated

   See Test 5505AF CHECK ON EFFICIENCY OF KEY RECOGNITION DEVICE
 

4

  

Disconnect the wiring connector from the control unit

   See M089 STEERING LOCK CONTROL UNIT (NBS)

Connect the diagnostic instrument in Ohmmeter mode with one probe between Pin A5 of the control unit and Earth

   See M089 STEERING LOCK CONTROL UNIT (NBS)

Press the clutch pedal and check for an earth signal.

Move on to Step 6

if no clutch signal is detected by the steering lock node, the car can still be started up by pressing the brake pedal

  

Move on to Step 5
 

5

  

Check the continuity of the wiring between Pin 4 of the clutch pedal switch and control unit Pin A5.

   See I031 CLUTCH PEDAL SWITCH

   See M089 STEERING LOCK CONTROL UNIT (NBS)

Repair if necessary

   See E7065 ELECTRIC STEERING LOCK

If necessary, replace the clutch pedal switch

   Op. 1056B78 SWITCH ON CLUTCH PEDAL - R.R (PETROL ENGINES)

or alternatively

   Op. 1060D42 SWITCH ON CLUTCH PEDAL- R. R (DIESEL ENGINES)

The test can be considered over

  

Replace the steering lock node

   Op. 5580E17 STEERING LOCK - R.R
 

6

  

With the diagnostic instrument in Voltmeter mode, connect the positive probe to control unit Pin B7 and the negative to earth

   See M089 STEERING LOCK CONTROL UNIT (NBS)

Press the clutch pedal.

Press the ignition button.

Check for a battery voltage signal of 12 Volts.

Move on to Step 8

  

Move on to Step 7
 

7

  

Check whether or not there is continuity in the wiring between Pin B7 of the control unit and Pin 6 of the button

   See M089 STEERING LOCK CONTROL UNIT (NBS)

   See H002 STARTING PUSH BUTTON

Repair if necessary

   See E7065 ELECTRIC STEERING LOCK

The test can be considered over

  

Replace the ignition button

   Op. 5520G30 START/STOP SWITCH FOR EASY-GO SYSTEM STARTER MOTOR - R.R.
 

8

  

Press the ignition button.

With the diagnostic instrument in Voltmeter mode, connect the positive probe to control unit Pin B6 and the negative to earth

   See M089 STEERING LOCK CONTROL UNIT (NBS)

Press the clutch pedal.

Press the ignition button.

Check for a battery voltage signal of 12 Volts.

Move on to Step 10

  

Move on to Step 9
 

9

  

Check whether or not there is continuity in the wiring between Pin B6 of the control unit and Pin 4 of the button

   See M089 STEERING LOCK CONTROL UNIT (NBS)

   See H002 STARTING PUSH BUTTON

Repair if necessary

   See E7065 ELECTRIC STEERING LOCK

The test can be considered over

  

Replace the ignition button

   Op. 5520G30 START/STOP SWITCH FOR EASY-GO SYSTEM STARTER MOTOR - R.R.
 

10

  

With the diagnostic instrument in Voltmeter mode, connect the positive probe to control unit Pin B1 and the negative to earth

   See M089 STEERING LOCK CONTROL UNIT (NBS)

The direct voltage reading should be 12 Volt.

  

Replace the steering lock node

   Op. 5580E17 STEERING LOCK - R.R
 

11

  

With the diagnostic instrument in Voltmeter mode, connect the positive probe to control unit Pin A1 and the negative to earth

   See M089 STEERING LOCK CONTROL UNIT (NBS)

The direct voltage reading should be 12 Volt.

  

Replace the steering lock node

   Op. 5580E17 STEERING LOCK - R.R
 

12

  

With the diagnostic instrument in Voltmeter mode, connect the positive probe to control unit Pin A8 and the negative to earth

   See M089 STEERING LOCK CONTROL UNIT (NBS)

The direct voltage reading should be 12 Volt.

  

Replace the steering lock node

   Op. 5580E17 STEERING LOCK - R.R
 

13

  

With the diagnostic instrument in Voltmeter mode, connect the positive probe to control unit Pin B2 and the negative to earth

   See M089 STEERING LOCK CONTROL UNIT (NBS)

The direct voltage reading should be 12 Volt.

  

Replace the steering lock node

   Op. 5580E17 STEERING LOCK - R.R
 

14

  

With the diagnostic instrument in Voltmeter mode, connect the positive probe to control unit Pin C8 and the negative to earth

   See M089 STEERING LOCK CONTROL UNIT (NBS)

The direct voltage reading should be 12 Volt.

  

Replace the steering lock node

   Op. 5580E17 STEERING LOCK - R.R
 

15

  

With the diagnostic instrument in Voltmeter mode, connect the positive probe to control unit Pin B8 and the negative to earth

   See M089 STEERING LOCK CONTROL UNIT (NBS)

Press the clutch pedal.

Press the ignition button.

Check for a battery voltage signal of 12 Volts.

  

Replace the steering lock node

   Op. 5580E17 STEERING LOCK - R.R
 

16

  

With the diagnostic instrument in Voltmeter mode, connect the positive probe to control unit Pin C4 and the negative to earth

   See M089 STEERING LOCK CONTROL UNIT (NBS)

Press the brake pedal and the battery voltage reading should be 12 Volts.

Release the brake pedal and the voltage reading should be 0 volts.

Move on to Step 20

  

Move on to Step 17
 

17

  

Check whether or not there is continuity in the wiring between Pin 4 of the brake pedal switch and Pin C4 of the control unit.

   See I030 BRAKE PEDAL SWITCH

   See M089 STEERING LOCK CONTROL UNIT (NBS)

Repair if necessary

   See E7065 ELECTRIC STEERING LOCK

The test can be considered over

  

Check the power supply is correct to the brake pedal switch and restore if necessary.

Move on to Step 18
 

18

  

Check the diagnostic instrument in parameter mode to ensure the brake pedal switch is operating correctly.

  

Replace the switch

   Op. 5550D10 BRAKE LIGHT SWITCH - R+R

or alternatively

   Op. 1060D38 SWITCH ON BRAKE PEDAL - R.R (DIESEL ENGINES)
 

19

  

Replace the steering lock node

   Op. 5580E17 STEERING LOCK - R.R

The test can be considered over

  

 

20

  

With the diagnostic instrument in Voltmeter mode, connect the positive probe to control unit Pin C7 and the negative to earth

   See M089 STEERING LOCK CONTROL UNIT (NBS)

Press the brake pedal and the battery voltage reading should be 0 Volts.

Release the brake pedal and the voltage reading should be 12 volts.

  

Move on to Step 17
 

21

  

With the diagnostic instrument in Voltmeter mode, connect the positive probe to control unit Pin C2 and the negative to earth

   See M089 STEERING LOCK CONTROL UNIT (NBS)

Turn on the side lights and check for the presence of a PWM signal

  

Replace the steering lock node

   Op. 5580E17 STEERING LOCK - R.R
