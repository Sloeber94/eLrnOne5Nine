Retract driveshaft from front wheel hub

  


1. Disconnect the left driveshaft from the differential and remove it.

2. Remove the safety ring on the left driveshaft using a screwdriver.

Refitting ( Removing )

- Refit a new safety ring in its seat on the left driveshaft.

- Lubricate the mating surfaces on the left driveshaft with oil.

- Connect the left driveshaft to the differential.

Fit the driveshaft until the safety ring locks. Take care not to damage the teeth.

- Refit the left driveshaft in its seat on the wheel hub and secure by screwing in a new nut without tightening.

1. Tighten the bolt securing the lower fork connecting the front suspension shock absorber to the lower track control arm to the recommended torque.
