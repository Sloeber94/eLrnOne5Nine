OPERATIONS IN NETWORK

To complete the information in the Service Manuals in your possession, we are enclosing a diagnostic procedure to determine the causes of the malfunction of the preheating system.

When carrying out the diagnostic procedure, at the end of the repair, check that the action taken has solved the problem and it is no longer present. If this case the diagnostic procedure is over and has led to the problem being identified and solved. If this is not the case, if the problem is still present it is necessary to continue with the diagnostic procedure by starting again from the Step indicated under "All OK".

Procedure 1 - restoring correct operation of preheating system

Foreword:

Each time of the preheating system components (heater plugs control unit, heater plugs, engine management control unit, wiring) is replaced/renewed, a complete "heater plugs activation plus heater plugs warning light flashing" cycle (described below) should be carried out in order to allow the error in the engine management control unit to be correctly deleted and avoid the heater plugs warning light continuing to flash in the instrument panel even if there is no fault in the preheating system.

OPERATION CYCLE

Follow the steps below in order and to the letter:

1) Turn the ignition ON and wait until the heater plugs warning light goes out (preheating stage).

2) Start the vehicle and wait until the preheating control unit is deactivated (the heater plugs warning light should be off during this stage)

3) The heater plugs warning light starts to flash for around 30 seconds.

Point 3 must never be interrupted because turning the ignition OFF before the warning light stops flashing could mean that the whole cycle must be repeated.

4) At the end of this stage, turn the ignition OFF and repeat the starting process.

5) If the heater plugs warning light no longer flashes (defect no longer present) at the end of the activation of the preheating control unit, delete the error in the memory using the diagnostic equipment (Examiner or Examiner Plus), otherwise continue with the other checks in the diagnostic procedure (defect still present).
