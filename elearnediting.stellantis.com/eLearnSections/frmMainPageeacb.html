Seat belt not fastened warning light - Network Information


OPERATIONS IN PRODUCTION

We wish to notify you that starting from December 2005 the red "SEAT BELT NOT FASTENED " warning light (1 - Fig. 1) will be in the instrument panel both on versions with a reconfigurable multifunction display (A) and a multifunction display (B). The warning light was previously located in the front courtesy light trim.

Fig. 1



A) Reconfigurable multifunction display

B) Multifunction display

1) Seat belt not fastened warning light

OPERATIONS IN NETWORK

As far as the above is concerned, the new instrument panel with a red "SEAT BELT NOT FASTENED" warning light or the front courtesy light without the red warning light should not be interpreted as a lack of conformity.

Please circulate this information to all personnel involved including Sales/Marketing staff.
