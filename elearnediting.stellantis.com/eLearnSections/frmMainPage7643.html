2. Refit the clutch mechanism in position.

It is not possible to replace the clutch plate on its own. Instead the clutch plate/tray assembly must be replaced.

When fitting the clutch plate, check the correct installation position shown by the wording "engine side" or "gearbox side".

Handle the clutch plate carefully. Do not touch the surfaces of the friction linings with your hands.

Clean any anti-corrosion products or detergents from the friction surface of the clutch tray.
