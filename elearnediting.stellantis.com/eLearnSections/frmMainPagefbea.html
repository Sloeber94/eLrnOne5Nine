Removing ( Refitting )

The 4x2 version is described below

The following procedure describes the removing-refitting of the right rear wheel hub stub axle strut; carry out the same operations for the left rear wheel hub stub axle strut.

1. Undo bolts (1a) and remove rear brake disc guard (1b).

1. Unscrew bolt (1a) and remove rear wheel rpm sensor (1b) from the stub axle strut.

- Position a hydraulic jack underneath the rear wheel hub stub axle strut.

1. Undo the bolt securing the rear suspension front wishbone, stub axle strut side.

2. Unscrew bolt (2a) and bolt (2b) securing the rear wheel hub stub axle strut, rear suspension longitudinal arm side.

3. Remove the rear wheel hub stub axle strut.

Refitting ( Removing )

1. Place the rear wheel hub stub axle strut back in its housing.

2. Tighten bolt (2a) and bolt (2b) securing the rear wheel hub stub axle strut, rear suspension longitudinal arm side, to the specified torque.

Removing ( Refitting )

The 4x4 version is described below

1. Undo the bolts (1a) and remove the rear brake disc guard (1b).

1. Remove the wiring for the retaining bracket.

1. Unscrew the bolt (2a) and remove the rear wheel rpm sensor (1b) from the stub axle.

1. Undo the bolt fixing the stub axle to the rear suspension camber rod.

2. Undo the bolt (2a) and the bolt (2b) securing the stub axle to the rear suspension longitudinal arm.

3. Remove the rear wheel hub stub axle.
Refitting ( Removing )

- Place the stub axle back in its housing.

- Provisionally tighten the camber and toe in adjustment bolts by hand.

- Tighten the front bolt securing the longitudinal arm to the stub axle to the recommended torque.
