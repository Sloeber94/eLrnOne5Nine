Removing ( Refitting )

- Support the power unit using a hydraulic hoist fitted with suitable chains connected to the dedicated brackets.

1. Undo the nut (1a) and remove the return pipe from power steering box to heat exchanger pipe (1b).

1. Loosen the bands (1a) and disconnect the engine coolant and return supply pipes from the automatic transmission fluid heat exchanger (1b).

1. Undo the nuts (1a) and disconnect the electrical connections (1b) from the starter motor.

Starter motor

  

   See A020 STARTER MOTOR

1. Undo the bolts (1a) and remove the starter motor (1b).

1. Rotate the crankshaft to gain access to and undo the six bolts fixing the torque converter to the engine flywheel.

- Using the hydraulic hoist prepared previously, lift the power unit to access the lower bolts securing the automatic transmission to the engine.

1. Undo the bolt fixing the automatic transmission to the engine oil sump.

1. Undo the bolt (1a) fixing the automatic transmission to the oil sump and the nut (1b) securing the automatic transmission to the engine block.

1. Undo the nut fixing the automatic transmission to the engine block.

- Lower the power unit and place it on a suitable support.

1. Undo the bolt (1a) and move the three-way connector (1b) aside complete with return pipes to the water pump.

- Support the automatic transmission using a hydraulic hoist fitted with a suitable chain.

1. Undo the remaining upper bolts (1a) and separate the automatic gearbox (1b) from the power unit.

1. Remove the torque converter.

Take care because the torque converter is full of oil.

Refitting ( Removing )

- Check and clean the threads on the torque converter housing the bolts securing it to the engine flywheel.

- Check that the automatic gearbox centering bushes are correctly housed in the engine crankcase.

- Lubricate the centering housing for the torque converter with a thin layer of lubricating grease.

- Check the torque converter centering pin. check for rust, clean, if necessary, and lubricate with a thin layer of lubricating grease.

- Place the torque converter back in its housing.

1. Check that the torque converter is correctly positioned by using a sliding gauge to check that the distance (a) between the torque converter connector threads and the matching surface of the automatic gearbox is more than 23.05 mm.

- Support the automatic transmission using a hydraulic hoist fitted with a suitable chain.

- Place the automatic gearbox back in its housing.

- Tighten the bolts and the nuts securing the automatic gearbox to the specified torque.
