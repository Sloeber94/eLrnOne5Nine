Removing ( Refitting )

1. Undo the bolt (1c) and open the fuse access cover (1a), releasing the retainers (1b).

2. Release the pins (2a) and extract them, keeping the cover (1a).
Refitting ( Removing )

- Check that the cover is not damaged.

- Position the cover.

- Fit the pins and attach them.

- Close the cover and attach the retainers.
