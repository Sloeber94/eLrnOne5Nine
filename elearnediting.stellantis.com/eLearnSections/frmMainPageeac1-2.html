Removing ( Refitting )

- Position the car on a lift.

- Raise the car.

1. Working on both sides of the car, undo the screws fixed to the wheel arches (1a) and the nuts fixed to the body (1b).



1. If the car is equipped with headlight washers, undo the screws (1a) securing the front left wheel arch as well.

2. Slightly move the front part of the left wheel arch.

3. Disconnect the quick coupling (3b) for the headlight washer delivery pipe from the pump (3a).

- Recover the washer fluid in a suitable container.

1. Undo the lower screws securing the bumper.

- Lower the car.

1. Undo the upper screws securing the bumper.

1. Move bumper (1a) slightly to one side by releasing from side retainers (1b).

2. Disconnect the electrical connections.

Refitting ( Removing )

- Check that the bumper is not damaged.

- Connect the electrical connections.

- Position the bumper and fit in its seat, doing up the side retainers.

- Tighten the upper screws securing the bumper.
