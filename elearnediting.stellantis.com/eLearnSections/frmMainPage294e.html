Removing ( Refitting )

- Drain the engine coolant, refer to

   Op. 1016A10 SOUND-PROOFING COVER - R.R


1. Loosen the band and disconnect the engine coolant pipe from the coolant reservoir, reservoir side.

2. Disconnect the rapid connector for the coolant pipe from the reservoir to the cooling circuit and remove it.
Refitting ( Removing )

- Place the coolant pipe from the reservoir to the cooling circuit in position, connect it to the reservoir using the band and to the three-way connector using the rapid connector.
