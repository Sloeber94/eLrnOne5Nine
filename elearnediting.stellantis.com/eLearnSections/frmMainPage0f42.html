140 - Alfa 159   2.4 JTD 20V   Particulate filter (2nd clogg. level exceeded)   P2002   

Lo strumento di diagnosi segnala: Errore P2002 (Filtro antiparticolato).

This error is detected if the control unit recognizes a malfunction in the circuit through an internal logic evaluation: short circuit to earth/positive or break in the connecting circuit at Pin 52 of the junction unit.
 

 

 

Test results
 

1

  

VISUAL CHECK ON CONNECTIONS

  

All OK

  

Problems encountered

  

Operation
 

 

Disconnect the built-in throttle body actuator wiring harness on the engine control unit side and ensure the pins are intact and positioned correctly.

Also check that they do not show signs of oxidation, burning or the presence of water

  

Move on to Step 2

  

The wiring is faulty

  

Restore or, if necessary, replace the faulty wiring
 

2

  

CHECK PWM SIGNAL

  

All OK

  

Problems encountered

  

Operation
 

 

Connect a T braid between the motor wiring and the throttle body actuator

Using the diagnostic tester in digital multimeter mode, check actuator Pin 6 to ensure

 See N075 INTEGRATED THROTTLE BODY ACTUATOR

it is carrying a PWM (square wave) signal with bandwidth between 2 and 98%

  

Move on to Step 3

  

The signal is fully up or fully down (there is no square wave)

  

Restore or, if necessary, replace the faulty wiring
 

3

  

CHECK USING DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

  

All OK

  

Problems encountered

  

Operation
 

 

Check whether the following error is present on the diagnostic equipment

- P2100

  

Move on to Step 4

  

Error P2100 is present

  

Replace the throttle body.

 Op. 1060G50 THROTTLE BODY - R.R
 

4

  

CHECK ON THROTTLE CASING OPERATION

  

All OK

  

Problems encountered

  

Operation
 

 

Check that the built-in throttle body actuator is working properly by carrying out the

 See Test 1060GO INTEGRATED THROTTLE BODY OPERATION CHECK
