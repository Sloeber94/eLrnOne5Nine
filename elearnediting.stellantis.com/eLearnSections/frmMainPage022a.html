140 - Alfa 159   2.4 JTD 20V   Supercharging pressure   P0234   

Lo strumento di diagnosi segnala: Errore P0234 (Pressione sovralimentazione).

This error is detected if the supercharging pressure is too high or too low. As a result, the exhaust gas recirculation valve is switched off and the supercharging pressure can longer be controlled.
