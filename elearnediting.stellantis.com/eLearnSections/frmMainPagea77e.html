140 - Alfa 159   2.4 JTD 20V   STEERING WHEEL OSCILLATIONS / VIBRATIONS / WITH THE VEHICLE STATIONARY   H021   

With the engine running and the vehicle stationary, the steering wheel oscillates and vibrates
 

0

  

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

First of all, check the engine idle speed

Check the tyre pressure.

  

Move on to Step 1

  

1. Engine idle irregular

  

1. Proceed as instructed in the

 See table D010 ENGINE IDLE IRREGULAR
 

2. Tyre inflation pressure not correct

  

2. Make sure tyres are inflated to the correct pressure.
 

1

  

ENGINE FLEXIBLE MOUNTINGS CHECK

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check the condition and the centering of the engine frame, the engine flexible mountings and the engine side and gearbox side connecting rods.

  

Move on to Step 2

  

1. Engine flexible mountings faulty.

  

1. Adjust the fastenings: loosen the bolts, centre them in their housings and tighten them to the recommended torque, see Assembly

 Op. 1008A POWER UNIT FASTENING
 

2. Engine side and gearbox side connecting rods faulty.

  

2. Adjust the fastenings: loosen the bolts, centre them in their housings and tighten them to the recommended torque, see Assembly

 Op. 1008C POWER UNIT REACTION ELEMENTS
 

3. Engine frame faulty.

  

3. Adjust the fastenings: loosen the bolts, centre them in their housings and tighten them to the recommended torque.

Op. 4410D13

 

2

  

STEERING COLUMN CHECK

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check the condition of the steering column support and that it is working properly. In particular, check:

  • that there is no clearance between the support bushes and steering column;

  • that the steering column joints are correctly tightened.

  

Move on to Step 3

  

1. Bushes worn or damaged.

  

1. Restore the efficiency of the steering column. If necessary, replace the bearings involved

 Op. 4110A30 SUPPORT WITH STEERING CONTROL SHAFTS - R+R
 

2. Joint spider clearance too large.

  

2. Tighten the bolts or replace the steering column

 Op. 4110A30 SUPPORT WITH STEERING CONTROL SHAFTS - R+R
 

3

  

STEERING WHEEL ADJUSTMENT DEVICE CHECK

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check the condition of the steering wheel adjustment device. In particular, check that the clearance between the device and the steering column is correct.

  

Move on to Step 4

  

Coupling with excessive clearance.

  

Restore the efficiency of the steering wheel adjustment device. Replace it if necessary.
 

4

  

TIMING CHECK

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check that the engine timing is correct.

  

Move on to Step 5

  

Timing not correct.

  

Restore correct engine timing

Op. 1032B14

 

5

  

FLYWHEEL BALANCING CHECK

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check that the flywheel is correctly balanced.

  

End of diagnosis

  

Balancing not correct.

  

Replace the flywheel

 Op. 1024B10 ENGINE FLYWHEEL - R + R FOR VERSIONS WITH MANUAL GEARBOX
