140 - Alfa 159   2.4 JTD 20V   DRIFT WHILST BRAKING   H002   

The vehicle tends to drift to the left or the right whilst braking. If necessary, carry out a road test on a straight, flat road without transverse gradients to check the effective drift

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

First of all, check that the tyres are the correct type, in good condition and correctly inflated.

Check that the wheel bolts are correctly fastened.

  

Move on to Step 1

  

1. Tyre inflation pressure not correct

  

1. Make sure tyres are inflated to the correct pressure.
 

2. Tyres unsuitable or worn

  

2. Replace the tyres
 

3. Wheel bolts not correctly fastened.

  

3. Tighten the wheel studs correctly.
 

1

  

BRAKING SURFACES CHECK

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check:

  • the condition of the front pads braking surfaces;

  • the condition of the rear pads braking surfaces;

  

Move on to Step 2

  

1. Brake surfaces vitrified or with traces of grease, oil or water

  

1. Clean again, check and remove the causes of the fault
 

2. Front pads damaged or not the recommended type.

  

2. Replace the front pads

 Op. 3310A60 FRONT BRAKE PADS - REPLACE
 

3. Rear pads damaged or not the recommended type.

  

3. Replace the rear pads

 Op. 3310B62 REAR BRAKE PADS - REPLACE

CHECK USING DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Connect the diagnostic test equipment (Examiner or other instruments) to the tester input.

Check that there are no faults in the ABS control unit.

  

Move on to Step 5

  

1. Error detected in the control unit.

  

1. Continue following the instructions for the diagnostic equipment
 

2. No communication with the control unit.

  

2. Restore connection between the ABS control unit and the diagnostic connector

 See E7020 ABS

 

5

  

ACTIVE DIAGNOSIS WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Connect the diagnostic equipment (Examiner or other instruments) and carry out the active diagnosis for:

  • Pump motor;

  • Solenoid valves.

  

Move on to Step 6

  

Pump and/or solenoid valves faulty.

  

Replace the electronic control unit electrohydraulic unit

 Op. 3340A12 ABS HYDRAULIC AND ELECTRONIC CONTROL UNIT - R.R.
