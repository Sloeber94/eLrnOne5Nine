Main specifications


The new Alfa Romeo Marque version is being launched under the name Alfa 150 Sportwagon (Fig. 1).

The new Sportwagon will be available in a 4 door version with a tailgate, 2 trim levels (Progression and Distinctive) and with six engine types including:

-three petrol (1.9 JTS - 2.2 JTS - 3.2 JTS with four wheel drive);-three diesel (1.9JTDM 8v - 1.9JTDM 16v - 2.4JTDM 20v);

Like the Saloon, the new Sportwagon will also be available (fitted as standard or on request) with an extensive range of optional equipment, including: cruise control; parking sensors (front and rear); xenon headlamps; satellite navigation with maps; built-in GSM hands-free telephone with voice controls; automatic bi or tri-zone climate control; radio with CD player (including MP3 player) and CD-Changer; BOSE Hi-Fi sound system; electrically adjustable seats and rear seat with folding backrest (both complete and 40%/60%); engine starting through a button in the console, etc.

The pages that follow contain the identification data, dimensions and a description of the main innovative/significant features of the new model that distinguish it from the Saloon version.

      Engines

      Like the Saloon, the new Alfa 159 Sportwagon, features the following engine types:

      -1.9JTS (1859 cc) four cylinders in line, 16 valves, running on petrol with direct electronic injection with a power output of 118kW (160 CV) at 6500 rpm, top speed 210Km/h.-2.2JTS (2198 cc) four cylinders in line, 16 valves, running on petrol with direct electronic injection with a power output of 136kW (185 CV) at 6500 rpm, top speed 220Km/h.-3.2JTS (3195 cc) six cylinders in a V formation, 24 valves, running on petrol with direct electronic injection with a power output of 191kW (260 CV) at 6200 rpm, top speed 237Km/h.-1.9JTDM (1910 cc) four cylinders in line, 8 valves, running on diesel with direct Common Rail injection with a power output of 88kW (120 CV) at 4000 rpm, top speed 190Km/h.-1.9JTDM (1910 cc) four cylinders in line, 16 valves, running on diesel with direct Common Rail injection with a power output of 110kW (125 CV) at 4000 rpm, top speed 208Km/h.-2.4JTDM (2387 cc) five cylinders in line, 20 valves, running on diesel with direct Common Rail injection with a power output of 147kW (200 CV) at 4000 rpm, top speed 226Km/h.

      Clutch

      The clutch, likeon the saloon, is the dry, single plate type with a self-adjusting external hydraulic control and pedal with no free play.

      Gearbox

      All gearboxes, like on the saloon, are 6 speed with special ratios for improved performance; in addition, to facilitate smooth gear changes and manouevrability, the engagement travel is reducedd and multiple cone synchronizers are used.

      To fully exploit engine performance, the 159 Sportwagon is available with 2 types of gearbox:

      -M32.6 6 speed for the 1.9 JTDM 8/16v and 1.9 JTS and 2.2 JTS versions-F40 6 speed for 2.4 JTDM 20v and 3.2 JTS versions.

      Braking system

      The braking system on the 159 Sportwagon, like on the saloon, is equipped with the most sophisticated electronic and mechanical assistance systems, namely:

      -ABS: anti-lock brakes;-EBD: electronic brakeforce distribution between the front and rear wheels-VDC: vehicle electronic stability control.-ASR (with VDC) : traction control via management of the brakes and engine control unit-Brake Assist (with VDC): automatic increase in braking pressure during emergency braking-Hill-holder (with VDC): assisted hill start system that keeps the vehicle braked while parked on slopes and automatically releases the brakes when the driver wishes to set off.

      Snow chains

      If necessary, notify the Customer that only reduced size snow chains can be fitted with a maximum projection beyond the tyre profile (205/55R16 and 215/55R16) of 9 mm.

      Only "spider" type chains can be fitted on 25/50 R17" tyres (neither traditional chains nor those with a profile of less than 9 mm can be fitted).

      Snow chains cannot be fitted on 235/45 R18" tyres on account of interference with the wing (neither traditional chains, nor those with a profile of less than 9 mm, nor "spider" type chains).

      If snow chains are fitted, it is advisable:

      - not to go above 40 km/h;

      - not to accelerate sharply;

      - to switch off the ASR system (light in button on).

      Also bear in mind that the stopping distances are increased with chains fitted.
