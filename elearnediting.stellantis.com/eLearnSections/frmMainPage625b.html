140 - Alfa 159   2.4 JTD 20V   CHECK ON NOISE FROM FRONT BUSH FOR LOWER TRACK CONTROL ARM SECURED TO SHOCK ABSORBER FORK   4410BC   

This test should be carried out when, following a road test there is a knocking noise from the front with a dull metallic sound from either one or both sides of the vehicle when driving over cobblestones at 20 - 30 Km/h.
 

STEP

  

CHECK TO BE PERFORMED

  

REMEDIAL ACTION IF THE CHECK IS NOT OK
 

1

  

Check on tightening torque

Check that the fork-front arm bush torque tightening is correct on the side where the noise is coming from.

Move on to Step 2

  

The torque tightening is lower than it should be (10 - 20 daNm), remove the fastening, clean any residues of grease from the washer for the bush (as indicated in Fig. 1) and the surfaces of the fork and tighten to the correct torque.
 

2

  

Removing lower track control arm

Remove the lower track control arm

   Op. 4410B40 LOWER TRACK CONTROL ARM (WITH BALLJOINT) FOR LEFT OR RIGHT FRONT SUSPENSION (ONE) - R+R - CHECK WHEEL GEOMETRY SEPARATELY

and place it with the bush in a vertical position (as illustrated in Fig. 2)

Move the inner cover for the higher bush and check that it remains in position (does not move through the effects of gravity)

Move on to Step 3

  

The cover moves under its own weight and therefore the track control arm should be replaced

   Op. 4410B40 LOWER TRACK CONTROL ARM (WITH BALLJOINT) FOR LEFT OR RIGHT FRONT SUSPENSION (ONE) - R+R - CHECK WHEEL GEOMETRY SEPARATELY

and move on to Step 4.
 

3

  

Refitting lower track control arm

Fit a new lower track control arm

   Op. 4410B40 LOWER TRACK CONTROL ARM (WITH BALLJOINT) FOR LEFT OR RIGHT FRONT SUSPENSION (ONE) - R+R - CHECK WHEEL GEOMETRY SEPARATELY

because no faults were discovered in the previous Steps.

Move on to Step 4

  

 

4

  

Check on correct operation on the vehicle

Carry out the check again to detect noise over cobblestones at 20-30 Km/h on the side where the repair was carried out.

The test can be considered complete

  

If the noise persists, follow the instructions in diagnostic tables S015, S016, S017.
