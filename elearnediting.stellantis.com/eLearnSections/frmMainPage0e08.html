Removing ( Refitting )

- Draw off the power assisted steering fluid using a suitable syringe.

1. Disconnect the rapid connector for the pipe, heat exchanger side.

2. Loosen the band, disconnect the pipe from the power assisted steering fluid reservoir and remove it.
Refitting ( Removing )

- Connect the pipe to the heat exchanger pipe and to the power assisted steering fluid reservoir using the band.
