Removing ( Refitting )

- Open the bonnet.

1. Release the retainers (1a) and remove the cover (1b) for the battery supply box.



1. Undo the screws (1a) and remove the auxiliary junction box cover (1b) in the engine compartment.



1. Undo the nuts (1a) and disconnect the electrical connections (1a) and (1b).

BATTERY

  

   See A001 BATTERY
 

1b

  

JUNCTION UNIT

  

   See B001 JUNCTION UNIT

2. Remove the cable between the battery and the auxiliary junction box.
Refitting ( Removing )

- Check that the electric cable is not damaged and that the leads are correctly fastened to the connections.

If necessary, carry out continuity tests.

- Position the cable between the battery and the auxiliary junction box.

- Attach the electrical connections and tighten the nuts.

- Position the auxiliary junction box cover and tighten the screws.

- Position the battery supply box cover and engage the retainers.

- Close the bonnet.
