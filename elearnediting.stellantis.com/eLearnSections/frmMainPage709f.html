140 - Alfa 159   2.4 JTD 20V    SUN ROOF NOT WORKING    Q001   

When the relevant control is operated, the sunroof does not move at all or moves jerkily.
 

0

  

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Carry out the procedure to initialise the sunroof electronic control unit and check whether the roof moves.

 See descriptions 7030 SUN ROOF/GLASS PANEL

  

End of diagnosis

  

The roof does not move.

  

Move on to Step 1.
 

1

  

CHECK THE SUNROOF ELECTRICAL SUPPLY CIRCUIT

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check the following, in order:

  • sunroof supply fuse;

  • sunroof supply relay;

  • sunroof electronic control unit;

  • connection between sunroof relay, fuse and electronic control unit

 See E3060 SUN ROOF - DESCRIPTION

  • correct power supply to the motor.

  

Move on to Step 2

  

1. Connections not correct

  

1. Repair the connections
 

2. Component defective

  

2. Replace the component involved (in the case of a fuse, find out what caused it to blow).
 

3. Electronic control unit not operating

  

3. Replace the electronic control unit

 Op. 7030B66 SUN-ROOF MOVEMENT ECU - R+R
 

4. The motor is not correctly supplied

  

4. Supply the motor directly from the battery bypassing the Body Computer (the cable terminals must be firmly secured to the battery poles). The battery must be charged to >= 13 Volt. IReplace the motor if it does not work

 Op. 7030B70 SUN ROOF MOVEMENT MOTOR - R+R
 

2

  

CHECK THE CORRECT OPERATION OF THE SUNROOF OPERATION SELECTOR

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check the electrical control circuit of the sunroof operation selector.

  

Move on to Step 3

  

Control selector not operating

  

Replace the control selector

 Op. 7030B64 SUN ROOF CONTROL SWITCH - R+R
 

3

  

CHECK THE TORQUE REQUIRED TO MOVED THE SUNROOF

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Use a torque wrench to check the torque required to move the sunroof by hand (70 Nm max with 100 mm lever).

  

Move on to Step 4

  

1. Incorrect effort (more than 100 Nm)

  

1. Move on to Step 4
 

2. Incorrect effort (between 70 and 100 Nm)

  

2. Roll the roof by hand.
 

4

  

SUN ROOF MOTOR CHECK

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check the operation of the sun-roof operating motor: In particular, check:

  • motor pinion status;

  • control rack status.

  

Move on to Step 5

  

Faults in motor components

  

Replace the motor

 Op. 7030B70 SUN ROOF MOVEMENT MOTOR - R+R
 

5

  

CHECK FOR FOREIGN BODIES

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check for foreign bodies inside the sunroof mechanisms (or guides).

  

End of diagnosis

  

1. Foreign bodies present.

  

1. Remove the foreign bodies.
 

2. No foreign bodies present

  

2. Replace the frame assembly

Op. 7030B90 SLIDING ROOF FRAME - R R
