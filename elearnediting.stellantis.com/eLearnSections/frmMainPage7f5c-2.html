Removing ( Refitting )

- Position the vehicle on a lift.

   Op. 4410A80 LOWER FORK CONNECTING FRONT SUSPENSION SHOCK ABSORBER TO LOWER TRACK CONTROL ARM (ONE), LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R. CHECK WHEEL GEOMETRY SEPARATELY

   Op. 4410A12 FRONT SUSPENSION DAMPER (ONE), LH OR RH - FOR MCPHERSON STRUT SYSTEM - INCLUDES DISMANTLING/REASSEMBLY OF ASSEMBLY AT BENCH - R.R

1. Undo bolts (1a) and remove front suspension wishbone (1b).

Refitting ( Removing )

1. Refit front suspension wishbone (1a) and secure by tightening new bolts (1b) to the specified torque.

The upper wishbones have DX or SX stamped on them, which identifies the front suspension fitting side. The flexible bush with the metal edge should be facing the front of the vehicle.
