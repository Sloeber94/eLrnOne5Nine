DESCRIPTION

The vehicle is equipped with a radio system comprising:

- radio;

- front speakers (woofers in the doors and tweeters in the dashboard);

- rear speakers located in the doors

- cables connecting the radio with the speakers;

- aerial with support fitted at the rear of the roof with a coaxial connecting cable.

A sophisticated HIFI system with special HIFI speakers and an outside amplifier can also be fitted.

On the version with the built in "CONNECT NAV +" radio-telephone-navigator the radio is incorporated in the unit.

The aerial has three functions (radio/GSM/GPS) for the operation of the telephone and for the navigator.

The radio is a CAN node through which information is exchanged with the Body Computer and with the other electronic units.

The radio is equipped with an anti-theft system through the connection of the radio at the CAN with the Body Computer; it also manages the volume control linked to the vehicle speed: the volume increases and decreases in relation to the speed of the vehicle: this information reaches the radio node via the CAN.

Some versions are equipped with a series of buttons on the steering wheel: these make it possible to replicate some of the commands which are acquired in the same way by the actual radio.

It is also possible to fit a multi-polar cable connecting the radio to a CD player located in the boot, on the right hand side.
