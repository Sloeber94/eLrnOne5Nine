140 - Alfa 159   2.4 JTD 20V   WINDSCREEN WIPERS NOT WORKING   J002   

With the ignition ON, when the lever on the right of the steering wheel is operated, the windscreen wipers do not work properly
 

0

  

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

With the panel off, lift up the wiper blades and check that the pins are correctly fastened to the arm holders.

  

Move on to Step 1

  

The arms are not correctly fastened (nut defective or not correctly tightened).

  

Replace and/or tighten the fixing nut to torque.
 

1

  

WINDSCREEN WIPER FUSE CHECK

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check the condition of the windscreen wiper fuse.

  

Move on to Step 2

  

The fuse is broken.

  

Replace the fuse (and find out what could have caused it to blow).
 

2

  

VEHICLE WIRING CONNECTIONS CHECK

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Following the removal of the wiper arms and trim, check the condition of the vehicle wiring connector and make sure it is correctly inserted in the windscreen wiper socket.

 Op. 5050B34 FRONT WINDSCREEN WIPER ARM(S) COMPLETE WITH BLADE - R.R.

  

Move on to Step 3

  

1.Connector damaged.

  

1. Replace the connector.
 

2.Connector not correctly inserted.

  

2. Restore correct connection.
 

3

  

CHECK ON TENSION AT WIRING TERMINALS

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Disconnect the connector from the windscreen wiper and switch on the panel and select the various functions using the steering column switch unit checking the tension at the wiring terminals (correct tension at battery terminals 13.5 V).

  

End of diagnosis

  

1. The voltage measured is not correct (the wiring is broken or the steering column switch unit is not working properly).

  

1. Restore the correct connections and/or replace the steering column switch unit.

Op. 5050A12 WINDSCREEN WASHER FLUID RESERVOIR FILLER PIPE (IN ENGINE COMPARTMENT) - R.R.
 

2. The voltage measured is the same as that of the battery (windscreen wiper defective)

  

2. Move on to Step 4.
 

4

  

WINDSCREEN WIPER MOTOR POWER SUPPLY CHECK

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check the following, in order:

  • Connection to earth of motor;

  • Motor power supply line

 See E3020 WINDSCREEN WASH/WIPE

 

Move on to Step 5

  

Connection broken.

  

Restore the correct connections.
 

5

  

WINDSCREEN WIPER MOTOR CHECK

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check that the windscreen wiper motor is working properly and, in particular, the internal electronic device.

  

End of diagnosis

  

1.Motor intermittent electronic device faulty.

  

1. Replace the intermittent electronic device.

Op. 5050B52 FRONT WINDSCREEN WIPER MOTOR INTERMITTENT FUNCTION - R R
 

2. Windscreen wiper motor faulty.

  

2. Replace the windscreen wiper motor.

 Op. 5050B42 WINDSCREEN WASHER JETS LOWER PIPE - R. R.
