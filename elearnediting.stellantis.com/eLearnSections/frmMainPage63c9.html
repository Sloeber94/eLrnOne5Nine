Automatic transmission

The vehicle is fitted with a 6 speed electronically managed automatic gearbox in which the gear changes take place automatically depending on the vehicle usage parameters (vehicle speed and accelerator pedal position).

It should be pointed out that any gear change request:

-sequential (manual) or automatic can be made using the selector lever (Fig. 1)-sequential (manual) can be made using the controls on the steering wheel (Fig. 3) (where present)

in addition, every request is shown on the multifunction display in the instrument panel.

Fig. 1



    Selector lever key

    P = Park

    R = Reverse

    N = Neutral

    D= Drive, automatic forward gear

    + = Shift from higher transmission ratio in sequential mode

    - = Shift from lower transmission ratio in sequential mode

    Multifunction display in instrument panel

    We wish to remind you that the following is displayed in the reconfigurable multifunction display:

    -in automatic driving mode the letter D is shown or the selection of the operating mode P, N, R.-in sequential driving mode, the engagement of the ratio selected manually (higher or lower) is signalled by a number 1, or 2, or 3, .........6.-if available on the version, in automatic mode, as well as the gear selected, the letters W (Winter) or S (Sport) appear if the respective modes are requested using the Sport/Winter button in the gearbox control trim (Fig. 2).

    Fig. 2



      Sequential driving mode

      In the sequential driving mode (manual) the 6 speed automatic gearbox works like a fixed ratio gearbox controlled in sequence and can be activated using the selector lever (Fig. 1) or the controls on the steering wheel (where present) (Fig. 3).

      Sequential command from selector lever

      The gearbox can be managed sequentially by moving the lever from position D sideways (to the right) to a sequential position (manual) therefore:

      -moving the lever towards +: a higher ratio is engaged-moving the lever towards -: a lower ratio is engaged

      It should be pointed out that every gear is shown numerically on the instrument panel display and every possible error is excluded through the continuous check by the gearbox management control unit.

      Sequential command from steering wheel (where present)

      The change can be managed sequentially via the controls on the steering wheel (Fig. 3).

      Fig. 3



        To use the controls of the steering wheel, the gear lever must be in a (manual) sequential position:

        -using the lever on the steering wheel +: a higher ratio is engaged;-using the lever on the steering wheel -: a lower ratio is engaged.

        It should be pointed out that engaging a lower (or higher) gear is only allowed if the engine revs permit it.

        If the vehicle is stopped in a higher gear than 1st speed, the gearbox automatically repositions itself in 1st gear.

        Automatic driving mode

        It is possible to select D from sequential operation in any driving condition. When shifting from sequential mode (manual) to D the optimum ratio is selected by the electronic control unit for the gearbox depending on the engine speed and load conditions (accelerator pedal position).

        For a rapid take off, fully depress the accelerator pedal beyond the hardening point activating the kick-down device to give maximum performance (take off and acceleration).

        Automatic driving mode selection (where present)

        It is possible to select different driving modes by pressing the Sport/Winter button on the gearbox control panel (Fig. 2).

        The following driving modes are available:

         

        NORMAL

        		 

        gear changes at low engine speeds, favouring comfort and reduced consumption

         

        SPORT

        		 

        gear changes at higher engine speeds, enhancing sporty driving

         

        WINTER

        		 

        programme for setting off on roads in poor grip conditions, such as snow, ice, mud. In these poor grip conditions the electronic management of the gearbox can cause the vehicle to set off in a higher gear than 1st to prevent the wheels from slipping: this is not an anomaly.

        The selection mode remains in force until the button is pressed again or the engine is switched off.

        To safeguard the life of the gearbox, if high oil temperatures are reached, the electronic control intervenes cancelling the sequential driving mode and activating the automatic driving mode.

        The sequential driving mode is restored when operating temperatures are reached.
