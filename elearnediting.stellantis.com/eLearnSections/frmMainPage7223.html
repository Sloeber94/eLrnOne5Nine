Removing-Refitting and Adjustment - Service Manual Update


OPERATIONS IN NETWORK

In advance of the Service Manual update we wish to point out that if the buffers on the boot and/or bonnet lid have to be removed-refitted or adjusted, or if the lock needs adjusting, it is necessary to follow the instructions given below:

Removing

-Open th boot and/or bonnet lid.-Turn the ring nut in an anti-clockwise direction to the end of travel position.-Insert a screwdriver with a flat blade (1a - Fig. 1) in the buffer (1b) between the ring nut and the support.-Use the screwdriver to get the ring nut out completely.-Remove the buffer (1 - Fig. 1).

Fig. 1



    Refitting

    -Check the profile of the boot and/or bonnet lid.-Fit the new buffers (1 - Fig. 1) supplied by the Parts Dept. (n° 60659963) in the housings in the boot and/or bonnet lid frame.-Check that the base of the mounting is in contact with the panel.

    during the above operation, make sure that the moving part of the mounting is still in the supply position.

    -Close the bonnet and/or boot lid twice, check that the lock catch has clicked (lock completely closed) and check the profile of the boot and/or bonnet lid again.

    during this stage the mounting, making use of a movement inside the moving casing, will position itself on the nominal dimension determined between the frame and the bodyshell and the difference in tolerance will be automatically recovered.

    -Open the boot and/or bonnet lid.-Apply a clockwise rotation torque of 340° to the upper housing of themounting (alignment of the reference on the buffer with the one of the seal) using a hexagonal spanner.

    during this stage the mounting will be automatically pre-loaded (lengthened by 2 mm) and it will be simultaneously locked in this position.

    -Close the boot and/or bonnet lid and check the profile once again in relation to the housing.

    Adjustment

    If it is difficult to close the boot and/or bonnet lid, it is necessary to:

    -Loosen the moving part of the mounting (1b - Fig. 2) and push the actual moving part outwards (this operation is also possible using a screwdriver 1a for leverage around the entire perimeter of the moving part) moving it through the teeth notches to the original (supply) position.
