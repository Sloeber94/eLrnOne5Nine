140 - Alfa 159   2.4 JTD 20V   CLIMATE CONTROL DOES NOT COOL TO THE DESIRED TEMPERATURE   L012   

APPLICABLE TO VERSIONS WITH: Automatic climate control

The control button activates the passenger compartment fan properly but the temperature of the air is not correct.

APPLICABLE TO VERSIONS WITH: Automatic climate control

 

0

  

PRELIMINARY CHECK

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Disconnect the battery and reconnect it at the Key Off and wait 2 minutes for the Key On checking whether there is a variation in the air flow rate.

  

End of diagnosis

  

There is no variation in the air flow rate.

  

Move on to Step 1
 

1

  

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION OF CLIMATE CONTROL NODE

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Before starting, check the following conditions: ambient temperature around 20°C, vehicle with doors and windows closed (during the cooling and measuring of the temperature)

With the engine on, at ambient temperature (check with the Examiner that the evaporator and passenger compartment temperature sensor readings are about 20°C), with the compressor on, place the control unit to AUTO, adjust the temperature to the maximum cold LO position, the distribution to the front position, the fan speed to 3 bars and the outside air intake function.

After around 5 minutes in these conditions check, using the Examiner, that the temperature at the air vents is decreasing: from the starting temperature of about 20°C (ambient temp.) gradually decreasing to the temperature of the front distribution air vents of between 6-10°C.

Feel whether the air coming out of the vents varies in temperature (getting colder).

  

End of diagnosis

  

The air coming out of the climate control vents does not feel colder

  

Move on to Step 2
 

2

  

SYSTEM PRESSURE CHECK

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Connect the climate control system to the special repressurizing equipment and check the system pressures.

 See Test 5040OB Air conditioning system operational check (pressure)

Check that the engine cooling fan is working properly.

  

Move on to Step 3

  

1.System pressure not correct.

  

1.Restore the correct system pressure value.
 

2.Refrigerant pressurizing too low or too high.

  

2.Restore correct refrigerant level

 Op. 0010T84 AIR CONDITIONING SYSTEM REFRIGERANT - DEHYDRATING AND REPRESSURIZING
 

3.Incorrect operation of engine cooling fan.

  

3.Carry out the

 See Test 5040AB REFRIGERANT CIRCUIT CHECK
 

3

  

CHECK USING DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Connect the Examiner diagnostic equipment and check the following at the Climate Control Node:

  • that there are no errors at the air mixing actuators;

  • that there are no faults in the outside temperature sensor;

  • that there are no faults in the passenger compartment temperature sensor;

  • that there are no faults in the evaporator temperature sensor.

  • that the solar sensor is working properly

 See Test 5040DC Check sunlight sensor operation

  

Move on to Step 4

  

1. Error at the air mixture actuators.

  

1. Move on to Step 5
 

2. Error at the outside temperature sensor.

  

2. Move on to Step 5
 

3. Error at the passenger compartment temperature sensor.

  

3. Move on to Step 5
 

4. Error in the evaporator temperature sensor.

  

4. Move on to Step 5
 

5. Solar sensor defective.

  

5. Replace the solar sensor

 Op. 5010D56 SOLAR SENSOR - R.R FOLLOWING ELECTRICAL SYSTEM CHECK
 

4

  

CHECKS USING DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Keeping the Examiner diagnostic equipment connected, check the correct operation of the air mixture actuator through active diagnosis.

  

Move on to Step 6

  

Air mixture actuator not working

  

Move on to Step 5.
 

5

  

ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

With the engine switched off, remove the control unit from the dashaboard support and disconnect the wiring connector from the control unit and check visually for any damage to the connector contacts and reconnect correctly to check for false contacts.

Check the continuity/insulation of the electrical connection:

  • of the air mixture actuators if there is an error at the mixture actuators;

  • of the sensor if there is an error at the outside temperature sensor;

  • of the sensor if there is an error at the passenger compartment temperature sensor;

  • of the evaporator temperature sensor if there is an error at the evaporator temperature sensor.

  

Move on to Step 6

  

1. Electrical connections loose or damaged.

  

1. Renew the electrical connections.
 

2. Electrical connection broken or not insulated.

  

2. Restore the continuity/insulation of the electrical connection.
 

6

  

PIPE AND FILTER CHECK

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check that the air conditioning high and low pressure pipes are not blocked.

  

Move on to Step 7

  

1. Pipe from compressor to condenser obstructed.

  

1. Replace the pipe from the compressor to the condenser.
 

2. Pipe from condenser to expansion valve obstructed.

  

2. Replace the pipe from the condenser to the expansion valve.
 

7

  

CONDENSER CHECK

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check that the condenser is not blocked or fouled with mud or damaged.

  

Move on to Step 8

  

1.Condenser obstructed or damaged.

  

1.Replace the condenser

 Op. 5040A40 AIR CONDITIONING CONDENSER - R.R.
 

2.Condenser fouled.

  

2.Remove the fouling by washing.
 

8

  

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION USING DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check that the following errors do not persist:

  • at the air mixture actuators;

  • at the outside temperature sensor;

  • at the passenger compartment temperature sensor;

  • at the evaporator temperature sensor.

  

Move on to Step 9

  

1. Air mixing actuator error.

  

1. Check that the Lin Bus cables are not caught between the metal parts (pay special attention to the gearbox bolts) and move on to Step 9.
 

2. Error at the outside temperature sensor.

  

2. Replace the outside temperature sensor

Op. 5010D12

 

3. Error at the passenger compartment temperature sensor.

  

3. Replace the climate control system control unit

Op. 5040D15

 

4. Error in the evaporator temperature sensor.

  

4. Replace the evaporator temperature sensor

Op. 5040B64

 

9

  

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION OF MIXTURE ACTUATOR

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check whether the electrical wiring connections are loose.

Disconnect and visually inspect for damage at the connector contacts and reconnect the connection to the motor mounting.

If it persists, before replacing the actuator, check its operation by manually activating the function that the actuator should carry out.

With the engine running, switch on the fan at maximum speed.

Place the flaps in the various distribution positions via the control/control unit (manually).

Check the air flow in the passenger compartment with your hand at the vents.

  

End of diagnosis

  

1. Electrical connections loose or damaged.

  

1. Renew the electrical connections

 See E6020 AIR CONDITIONING

 

2. There is no air flow intake into the passenger compartment through the vents.

  

2. Move on to Step 10.
 

10

  

CHECK ON IMPEDIMENTS TO THE ROTATION OF THE MIXTURE ACTUATOR

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Remove the actuator and check through the control unit that the actuator turns checking for the presence of mechanical impediments to its rotation

  

End of diagnosis

  

1.The actuator does not turn.

  

1.Replace the mixture actuator

Op. 5040D56

 

2.Kinematic components or flaps broken/stuck.

  

2.Replace the components that is faulty.
