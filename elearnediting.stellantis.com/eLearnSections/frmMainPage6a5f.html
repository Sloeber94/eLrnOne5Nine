1. Undo bolts (1a) and remove cover (1b) by undoing retainers (1c).

2. Undo the bolts (2a) and remove the air intake unit (2b).

3. Disconnect end (3a) of lever (3b) (ball fitting).

4. Undo the bolts (4a) and separate the upper half (4b) from the lower half (4c).

5. Remove radiator (5a) complete with pipes (5b).

6. If necessary, separate radiator (5a) from pipes (5b) after undoing collars (6a).
Refitting ( Removing )

Replace any damaged gaskets.

- Check that the radiator is intact and does not show signs of damage or deposits of dirt on the finned surface.

- Position the radiator.

- Position the pipes and secure to the radiator by means of retaining bands.

- Join the upper half to the lower half and tighten the bolts.

- Connect the end of the lever (ball fitting).

- Position the air intake assembly and tighten the bolts.

- Position the cover and do up the retainers, then tighten the bolts.
