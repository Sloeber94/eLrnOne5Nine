- Check that the brake/clutch fluid in the reservoir is at the maximum level; if not, top up using the recommended fluid.

- When topping up the brake/clutch fluid, remove the reservoir cap and top up until the maximum level is reached.

To prevent impurities entering the brake-clutch fluid reservoir, before undoing the cap clean it and the entire upper surface of the reservoir next to the filler thoroughly.

Always use a funnel with a built-in filter with a mesh of 0.12 mm or less.

The brake-clutch fluid in the reservoir should not exceed the MAX notch.

The symbol on the resevoir identifies synthetic type brake/clutch fluids, distinguishing them from mineral type fluids. Using mineral type fluids irreparably damages the special braking system rubber seals.

Take great care to prevent the brake/clutch fluid, which is extremely corrosive, from coming into contact with the paintwork. If this does happen, was immediately with water.

Brake-clutch fluid is poisonous and highly corrosive. In the event of accidental contact, immediately wash the affected parts with water and neutral soap. Then rinse thoroughly. Consult a doctor immediate if you swallow the fluid.
