Functional description

Control unit M47 is supplied at pin 1 directly from the battery by a line protected by fuse F39 of the junction unit under the dashboard B2.

The ignition-controlled power supply (INT) arrives at pin 16 from the line protected by fuse F51 of the junction unit under the dashboard B2.

The control unit is earthed at pin 10.

The A-BUS serial line connected to the Body Computer M1 - pin 25 of connector B, leaves from pin 18 of M47.

The Body Computer M1 is supplied directly by the battery through the line protected by maxifuse B99 at pin 18 of connector F interfaced with the junction unit under the dashboard B2; the ignition-controlled power supply (INT) reaches pin 9 of connector F of the interface with junction unit B2.

The four receivers for the sensors P12 receive a 5V supply from pin 2 of M47 and a reference earth from pins 12, 13, 14 and 15 ; they send a pressure signal to pins 3, 4, 5 and 6 of M47.

The control unit autodiagnostic data can be read by connecting to connector C of the Body Computer M1, pin 12: it receives signals from pin 17 of control unit M47 via the special diagnostic line.
