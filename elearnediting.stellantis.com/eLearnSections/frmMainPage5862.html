FUNCTIONAL DESCRIPTION

The EOBD central tester output is built into Body Computer M1 connector C.

It is supplied directly by the battery at pin 16 of the line protected by fuse F39 of the junction unit under the dashboard B2 ; pins 4 and 5 are earthed.

Pin 1 is connected to the K line at ABS control unit M50 (pin 6) or VDC (pin 29)

    See E7020 ABS

    See E7022 VDC - DESCRIPTION

Pin 7 is connected, via the K line, to the engine management control unit M10

    See E5030 PETROL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

    See E5050 DIESEL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

Pin 7 is also connected through the K line to the automatic transmission control unit M55 (only on versions with Automatic Transmission)

    See E7050 AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION

or with the robotized gearbox control unit M54 (only on the Robotized Gearbox version)

    See E7051 ROBOTIZED GEARBOX

Pin 12 is connected by the K line to the management control units for the xenon headlamps F10 and F11.

    See E3080 HEADLAMP AGLINMENT CORRECTOR

and to the tyre pressure control unit M47, pin 17

    See E7060 TYRE INFLATION PRESSURE CONTROL SYSTEM

The remaining outlets for other possible K lines are not used on this version.

Pins 6 and 14 are connected to B-CAN: this network controls and "diagnoses" the junction unit under the dashboard B2, the instrument panel E50, the driver''s door control unit M66, the passenger door control unit M67 and the boot compartment control unit M63, the controls for the seats H66 and H67, the TEG Reader H3, the steering lock control unit M89, the radio or the built-in radio-telephone-navigator P20 and the controls on the steering wheel D47, the climate control system control unit M70, the Air Bag control unit M60 and the parking sensors control unit M84.

    See E1050 CAN CONNECTION LINES - DESCRIPTION
