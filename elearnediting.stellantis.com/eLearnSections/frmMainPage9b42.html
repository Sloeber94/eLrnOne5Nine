0010 A 140 AC FORMS FOR ROUTINE P.D.I.
New part number - Network Information


We wish to notify you that the new forms to be filled in during the routine P.D.I. operations are now available.

The new forms differ from the previous ones through the inclusion of some new operations including:

-bush initialization (specific to 154Q4 CROSSWAGON versions)R-fitting of front cover (specific to 156Q4 versions)-instructions for checking battery charge

The new routine P.D.I. operation forms can be ordered by quoting part no. 507285 - 60448402 and following the same procedure as for Service Manuals.
