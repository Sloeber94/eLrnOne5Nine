SPECIFICATIONS

The driveshafts, constant velocity joints and intermediate drive shaft that make up the transmission assembly transmit motion from the gearbox to the drive wheels.


The transmission features wheel side constant velocity joints and one gearbox side constant velocity tulip joint. The opposing drive shaft features a constant velocity joint with an intermediate drive shaft.

The intermediate drive shaft transmits drive to the right drive shaft, which is the same as the left.

This solution allows drive torque to be distributed in a perfectly symmetrical manner over both wheels.

This prevents the longer drive shaft from curving due to bending induced by the transmitted drive torque, which could cause changes in the vehicle''s dynamic ride setting.
