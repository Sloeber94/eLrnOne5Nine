Removing ( Refitting )

- Position the vehicle on a lift.

- Undo the bolts and remove the left front wheel.
Refitting ( Removing )

- Position the left front wheel in its housing and secure it by tightening the bolts to the recommended torque.

The wheel studs used on this vehicle have M14 x 1.5 threads and can be identified by a groove in the centre of the head.

It is absolutely forbidden to swap these bolts for bolts from other vehicles.

The use of other studs could irreparably damage the wheel hub threads and make it necessary to replace them.
