Removing ( Refitting )

- Place the vehicle on a lift.

   Op. 7055B54 PROTECTION/GUARD UNDER ENGINE -R.R.

   Op. 7210E10 R.R. FRONT BUMPER

1. Undo the bolt (1a) and remove the cold air intake for the air filter (1b) after having released it from the air filter.

2. Undo the bolts (2a) and remove the radiator air duct (2b).



1. Partially undo the screws (1a) securing the engine oil radiator (1b) to the cooling module.

1. Undo the bolt (1a) and disconnect the engine oil supply pipe to the cooling radiator (1b), engine oil cooling radiator side.

Collect the engine oil that comes out in a suitable container.

1. Undo the bolt (1a) and disconnect the engine oil supply pipe to the cooling radiator (1b), rear pipe side, then remove.
Refitting ( Removing )

- Refit the front pipe carrying engine oil to the cooling radiator, connect to the rear pipe and tighten the bolt.

- Connect the front engine oil supply pipe to the cooling radiator, engine oil cooling radiator side, and tighten the bolt.

- Tighten the screws securing the engine oil radiator to the cooling module.

- Fit the radiator air duct in its housing and secure it using the bolts.

- Place the cold air intake for the air filter back in its housing and secure it using the bolts.
