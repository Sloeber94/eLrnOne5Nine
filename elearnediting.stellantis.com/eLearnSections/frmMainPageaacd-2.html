Removing ( Refitting )

It is not possible to remove-refit the camshaft housing on the vehicle on this version.

The power unit must be removed-refitted to perform this operation

   Op. 1004B10 POWER UNIT WITH MANUAL GEARBOX - R.R.

or

   Op. 1004B11 POWER UNIT WITH MECHANIC TRANSMISSION - R.R. FOR FOUR-WHEEL DRIVE MODELS

or

   Op. 1004B14 POWER UNIT WITH AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION R + R

and then proceed as described below keeping the power unit secured using a suitable support.

1. Loosen the band (1a) securing the oil vapour pipe (1b) to the separator.

2. Undo the bolt fixing the oil vapour pipe to the intake chamber.

1. Undo the bolt (1a) securing the bracket (1b) which supports the oil vapour pipe (1c) to the rigid return pipe to the water pump and remove it.

1. Undo the nut (1a) and remove the supply lead (1b) from the alternator.

1. Disconnect the electrical connection for the air conditioner compressor.

1. Undo the injector bracket retaining nuts.

2. Remove the injectors (2a) complete with brackets using the tools (2b) and (2c).

If new injector/s is/are fitted it is necessary to:

- determine the identification code stamped on the top surface;

- check, using an Examiner, that the new injector(s) is (are) recognised by the engine management control unit.

Start the removal beginning with the injector for the first cylinder and continue with the injectors for the second, third, fourth and fifth cylinders.
