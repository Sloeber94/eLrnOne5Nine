- Raise the vehicle and disconnect the intermediate connector for the rigid pipe.

1. Disconnect the handbrake cable from the right rear caliper.

1. Undo bolts (1a) and remove the handbrake cable from rear suspension longitudinal arm (1b).

- Disconnect the connector connecting the rigid pipe with the hose to the brake calliper.

- Connect the rigid pipe to the brake calliper hose and tighten the connector to the recommended torque.
