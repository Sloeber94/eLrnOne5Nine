Removing ( Refitting )

- Position the driveshaft in a vice fitted with protective jaws.

1. Remove the protective gaiter retaining bands.

1. Retract the protective gaiter on the driveshaft.

1. Clean the drive shaft constant velocity joint until the retaining ring is accessible.

1. Extend the retaining ring using suitable pliers.

2. Extract the wheel side constant velocity joint from the driveshaft.

1. Remove the wheel side constant velocity joint retaining ring.

1. Remove the protective gaiter.
Refitting ( Removing )

- Wash the constant velocity joint components thoroughly using fuel oil or petrol and visually inspect that there are no signs of seizing or grooves on the balls or seats.

- Position the new protective gaiter on the driveshaft.

- Place a new retaining ring in the housing on the constant velocity joint.

Check that the retaining ring is correctly fitted.

- Place the wheel side constant velocity joint in its housing on the driveshaft.

Push the constant velocity joint until the retaining ring clicks into position.

- Fill the joint and the gaiter with the specified grease.
