140 - Alfa 159   2.4 JTD 20V   ENGINE COOLANT LEAKS   X010   

Go back to ENGINE COOLANT CONSUMPTION

An engine coolant leak can be seen with consequent dripping and/or fouling of the engine.
 

 

 

 

 

 

0

  

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

  

All OK

  

Problems encountered

  

Operation
 

 

- Carry out an initial visual inspection to locate the component that is leaking or the area of the leak.

- Check that there is no engine oil in the coolant.

- Check that there is no water or emulsion in the engine oil.

- If the visual check is not sufficient, wash the engine, dry it and start slowly to bring it up to the correct temperature. Test the watertightness of the system to identify the leak

 Op. 0510C30 ENGINE COOLING SYSTEM - LEAKAGE TEST

PIPE CHECK

  

All OK

  

Problems encountered

  

Operation
 

 

Check the condition of the pipes and the seal. More specifically:

- the condition of the bands making sure they are correctly fitted;

- the condition of the hoses, connectors and plugs

  

Move on to Step 2

  

Tightening not correct or components damaged.

  

Restore the correct tightening or replace the components involved

Check the condition of the radiator

  

Move on to Step 3

3

  

EXPANSION TANK CHECK

  

All OK

  

Problems encountered

  

Operation
 

 

Visually check:

- the condition of the expansion tank;

- the cap seal. Check the condition of the cap breather valve.

  

Move on to Step 4

  

Tank damaged or distorted.

  

Replace the tank or the cap

- the condition of the water pump;

- the seal of the gaskets.

  

Move on to Step 5

Replace the water pump or the gaskets

Problems encountered

  

Operation
