DESCRIPTION

The integrated system for Homelink remote controls is a device that can be programmed to allow garage doors and gates to be opened remotely or to turn lighting and alarm systems on and off.

A single fixed control inside the vehicle, in the driver''s sun visor, can replace up to three portable remote controls carrying out the same functions as the individual remote controls it is replacing.

For example, to operate the door, simply press one of the three buttons instead of the portable remote control. The HomeLink warning light remains on whilst the signal is being transmitted.

    See descriptions 5505 INSTRUMENT/GAUGE ELECTRICAL CIRCUITS

Both the HomeLink control unit and the control panel are housed in the sun visor and replace the courtesy mirror and light.

    See E2520 GLOVE COMPARTMENT LIGHT - BOOT LIGHT - SUN VISOR LIGHT - DESCRIPTION

The device receives a direct battery supply through the SBMT function.

The Body Computer continuously acquires the battery voltage signal to compare it with a limit figure (SBMT function): if the battery voltage measured is below the level, it intervenes via the CAN sending a signal to disable a series of loads: these include the HomeLink device.
