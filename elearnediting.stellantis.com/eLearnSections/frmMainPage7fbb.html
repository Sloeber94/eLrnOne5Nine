Notify the Customer that, before undertaking long journies or if the vehicle is subject to heavy usage, the following recommendations as well as those in the Owner''s Handbook should be followed as well as respecting the intervals indicated in the Planned Maintenance Programme in order to ensure the smooth running of the vehicle.

PERIODIC CHECKS

Every 1,000 km or before long journies, check and, if necessary, top up:

- engine coolant level;

- brake fluid level;

- windscreen washer fluid level;

- tyre inflation pressure and condition;

- operation of lighting system (headlamps, direction indicators, hazard warning lights, etc.);

- operation of screen wash/wipe system and positioning/wear of windscreen/rearscreen wiper blades.

Every 3,000 km check and, if necessary, top up: engine oil level.

HEAVY USAGE OF THE VEHICLE

If the vehicle is used mainly in any of the following particularly harsh conditions:

- towing a trailer or caravan;

- dusty roads;

- short, repeated journeys (less than 7-8 Km) and at sub-zero outdoor temperatures;

- engine often idling or driving long distances at low speed (e.g. taxi or door to door deliveries) or long period of idleness.

- town driving

the following checks must be carried out more often than indicated in the Planned Maintenance Programme:

- check front disc brake pad condition and wear;

- check cleanliness of bonnet and boot locks, cleanliness and lubrication of linkage;

- visually inspect condition of: engine, gearbox, transmission, rigid and flexible pipes (exhaust - fuel system - braking system) rubber elements (boots - hoses - bushes - etc.);

- check battery charge and battery fluid level (electrolyte);

- visually inspect condition of various drive belts;

- check and, if necessary, change engine oil and replace oil filter;

- check and, if necessary, replace pollen filter;

- check and, if necessary, replace air filter.
