140 - Alfa 159   2.4 JTD 20V   DASHBOARD CREAKING   S061   

With the vehicle moving, particularly over rough ground or during acceleration or braking, noise can be heard from the dashboard area.
 

0

  

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Road test the vehicle to check the type of noise

 See Test 70B ROAD TEST FOR NOISE RECOGNITION

  

Move directly to the relevant step.

  

1. Squeaking noise cannot be located.

  

1. Move on to Step 1.
 

2. Noise from one of the front doors.

  

2. Proceed as instructed in the

 See table S062 FRONT DOORS CREAKING
 

1

  

CHECK DASHBOARD/WINDSCREEN COUPLING

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check the gap between the lower area of the windscreen and the dashboard is correct: the gap must be sufficiently large for a card to be inserted

  

Move on to Step 2

  

Faulty dashboard fastening.

  

Renew the felt pads behind the facia in the contact area with the windscreen.
 

2

  

CHECK OUTLET FASTENINGS

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check the condition of the air outlet fastening tabs on the dashboard.

  

Move on to Step 3

  

1. Fault in central outlet fastening.

  

1. Renew the correct fastening

 Op. 5010C30 CENTRAL AIR VENT IN DASHBOARD - R.R.
 

2. Fault in side outlet fastening.

  

2. Renew the correct fastening

 Op. 5010C38 SIDE AIR VENT (ONE) LEFT OR RIGHT IN DASHBOARD - R.R.
 

3

  

CHECK GAUGE FASTENINGS

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check the correct fastening of the three gagues behind the steering wheel: in particular, check for the felt pads in the coupling area with the dashboard.

  

Move on to Step 4

  

1. Faulty fastening of gauges behind the steering wheel.

  

1. Restore the correct fastening of the gauges. Replace the felt pads if necessary. See assembly

 Op. 5560B ANALOGUE CONTROL PANEL
 

2. Faulty fastening of check gauge behind the steering wheel.

  

2. Restore the correct fastening of the check gauge. Replace the felt pads if necessary. See assembly

 Op. 5560C OTHER INSTRUMENTS
 

4

  

CHECK STEERING COLUMN TRIM FASTENING

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check the steering column trim is correctly fastened to the dashboard.

In particular, check for:

  • insulating material around the cables;

  • felt pads in the mating area with the dashboard;

  • free movement of the steering wheel height adjustment device;

  • fastenings of the steering column cover sections.

  

Move on to Step 5

  

Faulty fastening.

  

Restore the correct fastening on the facia. Restore the felt pads if necessary

 Op. 7040A26 STEERING COLUMN TRIM - R+R
 

5

  

CHECK THE CENTRAL TRIM FASTENINGS

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check the central console trim is correctly fastened to the dashboard. In particular, check for:

  • correct fastening of the central instrument module;

  • insulating material around the cables;

  • felt pads in the mating area with the dashboard;

  

Move on to Step 6

  

1. Faulty central console fastening.

  

1. Renew the correct fastening. Restore the felt pads if necessary

Op. 7040A60 UNDERDASHBOARD TRIM - R.R.
 

2. Faulty central instrument module fastening.

  

2. Renew the correct fastening

 Op. 5560C51 COMBINED ANALOGUE CONTROL PANEL - R+R OR REPLACE
 

6

  

CHECK THE CLIMATE CONTROL MODULE FASTENING

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check the climate control unit is correctly fastened.

  

Move on to Step 7

  

Faulty climate control unit fastening.

  

Renew the correct fastening of the control unit

 Op. 5040D18 AIR CONDITIONING CONTROL UNIT - R.R.
 

7

  

CHECK THE GLOVE COMPARTMENT

  

ALL OK

  

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

  

ACTION
 

 

Check the correct fastening of the dashboard glove compartment and its flap. In particular, check for the presence of the rubber pad used to secure the compartment to the dashboard.

  

End of diagnosis

  

1. Faulty glove compartment fastening.

  

1. Restore the correct fastening of the glove compartment on the dashboard. Restore the felt pads if necessary

 Op. 7040A31 DASHBOARD COMPARTMENT ASSEMBLY - R.R.
 

2. Faulty flap fastening.

  

2. Restore the correct fastening of the glove compartment flap on the dashboard. Restore the felt pads if necessary

 Op. 7040A14 FLAP FOR GLOVE COMPARTMENT IN DASHBOARD - R.R.
 

3. Flap will not close correctly.

  

3. Adjust the striker

 Op. 7040A33 GLOVE COMPARTMENT LOCK STRIKER - ADJUST
