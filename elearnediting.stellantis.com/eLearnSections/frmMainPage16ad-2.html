It is not possible to remove-refit the camshafts on the vehicle on this version.

In order to carry out this operation the engine must be placed on the overhaul stand and then the following operations performed

    Op. 1004B10 POWER UNIT WITH MANUAL GEARBOX - R.R.

or

    Op. 1004B11 POWER UNIT WITH MECHANIC TRANSMISSION - R.R. FOR FOUR-WHEEL DRIVE MODELS

or

    Op. 1004B14 POWER UNIT WITH AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION R + R

    Op. 1004D10 POWER UNIT (MANUAL GEARBOX), REMOVED, DISMANTLE AND REASSEMBLE

or

    Op. 1004D20 POWER UNIT (AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION) REMOVED, REMOVE AND RE-BUILD

    Op. 1004D40 ENGINE - POSITION ON STAND AND REMOVE

Proceed with the removal of the cylinder with the engine on the overhaul stand with reference to

    Op. 1004E10 ENGINE, REMOVED - REMOVE CYLINDER HEAD(S) AND OIL SUMP FOR INSPECTION - INCLUDES POSITIONING ON STAND AND REMOVAL
