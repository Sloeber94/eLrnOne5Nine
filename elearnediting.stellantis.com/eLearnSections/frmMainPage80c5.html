Irregular engine operation, breaking of connection at the injectors - Service Solution


FAULT COMPLAINED OF

Irregular operation of the engine and/or exhaust fumes; the engine is firing on three/four cylinders.

The analysis using the Examiner detects the presence of one or more of the following errors:

- P0201 = Injector for cylinder 1;

- P0202 = Injector for cylinder 2;

- P0203 = Injector for cylinder 3;

- P0204 = Injector for cylinder 4;

- P0205 = Injector for cylinder 5.

TECHNICAL CAUSE

The pulsating vibrations transmitted by the stiff injector piping adversely affects the connectors, generating interruptions in the connection with consequent failure of injector operation.

OPERATIONS IN PRODUCTION

Adoption of stiff piping equipped with a damping mass starting from engine:

- n° 5853303 – 1.9 16v 150 bhp;

- n° 5753255 – 2.4 20v 200 bhp;

- n° 5741021 – 2.4 20v 210 bhp.

OPERATIONS IN NETWORK

Network behaviour standards towards the Customer

-When this problem is reported and confirmed, reassure the customer that it can be solved by fitting the damping masses on the rigid injector pipes.-After the operation show the customer what has been done and make sure they are satisfied.

Service Solution

When this problem is reported, fit damping masses on the rigid piping for the injectors and, if necessary, restore the electrical connections by carrying out the Operating Cycle described below.

if there are other types of damping masses already present but only on some high pressure pipes (for example on the 1st, 4th and 5th cylinders) remove them and replace them with the definitive versions.

OPERATION CYCLE

Damping masses

The rubber damping masses (part no. 55214403, illustrated in Fig. 1) to be fitted on the rigid pipes for the injectors, as illustrated in figures 2 and 3, are ready to fit by simply applying gentle pressure.

Fig. 1



    1.9 JTD 16v engines

    Remove the soundproof covering, referring to the instructions provided in Op. 1016A10 in the Service Manual.

    Then fit the damping masses part no.55214403, supplied by the Parts Dept., to the rigid piping for the injectors and to the rigid piping from the high pressure pump to the common rail at the points illustrated in Fig. 2.

    Fig. 2 - 1.9 JTD 16v engines



      2.4 JTD 20v engines

      Remove the soundproof covering, referring to the instructions provided in Op. 1016A10 in the Service Manual.

      Then fit the damping masses part no.55214403, supplied by the Parts Dept., to the rigid piping for the injectors and to the rigid piping from the high pressure pump to the common rail at the points illustrated in Fig. 3.

      Fig. 3 - 2.4 JTD 20v engines
